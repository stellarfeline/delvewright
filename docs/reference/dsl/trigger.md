| `DW0194` | Environment-trigger id malformed/duplicated, or `approach` `range` 0. |
| `DW0350` | A `use` trigger anchored where an NPC stands. Right-click on an NPC already belongs to its dialogue advancement; a second interaction hitbox in the same cell makes the client's entity ray-pick ambiguous, and whichever entity loses the tie is silently dead — the soft-lock class that starved the giant's dialogue of every right-click. Left-click triggers are exempt (a left-click has no dialogue meaning): they ride the NPC's own hitbox instead of summoning a second one. Validation-tier (exit 1), `dsl::validate`. Prescription: move the trigger to its own anchor, express the interaction as a dialogue option, or — if the NPC's body is genuinely the target — use `on: strike-npc`, which takes no anchor at all. |

### DW03xx — build / solver / nav (`compiler`; error; exit 3, `stage:"build"`)

This module's rows of a section whose prose is on the [`delvec::compiler::nav` page](../delvec/compiler/nav.md#dw03xx--build--solver--nav-compiler-error-exit-3-stagebuild).

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW0427` | **A press answer addressed to an event nobody is named for** (spec-0119, spec-0117). A trigger declares `audience: presser` or `answers` on an `on: approach` (`EnvTrigger::attributes_its_actor`, the DSL-side statement of the compiler's criterion table `emit::trigger::press_criterion`). A presser is named by the event's own carrier: a right-click on an interaction entity by `minecraft:player_interacted_with_entity`, a left-click on one by `minecraft:player_hurt_entity` (read site: the pinned server's `CriteriaTriggers.PLAYER_HURT_ENTITY` and `PlayerHurtEntityTrigger.trigger`, measured in [`click-attribution.md`](../click-attribution.md)), a step by the cell's own selector. So `audience: presser` and `answers` pass on a `use`, a `step`, a `strike` at a place, a `strike-npc` and a `strike-assembly`. An `approach` is a distance test on the tick with no carrier entity, so no criterion names its player and no refusal line has a presser to reach; approximating it is downstream folklore, so the capability is refused rather than faked. `dsl::trigger::press_answer_checks`, validation tier (exit 1). Prescription: make it a `use`, `strike` or `step` trigger on something the player presses, or drop `audience` and `answers` and let the beat address the party. |
| `DW0428` | **An authored trigger id in the compiler's reserved `dw-` namespace**. The compiler synthesizes triggers of its own — one per declared press answer (`trigger/dw-answer-<owner>-<state>`, spec-0117 §5.2) — and two triggers sharing an id would share one `dw_trig_…` tag and one emitted function, so one of them would silently disappear. Reserving the prefix makes the collision impossible by construction rather than improbable. `dsl::trigger::press_answer_checks`, validation tier (exit 1). Prescription: rename it; any kebab id not opening with `dw-` is the campaign's. |

### DW105x — the pressable class (`dsl::trigger`; error; exit 1)

Spec-0117. Every interaction point a player presses is a **pressable**: a real block named by its `prop` (or a gate's own block), in one of three states — **open** (the press acts), **settled** (the act is done), **shut** (neither) — and it answers every press: one `Refusal` line per state it has outside **open** (`answers.not_yet`, `answers.done`, and `answers.missing_item` for an `interact` with `requires_item`), shown on the presser's action bar with the refusal cue. The engine never writes the words. `dsl::trigger::press_sites` is the one enumeration of pressables (`interact`, `use`/`strike`/`strike-npc`/`strike-assembly` triggers, shortcuts, shops, disarms, bonfires) and `press_form` the one classification of how a block is pressed, read by the validator and the compiler alike. Every validation prints two binding lines: `press bodies (DW1059): N interaction point(s) — O pressed on their own (… by kind), B boxed (… acting on their own, … unobservable, … struck), R riding a body or a gate, X refused` and `press answers (DW1051): P pressable(s) — A answer(s) owed, D declared, X refused; G gate answer(s) judged by DW0429; L released; K with only the open state`.

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1051` | **A press that would be silent** (spec-0117 §6). A pressable has a state a player can press it in — shut (an `interact` before its step, a gated trigger, a bonfire armed after the start, a shortcut's sealed door from its far side) or settled (an `interact` after completion, a `once` trigger that fired, a `serves` trigger whose referent settled, a spent disarm) — and declares no line for it; or an `interact` with `requires_item` declares no `missing_item`. A press there does nothing and says nothing. Also the **silent block**: `release` on a boxed block the game reports no press of (a lantern, a torch), which would leave scenery a press reaches in silence. Passes with no answer: an ungated, repeatable, unserved `use` trigger, a shop, a bonfire armed at step 0, a gate's own answer (it has no settled body). Validation tier (exit 1), `dsl::trigger::press_checks`. Prescription: write the named line, one sentence in the campaign's voice saying why nothing happened ([`game-writing.md` F2](../game-writing.md)). |
| `DW1052` | **An answer for a state the press never has**, or a pressable field that binds to nothing (spec-0117 §6): `missing_item` without `requires_item`; `not_yet` on a press with no shut state (an ungated, unserved trigger; a knocker of a shortcut gate, sealed from load); `done` on a press with no settled state (a `once: false` unserved trigger, a shop), on a released pressable (its box is gone and the block is the player's), or on a gate's own answer (the gate opening removes its body); `release` on a block pressed on its own (a bell, a button) or on a pressable with no settled state; `prop` on a gate's own answer (the gate names its block); `serves: {gate}` naming an anchor no `close-gate` or shortcut seals. An inert declaration is refused, never ignored. Validation tier (exit 1), `dsl::trigger::press_checks`. Prescription: delete the field, or give the press the state it answers. |
| `DW1059` | **A press target that is not a real block** (spec-0117 §6): (a) an interaction point with no `prop` where one is required (`interact`, `use`/`strike` at a place, shortcut, shop, disarms, bonfire); (b) a `prop` whose blockstate the pinned game gives no outline (`minecraft:air`), or whose outline leaves its cell; (c) a door named by its upper half (a door is placed from `half=lower`); (d) a trigger anchored at a gate **region** anchor, many cells — anchor it at a cell of the door, where the gate's own block is its body (build tier, `compiler::pressable::check`, where regions resolve). The outline is the pinned jar's (`crates/dsl/data/outline-shapes-1.21.11.tsv`). Validation tier (exit 1) for (a)–(c), build tier (exit 3) for (d). Prescription: name a block the player sees, at a point anchor. |
