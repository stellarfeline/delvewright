# A body's native entrance — what the pinned server does

Reader: the agent designing a level where a body arrives the way its kind arrives in vanilla — a warden digging out of the ground, a wither charging up — and the engine work that emits and proves it. Every statement is **[cited]** — measured on the pinned server, Minecraft Java 1.21.11, by `validation/warden-maze-probe.mjs`, with the trial named — or **[authored]**, a judgement made here from the measurements. The warden's own entrance, the emerge, is measured in `warden-behaviour.md` §6 and is not restated here; this record holds the wither's charge-up and what a client is told of a body that stands for no tick. The engine's surface for an entrance is spec-0132.

## Instrument

- Server: `ghcr.io/stellarfeline/delvewright-base@sha256:3e7db2562b492dbf442568a327d361547628c98c04a7cb68218c8dde6abdd1de` (`versions.toml`, `[images.base]`), `TYPE=VANILLA VERSION=1.21.11` (the server's own `version` reply: `data = 4671`, `pack_data = 94.1`), superflat, `difficulty easy`, `spawn_mobs false`, `random_tick_speed 0`, a throwaway container with no published port.
- Arena: the stone pad of `warden-behaviour.md`, 91 × 91 at y = 100, forceloaded; the body stands at its centre and is read over rcon (`data get entity`, `execute if entity`, `execute if block`) every 0.5–0.8 s.
- Player: one mineflayer 4.37.1 bot (offline mode) under Resistance V, parked 48 blocks from the wither — past its 40-block `follow_range`, inside its tracking range — reading the vanilla boss bar's progress off its own client; 12 blocks from the warden of T9, recording every entity spawn its client receives.
- Two runs of T7: run 1 with a stone ring on every arm; run 2 with the open arm added (§1, the blast), the other arms repeated and agreeing to the tick. One run of T9.
- `mob_griefing` is set per arm: `false` is what every delve runs under (`compiler.md`, the world init gamerules), `true` is the instrument's own cross-check that the ring can be seen to break.

## 1. The wither's spawn charge-up

- **A bare command summon has no charge-up.** [cited, T7-wither-bare, both runs] `Invul` read 0 at 0.3 s; the body had moved 5.8 blocks by 1.2 s and 10 by 2 s; wither skulls were in the air by 2 s; a blow of `damage … 10 minecraft:generic` at 4 s took it 300 → 291 (run 1) and 300 → 290 (run 2), regenerating 1 per second after; the boss bar the client drew followed its health (0.966 at 3.9 s).
- **`Invul:220` plays the charge-up, and it lasts 220 ticks.** [cited, T7-wither-invul-220, T7-wither-invul-220-open, T7-wither-invul-220-griefing] `Invul` read 218 at 0.3 s, 155 at 3.5 s, 50 at 8.7 s, 3 at 11.1 s and 0 at 11.9 s — 220 ticks, 11 s. The boss bar read off the client rose from 0.004 at 0.1 s to 1 at 11.06 s in steps of 1/220 (0.004, 0.009, 0.013, …), the same clock seen from the other side.
- **It heals 10 health every 10 ticks while charging, to its maximum.** [cited, T7-wither-invul-220] Summoned at `Health:100f` (a third of 300, the health a block-built wither starts at): 110 at 1.1 s, 150 at 2.6 s, 200 at 5.5 s, 250 at 7.9 s, 300 at 10.3 s and 300 after. Summoned without a health it stands at 300 throughout.
- **While charging it does not move, does not shoot, and cannot be hurt.** [cited, same three arms] Feet displacement 0.00 at every reading until `Invul` reached 0 (0.11 at `Invul` 2 in one arm); no wither skull at any reading before `Invul` 0; `damage … 10 minecraft:generic` at 3.9 s answered `Target is invulnerable to the given damage type` in every arm, health unchanged.
- **The charge ends in a blast that breaks nothing in a delve.** [cited, T7-wither-invul-220, T7-wither-invul-220-griefing] In the tick `Invul` reached 0: under `mob_griefing false` a ring of 24 stone two blocks out, three high, stood whole (24 → 24, both runs); under `mob_griefing true` the whole ring was gone (24 → 0). The delve's gamerule is `false`.
- **The blast hurts bodies in the open: about 51 at 3 blocks and 40 at 6.** [cited, T7-wither-invul-220-open, run 2] Two villagers of 1024 health, no armour, at `[+3, +3]` (4.2 blocks horizontally) and `[+6, 0]`, read 0.16 s after `Invul` 0: 1024 → 972.7 and 1024 → 984.1. Behind the ring the same bodies lost 1 each (runs 1 and 2, both ringed arms): a block in the line shadows an explosion, which is why the ring and the bodies are measured in separate arms. [authored] A player has 20 health: an unarmoured one within 6 blocks of a wither's entrance does not survive it, and nothing in these readings bounds where the damage falls below 20 — a body between 6 and 14 blocks (twice the blast's power of 7) was not placed.
- **After the charge it is the free body at once.** [cited, same arms] Moved 1.2–7.2 blocks in the reading at `Invul` 0 and 8–11 within 3 s; a skull in the air within 2.5 s of the blast; the target at 8 blocks under Resistance V still standing at 20 health, so the attack is read from the skulls and never from a body's health.
- **A `NoAI` body does not play it.** [cited, T7-wither-invul-220-noai, both runs] `{Invul:220,NoAI:1b}`: `Invul` read 220 at 0.3 s and 220 at 3.5 s, health 300, no movement, no skull, the boss bar created at 1 and never moved.
- **A wither needs no spawn finalization.** [cited, T7-wither-bare against the `Invul` arms] The bare summon and every NBT summon acted alike once free: the `finalizeSpawn` a warden depends on for its `dig_cooldown` (`compiler.md`, the `unleash-actor` row) seeds nothing a wither misses.

## 2. A body summoned and removed in one tick

Three datapack functions, each run once by command with the bot 12 blocks away and listening for entity spawns on its client.

- **The client is told of the body, at the cell it stood in.** [cited, T9-blink_kill, T9-blink_unseen, T9-blink_control] `summon` then `kill` in one function: the client received one warden spawn at `[200.5, 101.0, 200.5]`, the mark, 82 ms after the call, and held no warden 2 s later while the server held none. `summon` then `tp ~ -128 ~` in one function — the shape of the compiler's unseen removal (`compiler.md`, "A body the story removes is never seen to die"): one spawn received, at the mark and not at −128; the server held the body under the world. The control, `summon` alone: one spawn, at the mark. The instrument counts what it should: three functions, three spawns seen, each where the body was summoned.
- [authored] A puppet that stands for no tick is still a body the client was told about where it stood. Whether a frame draws it is a client question these readings do not answer, so the engine does not rely on it: a body that must not be seen before its entrance is never summoned as a puppet at all; its `unleash-actor` is its first appearance (spec-0132 §3).

## 3. What the engine takes from it

- **The wither's entrance is `Invul:220` on the twin's summon, with its health at a third of its maximum**; it lasts 11 s, during which the body is still, silent and unhurtable, and it ends in a blast that breaks no block in a delve and kills an unarmoured player within 6 blocks. [authored, from §1]
- **The warden's entrance is one `is_emerging` memory beside its `dig_cooldown`**; it lasts 133 ticks, during which the body hears nothing and cannot be hurt, and an anger seeded on it is kept for the hunt that follows. [cited, `warden-behaviour.md` §6]
- **Only the free body plays an entrance.** [authored, from §1 and `warden-behaviour.md` §6] A `NoAI` puppet keeps whatever entrance data it was summoned with and never consumes it; the twin `unleash-actor` summons is the one body in the engine that can enter.
- **A body that enters from nothing is never a puppet first.** [authored, from §2]
