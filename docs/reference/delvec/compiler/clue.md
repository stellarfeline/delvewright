# `delvec::compiler::clue`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/clue.rs`: the one module that answers whether a step's stated clue has been presented before the step (spec-0143), and the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code it declares. The surface is in [`compiler.md`](../../compiler.md) (the stage-5 rows `quests[].objectives[]` and `triggers[]`, field `clues`). The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

**What is judged.** An act — an objective, or an environment trigger — states what tells the player its answer as a `clues` list (`dsl::Clue`): `{by: hint, objective}`, `{by: record, objective}` or `{by: sight, of}`. The engine confirms that on the main line each stated clue is presented before the party reaches the act. It judges nothing else: not whether the act can be found, and not whether the clue is enough — that is judged in play. An act that states no clue is owed nothing.

**The main line is derived.** It is the stage-4 spine, `QuestPlanContent::spine`: the finale and every quest its `depends_on` chain demands. An objective is on the main line when its quest is in the spine; a trigger is when the objective the walked path performs it in front of is. **An optional act is owed nothing**: an objective of a quest outside the spine, or a trigger no walk performs in front of a spine objective, is a side puzzle whose clue the player goes looking for. It is counted on the binding line and judged nowhere.

**The walks** are `compiler::acts`'s: the exported critical path and every realised branch's own path. A main-line act is judged on every walk that performs it.

**Forced before.** *Before* is every play order, never one walk's. An objective `X` is forced before an act when it is a strict ancestor of the act (`Plan::strict_ancestor_steps`, a branch's own from `Plan::branch_gate_model`: the transitive `after` of the act's objective, and every objective of a quest its quest transitively `depends_on`; a trigger takes the ancestors of the objective it is performed in front of), or when `X`'s `on_objective_complete` bundle is the only place in the campaign that sets a flag which the act, the objective a trigger is performed in front of, or anything already forced, requires — no other objective, no quest completion, no trigger, no dialogue option, no optional root. Taken to a fixed point.

**Presented.**

- `record`: its objective is forced before the act, so its finished journal page (spec-0137) holds the line in every run that reaches the act.
- `hint`: its objective is the act itself, or is forced before the act, or is a main-line objective on the walk that the replay holds started (`RegionEvents::armed`) at or before the act's step.
- `sight`: its anchor's cell is in sight — the one sight rule, `framing::in_sight`, one of nine lines clear, within `SIGHT_BLOCKS` (64) of the standing eye — from a cell the main line stands on before the act: the walk's entry cell, the place of each step forced before the act, the act's own place, and the cells of the route into each (`World::route_between`: `find_route` between the two cells the walk names, snapped to footing), each in the world the walk holds when it arrives there (`nav::worlds_at`). The eye's own cells never stop its line. `every_consumer_of_in_sight_answers_alike` (`crates/delvec/src/compiler/framing/shot/tests.rs`) holds `clue::unseen`, a shot's sight and a review frame's manifest to one answer over generated cases.

**What is not proven**: that the player read the page or looked that way; that the route the model walks is the one a player takes. A flag with a second setter forces nothing, so a real order that rests on one is stated with `after`.

**Binding.** `delvec validate` prints `clues stated (DW1136): S of A act(s) state C clue(s); R name nothing that presents them`. Every build prints `DW0863 binding: S of A act(s) state C clue(s) — M on the main line of W walk(s), J clue judgement(s): T text clue(s) and V sight clue(s) presented before their act, R refused; O optional act(s) owed nothing; N act(s) state no clue and are owed nothing`, and writes `validation/clues.json`: those counts (`examined` is `J`) and one row per stating act — `id`, `line` (`main` or `optional`), `main_on` (the walks that judge it), and per clue its `by`, its name and a verdict per walk (`presented`, `how`).

## Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW0863` | **A main-line act's stated clue is not presented before the act** (spec-0143 §4; build tier, `compiler::clue::check_presented` from `emit::build`, because it needs the walks' places and the world's blocks). The message names the walk, the act, the clue and its pointer (`…/clues/<i>`), and why: the clue's objective is not on the main line (a clue an optional detour shows does not count for a main-line step); it comes earlier on this walk and nothing forces that order; it is performed after the act; its page has not opened when the walk reaches the act; or its cell is in sight from none of the counted cells, with the count and the nearest reason (the distance, or the first cell that stops the centre line). Prescription, per reason: make the clue's objective something the story cannot go on without (name it in an `after`, or let its completion alone set a flag the step requires); move the clue to a text the main line reads; let the objective start before the step; move the thing where the main line passes it. The gallery's probes `a-clue-in-an-optional-bay` and `a-clue-read-after-the-step` red it. |
| `DW1136` | **A clue names nothing that could present it** (spec-0143 §3). At `delvec validate` (`compiler::clue::check`, exit 1), at `/content/quests/<q>/objectives/<o>/clues/<i>` or `/content/triggers/<t>/clues/<i>`: a `hint` or `record` clue naming no objective; a `hint` clue naming an objective with no `hint`, or with no `title` to carry it; a `record` clue naming an objective with no `record`. At the build (exit 3): a `sight` clue whose anchor resolves to no cell. Prescription: write the text on the objective named (`hint`, `record`), or name the text or the anchor that exists. The gallery's probe `a-clue-that-names-no-text` reds it. |
