# `delvec::compiler::loop`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/loop.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW0945–DW0950 — an endless corridor (`compiler::loop` / `dsl::validate` / `compiler::plan`; spec-0086; error + one advisory)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW0945` | **The slab's geometry.** A `loops[]` region is not a slab a body crosses once: more than one axis of `extent` is the crossing axis, or `d` is zero along it; `|d[a]|` is smaller than the slab's thickness `t = 2·extent[a] + 1`, so a moved body is still in the slab next tick; the slab is thinner than the poll needs (the fastest displacement per tick along `a` — `dsl::metrics::POLL_HORIZONTAL_BLOCKS_PER_TICK` 0.5878 horizontally, `POLL_FALL_BLOCKS_PER_TICK` 3.92 under a fall, both measured by `tools/spike-seamless-loop/ at 2278c55877e2` — must be less than `t` plus the body's reach on that axis, so a vertical slab under a fall is at least three cells); a horizontal slab whose top face stands no more than a jump's apex over the walk plane of its lowest course (`MAX_JUMP_RISE_16`, so a slab must be at least two courses tall from the floor), which a jumping body passes over; it meets another loop's slab or landing, a `teleport` volume or a lethal volume's keep-out; or a cell of it is not passable. Build tier (exit 3). Prescription: one axis, a move that clears the slab, a slab as thick as the bound names, and a slab of open air. |
| `DW0946` | **A near-field cell whose block or light differs from its image.** A cell `c` of the visible set nearer than the loop's near range to some eye (before or after the jump) whose full block state differs from `c − d`, or whose light (the model's flood at the campaign's darkest and brightest reachable sky) differs, in some configuration the route passes through — the base world, each runtime write reaching the span, each exercise crossing's `on_cross` writes — or a lethal keep-out or `teleport` volume in the near field that is not equal to its own image. Build tier (exit 3). The message names the loop, up to six cells with both states or both levels and the sky, the count, the configuration with the write and its root, and the near range. Prescription: make the two sections the same; never shorten the view. |
| `DW0947` | **The view out of the landing is open, or its far field moves.** Two shapes. *Open*: the span's walk reaches `SPAN_REACH` (128) cells past the landing slab, rises above the built volume into open sky, or leaves every placed piece, at an open cell in sight of a judged eye and nearer than its fog end; the message names the loop, the eye, its fog end, the open cell and the face, and prescribes closing the view inside the span (a turn, a jog, a door, a grille, a pillar) or an atmosphere whose fog end the eye reads whole. *Far field* (spec-0090): a visible cell past the near range differs from its image, in block or in light (judged over its lit area), or a compiler-placed body stands past it, and the jump shifts it on screen more than `FAR_FIELD_SHIFT_DEGREES` (1.2852°); the message names how many far differences are over the threshold and the worst — its cell, both states or levels, the configuration, the eye, its distance, the shift and the threshold — and prescribes putting the difference farther from the eye, a shorter offset, or making the sections the same. Build tier (exit 3). |
| `DW0948` | **A compiler-placed body in the loop's near field.** An NPC, a trigger body, a seated wave, an actor, an item display or a shop marker whose cell lies in the span and nearer than the near range to some eye: a body has an identity the move cannot repeat. A body past the near range is a far feature (`DW0947`). Build tier (exit 3). Prescription: place it outside the span or far off, or summon it after the release. |
| `DW0950` | **Advisory: a loop the forced route never meets while it holds.** No exercise step is spliced for the loop; the message names the step at which its gate was first read shut. Warning (exit 0), `Plan::build`. A corridor endless only until something is found elsewhere is a design; a mechanism nobody is made to experience is reported. |

**Binding.** Every build that declares a loop prints `loop binding: N loop(s); slab cells N; eyes N (fog end A..B blocks as the kernel reads it); span N cells closed in N steps, frontier cells closed by geometry N and by fog N, open faces N; visible cells N compared as blocks and as light at 2 skies over N configuration(s), N of them in the near field (A..B blocks); far-field differences N, largest shift X° of T°; volumes in span N, bodies in the near field N, bodies in the far field N; forced route meets N of N holding, exercise steps N`, and writes `validation/loop-gate.json` when it holds: `far_field_shift_degrees`, and one row per loop with the same counts, `near_range`, `near_visible`, `far_differences`, `far_largest_shift_degrees`, `far_largest_shift_at {cell, eye}`, `bodies_in_far_field`, the exercise steps, and the `unchecked` list above. The proofs run before `DW0311` in `emit::build`, and again at the stage-7 edit replay door, which prints its own binding line before a refusal it raises; a batch it admits prints none, so a build prints one binding line, the deciding pass's.
