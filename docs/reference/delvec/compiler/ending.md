# `delvec::compiler::ending`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/ending.rs`: what an ending reads, and the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## What an ending reads (spec-0108)

An ending is a `campaign-complete`, named `ending/<kebab>` (a sentence is `DW0110`); one that names none is reported as `ending/<unnamed>`. Its **final scene** is the whole bundle the act that fires it fires: the effect root it stands in at any depth (a quest's `on_complete`, an objective's completion, a trigger's `effects`, …), with every list nested under it (a `sequence` step, a cutscene's `then`, an `on_arrive`). Its **inputs** are every flag a `requires_flags`/`forbids_flags` names and every datum a `requires_state` term names, on any `when` in the final scene — the ending's own gate, the gates of the lines that hold it, and every other line's alike: a line gated on an input is the epilogue line that states that thread's outcome, whether it is spoken before the banner or after the camera returns.

Every **writer** of an input is a producer (a `set-flag` or datum write on any root, a dialogue option's `set-flag`, a trap's or timed gate's `disarm.sets_flag`; never a reaction bundle), and has a kind: **said** (a dialogue option, or a bundle under a `talk-to` objective's completion), **did** (a bundle under a `reach-anchor`/`kill`/`collect`/`interact` completion, a trigger, a trap, a disarm, `on_kill`, `on_unlock`, a shop offer, an assembly's `on_land`, a loop's `on_cross`, `on_death`) or **finished** (a quest's `on_complete`). A **deed** is did or finished. Each writer is dated by the quest whose step fires it (none for an ambient root). An input is **seeded** when some writer is dated outside the ending's host quest (the quest whose bundle holds the scene) or belongs to no quest; otherwise it is **final-scene**.

An ending's **aftermath** is every effect of its final scene whose verb changes the world (`Verb::changes_world`): block writes, carries, spawns and despawns, walks, the clock and the weather, flags and data, checkpoints, stealth, strikes, clips, volleys, items and status effects. A cutscene, a narration, a sound, a burst and the ending itself only say.

The reading is judged over the **story worlds** of `compiler::flow` ([`flow.md`](flow.md#story-worlds-spec-0108)): an ending is reached by a world whose run fires its `campaign-complete` with every step legal where the run took it.

`validation/endings.json` carries, per ending: `id`, `path`, `scene`, `host_quest`, `inputs[]` (`key`, `of`, `kind`, `seeded`, `deed`, `writers[]` with `path`, `stage`, `kind`, `quest`, `act`), `aftermath[]` (`path`, `verb`, `observable` — the cells and block, the volume and destination, the tag, the clock, the weather, the score or checkpoint mirror — or `emission-only`), `worlds[]`, `undecided_worlds[]`, `packtests[]` (the `ending_<slug>_<k>` templates and how many observables each asserts — one per world that reaches the ending and fires an observable aftermath effect, wherever the `campaign-complete` stands; the template runs the root's own function and drives every scheduled part of the bundle, and a build that cannot make one refuses as an internal invariant, `DW_BUILD`) and its `endings_read` verdict; and, for the campaign, the electives, the forced acts, every story world, every target with its witness or its reason, and the binding line.

**Binding** (every validate, analyze and build): `ending binding: E ending(s); I input(s) read, S seeded (D with a deed among their writers), F final-scene only; W story world(s) enumerated (X unrealizable target(s), B at the bound); A aftermath effect(s), O of them observable after the scene.`, then one line per ending: `ending/<slug>: reads N input(s) — <input>: <kinds>, <seeded|final-scene>, written at <quests|ambient>; …; aftermath: A effect(s) [<verbs>]; reached by world/<k>, …`. Zeroes are printed.

## Diagnostics

### DW1029, DW1030 — an ending reads the run (`compiler::ending`; error; exit 2)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1029` | **An ending reads fewer inputs than the campaign declares every ending reads.** Fires per ending and per term when stage 4 declares `endings_read` and the ending's count of seeded inputs is below `at_least`, or its count of seeded inputs some writer of which is a deed is below `deeds_at_least`. Silent when `endings_read` is absent; a declaration over zero endings states `0 ending(s)` and refuses nothing. Path: the `campaign-complete`. The message names the ending, the term, the declared number, the count, and every input with its kinds, seeded or final-scene, and the quests that write it. Remedy: gate a line of the final scene on a flag or datum a deed in another quest writes, or lower the declaration. Analysis tier: seeding is a fact of how the writers are dated, not of the gate alone. |
| `DW1030` | **An ending no story world reaches.** Fires per declared ending (`ending/<unnamed>` included) that no story world's coherent run fires; per ending that fires only on a gate term read against a datum no world dates (a stake's forfeit, a loop's count, a reaction bundle, a forced act, a `player`-scoped datum at two or more players) — refused as **unproven**, naming that datum; and per ending no enumerated world fires while the enumeration stopped at its bound — refused as **unproven**, naming the bound. The message names the ending, its own gate, and the scene's unrealizable targets grouped by reason with their counts (a mandatory or forced writer sets the flag; nothing writes it; no writer fires before the scene; no subset of elective amounts reaches the interval, with the values a world can reach; the interval needs part of a quest; an undatable writer moves the datum; the classes are reachable but not together; the search stopped at its bound). Remedy: give the input an elective writer the proof can perform, loosen the interval, or delete the ending. Withheld when the mainline is degenerate or does not replay: `DW0201` or the critical path's `DW0204` already names that cause, and a target no world's walkable run reaches says so as its reason. Distinct from `DW0482`, which quantifies over declared branches: this quantifies over endings, so a tally-chosen third ending between two extremes is reached or refused. |
