# `delvec::compiler::nav::width`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/nav/width.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW1057/DW1058 — a way is whole (`compiler::nav::width`; error; exit 3)

spec-0120. Asked in the world block right after `DW1047` (`emit::routes::prove_world`), over the blocks as shipped (`assembled::shipped_blocks`: every gate as authored at world load, the relight fixtures, every trigger's prop, every holder's shown contents) and that world after each runtime write alone — every `fill-region`, `set-block`, `open-gate`, `close-gate`, laid or cleared way and clear at any root and depth (`plan::effect_writes`), every write the region model carries, and both phases of every timed gate (`nav::width::state_writes`). It reads the plan, the blocks and the prefab contracts, never a route. The research behind the rule is `docs/reference/clear-width.md`.

**The population** (`nav::width::population`; its stations are also the cross-sections the shut-way check cuts a branch at, `nav::width::plan_cross_sections`, [`learned.md`](learned.md)): every **portal** — a `walk`, `stair`, `climb` or `drop` seam with an `opening`; every **contact**; every **way place** — a site-plan box that declares `way`; and every **piece way** — a placed piece's contract edge of class `walk`, `stair`, `climb`, `drop` (its `via`) or `barred` (its `bar`) that answers no plan seam. In a site-plan campaign every exterior contract edge answers a seam and is judged under it. A stair seam's run is the courses the stand-in laid (`Blockout::runs`), or — where a piece hosts the stair — the contract `stair` edge whose `via` the contract's own graph joins to the space at the face answering the seam (the exterior edge whose region meets the opening); that flight is judged once, under the seam, and is no piece way of its own. A host piece whose contract declares no such flight (an exterior `stair` edge's own `via` is the face, not a run) is counted *run undeclared by its piece*, and the approaches its seam has are still judged.

**The corridor and its columns** (spec-0120 §3). A wall portal's approach on each side — one cell off the plane, across the opening's width, at that place's walk plane; its plane is `DW0836`'s and is counted *left to `DW0836`*, never judged twice. A `drop` or a `climb` across a wall is walked on its high side only. A stair's head approach (the other place's, across the wall, or beside the hole's top on the upper floor), every course of its run (the body cell over each tread, where the run was laid; the lowest cell a body stands in, measured, over a piece's flight), and its foot's approach at the host's floor. A piece's flight is read off its climb: its head is the end whose course stands higher (the end nearer the plane only when the two end courses stand at one height), so a flight that runs along the wall under its opening is judged at its foot exactly as one that climbs toward the plane; across such a flight, which the plane does not cut, its columns are counted from the flight's own first column, and across a flight the plane cuts, from the opening's. A hole through a floor that is not a stair: the hole's columns from two cells over the upper floor and from under the hole down to the lower floor, every cell free. A contact: its span, judged (`DW0836` admits a front with one passable column), at the higher of the two floors, and its approaches. A way place: its `clear` columns, from `way.at` (centred by default), at every station along `way.axis` across the box's play space, at its floor — a station cell a seam's approach covers is judged once, under the seam. A piece way: its region's layers along the axis on which it separates its two spaces (one cell beyond its own footprint, at its course or one above or below; the longer horizontal extent for a stair whose spaces name none; `y`, every cell free, where no horizontal axis does), and one approach cell in each endpoint space the piece owns; a station's walk plane there is the lowest a body stands in any of its columns as built.

**Clear** (one predicate, `nav::can_be`): a column is clear when a body can be in its body cell — standing, holding a climbable, or afloat — or in the cell over it when what stands in the body cell is a floor a body steps onto (`MAX_AUTO_STEP_16`: a slab, a path, a lantern standing on the floor). A beam over a body's head blocks nothing; a `Thin` block blocks nothing; a post, a wall, a fence, a raised full block or a closed hatch takes its column. Each state is classified by the one occupancy model (`assembled::occupancy_over`) over a window of the blocks round the way — eight cells across and above, so a cell water reaches is classified with its source — geometry only (`Premises::geometry_only`): the width is a fact of the blocks, and each state already says what is shut. **The way's own regions stand open in every state**: a shut seam's bar, every gate region the beat that opens the seam also opens (a gate station cleared by the same effect list — the hiding structure spec-0118 §9 advises), a piece way's `bar` and `way` (a laid way's block, a cleared way's air).

**Whole, narrowed, shut.** At a station, in a state: whole when every column is clear, shut when none is (the gate's own state: `DW0836`, `DW1011`), narrowed otherwise. A column is *taken* when it is not clear in some state the station is not shut in.

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1057` | **A way narrowed by something the author did not declare.** A taken column no `narrowed[]` entry of the way names. Raised once per way, at its first narrowed station. The message names the way (the connection with its class and `form`, the way place by its node, or the piece and its contract edge), the station (*the foot's approach in `node/undercroft`*, *course 3 of the run*, *the span*, *12 cell(s) along `node/causeway`*), the declared clear width, the free width per state (*as built: 1 of 3; once `anchor/barricade` opens: 2 of 3*), and every taking cell as `[x, y, z]` with its block and the state in which it takes the column (*as built*, *as built: the relight fixture the build sets at setup*, *while `anchor/barricade` is shut*, *after `set-block` at `…`*). Remedy: move the structure out of the way's columns; or, where it is meant, declare it — the entry the author would write, its `side`/`across`/`course` (a seam), `along`/`across` (a way place) or piece-frame `cells` (a contract edge) and its `block` filled, its `what` left as `…` for the one word the engine cannot write. Every other refusal of the build is printed beside the first. |
| `DW1058` | **A declared narrowing the bytes do not show.** Every `narrowed[]` entry on every way: a column it names that is clear in every state judged; a block other than the one standing (`block` matches when the id is the same and every property the declaration states has the same value); a side, a course, a station or a piece cell the way does not have. A stale excuse — the piece re-detailed, the post gone — is this code, so an excuse never outlives the thing it excused. Remedy: correct or remove the entry, or put the structure back. |

**A narrowing on an edge that realizes a plan seam** — an exterior contract edge in a site-plan campaign, or the flight a stair seam took as its run — is refused `DW0888` (`compiler::claims`), the contract's own gate: the seam's `narrowed[]` is the one authority for what narrows its way.

**Binding.** Every build that assembles a world prints, before the verdict and zeroes included: `clear width (DW1057/DW1058): W way(s) of T traversal seam(s), B box(es) and E contract edge(s) — P portal(s), S stair run(s), C contact(s), Y way place(s), K piece way(s); M column(s) at N station(s) judged over V state(s); H way(s) whole, U narrowed undeclared, D narrowed as declared (G column(s) excused by F declaration(s)), X declaration(s) not shown; L plane column(s) left to DW0836; R stair run(s) undeclared by their piece`. `W` is `P + C + Y + K`; `S` counts the stair seams whose run was judged (laid, or a piece's flight); `V` counts the states that reached a judged column, as built included. `validation/clear-width.json` carries the same counts (`ways`, `examined` — the same number, which the gallery's coverage gate refuses at 0 —, `traversal_seams`, `boxes`, `contract_edges`, `portals`, `contacts`, `way_places`, `piece_ways`, `states`, `plane_columns_left_to_dw0836`, `stair_runs_undeclared`) and one row per way: its kind, its name, its declared width (the opening's extent on its in-plane axis for a wall seam, the span for a contact, `clear` for a way place, never a count of the columns a run or flight happens to be judged on, which a station's own `columns` states — so a flight that runs along the wall under its opening reports the opening's width, not the one cell the opening spans across that flight), the states judged, its verdict (`whole`, `narrowed`, `narrowed-as-declared`), every station with its columns, its clear count per state, the states in words, its taken columns with every taking cell (`cell`, `block`, `state`) and its verdict (`whole`, `narrowed`, `shut`, `left to DW0836`), and every `narrowed[]` entry with its columns and whether the bytes confirm it. Written on every build that assembles a world and is not refused; a refused build writes no tree at all, so what a refusal states is the binding line, printed before the verdict.
