# `delvec::compiler::nav::vehicle`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/nav/vehicle.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW1096–DW1099 — a body carries another (`dsl::vehicle` / `compiler::nav::vehicle`; spec-0135; error)

A vehicle is a body the campaign places in `vehicles[]` that a player boards and leaves: a boat, the rider's minecart or a saddled mount. The engine resolves each one's seat (its mark), its `bounds`, and its **medium**, each cut at `bounds`: for a boat, the surface cells of the water a body swims (`World::is_water_surface`) across the boat's footprint; for a cart, the run a push along its `facing` takes from the mark (`compiler::rail::driven_run`) — along the rails through every stretch that does not drive it of at most 45 cells, ending where the rail ends, on a brake, or where such a stretch exceeds that coast (`docs/reference/vehicles-animals-and-targets.md` §6, §11); for a mount, ground at its footprint, out of water, rising at most one block. Its **boarding cells** are the standable cells whose eye reaches its body within the 3-block interaction reach, nearest the mark first; its **leaving cells** are the standable cells within a block past its widest column of a medium cell, a block up or down, and for a mount the cell it stands in.

**A ride is declared.** The route proof rides a leg only where its objective carries `ride` (`Plan::declared_rides`), whether or not a walk crosses it: `nav::vehicle::ride_of` walks the party from the leg's start — round the cells the vehicle's body stands in (`VehiclePlan::body_cells`, sealed for that walk and named on the step as `body`) — to the nearest boarding cell it can reach, rides the medium from the mark, and leaves at the leaving cell nearest the leg's end from which the party walks on (then the shorter ride, then cell order; at most 256 leaving cells are tried); a ride moves the vehicle at least one cell, and a cart is left only where its run ends. No cell a ride boards from, passes or leaves at is one a body touches an armed completion volume from — the leg's own objective's included — by `World::meets_keep_out_fp`, the rule a walk keeps out by; a ride ends short of its `reach-anchor` and the party walks in, and a cart whose run enters such a volume carries nothing. A ride is not left on the goal's own cell. **An act from the seat** (spec-0135 §5): where the leg ends in an act a rider can do — a container a `collect` opens or a lever or button an `interact` presses, at `crosshair::BLOCK_INTERACTION_REACH`; an `interact`'s hitbox or a `talk-to`'s NPC, at `crosshair::INTERACTION_REACH` (`route::leg::seat_target`) — `ride_of` first takes the shortest ride to a medium cell from which `VehiclePlan::rider_eye` has the target in reach and in sight (`vehicle::eye_acts_on`, the sight rule a standing body is held to); the party stays aboard (`RideTake::stays`). The rider's eye is the vehicle's rest (a boat `BOAT_AFLOAT`, 0.523 over its water cell's floor, a pig on its floor) plus its passenger point (a boat's height ÷ 3, a raft's × 0.8888889, a pig's 0.86875) less the player's vehicle attachment 0.6 plus the standing eye 1.62 — the vanilla record §15; a cart and every other mount have none, and their riders act after leaving. The route proof then carries the seat (`route_with_links`): a next leg declared on the same vehicle rides on from that medium cell with no boarding (`RideFrom::Aboard`), and any other leg is led by a ride on to the leaving cell nearest its end from which it goes on (`LinkTakes::leaves`), spliced in front of every link and ride the leg takes. A lapse (an `expire` step, spec-0133 §8) after an act from the seat is no leg: the party waits where it sits, aboard, and the step's `pos` and `stand` are that medium cell (`LinkTakes::stood`); the leg after it is led by the ride on as any other. `positions_of` reads an act from the seat and a ride that does not `board` as carries. One exception to the keep-out: an objective that waits for its own rider — its gate reads a `riders` census of the vehicle its leg is ridden on (`Plan::rides_aboard`) — completes under the rider, so its ride goes into its volume and its `ride` step carries that census. The path gains a `ride` step in front of the objective's step, at the kind's rate: a boat 5 blocks a second, a mount 4, a cart 2.4 — each a rate the record measured. A declared leg its vehicle does not carry is `DW0311`, naming the vehicle and the miss. A leg with no `ride` is walked and no vehicle is tried for it. Every validate prints `vehicle binding (DW1097–DW1099): <n> vehicle(s) — <kind> <id> medium <m> cell(s) in bounds, board <b> cell(s), leave <l> cell(s), seats <s>; …; <d> objective(s) declare their leg ridden; <r> ride leg(s) on the critical path`, and per branch world `vehicle binding, <branch>: <w> ride leg(s) on its path`. The pacing model does not price a ride.

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1097` | **A vehicle seated outside its medium**: a boat whose footprint is not surface water; a cart off a rail, on a slope, on a powered rail that is powered with a solid block at one end (a launcher runs an empty cart away — the record §11), whose `facing` does not run along its rail, or with neither rail nor a solid block behind it (a body that walks into it rolls it off the open end of its line); a mount that cannot stand at its footprint or stands in water. Build tier (exit 3), judged over the assembled world before the route proof's verdict — at the final build and after every world-edits batch alike (`nav::vehicle::judge_plan_vehicles`, the one call both doors make), so an unseated vehicle is named as that, never as its leg judged as a walk. Prescription: the mark into the medium, or the medium under the mark. |
| `DW1098` | **A medium cell the vehicle reaches from which a body that leaves does not get to the region the party walks** (the walk region rooted at every anchor): the reach graph (`World::body_reach` — walking, falling, jumping, climbing, swimming) is flooded from that cell's leaving cells and, for a boat, from the water under the hull, and must stand a body in the walk region. The graph is asked once per closure, not once per cell (`OffWalk`): the states of a closure asked that reach the walk region over its own edges are kept, and a later cell starting in one of them is answered from them; any other cell asks for its own closure. A rail into a pit is this; a ledge a rider drops from to the party's floor is not. Build tier. Prescription: a way off that stretch, `bounds` cut short of it, or the medium ended before it. |
| `DW1099` | **A vehicle nobody can board, or that cannot carry the party**: a mark outside the vehicle's own `bounds`; no boarding cell; or a declared ride whose leg no walk crosses and whose boardable vehicles seat fewer than four between them (the steering table's seats), because a required ride carries the whole party and the engine never proves a party split. A declared ride beside a walk seats whoever takes it. Build tier. Prescription: the mark inside `bounds`; a floor beside the vehicle; more vehicles at the leg, or a walk. |
