# `delvec::compiler::nav::vehicle`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/nav/vehicle.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW1096–DW1099 — a body carries another (`dsl::vehicle` / `compiler::nav::vehicle`; spec-0135; error)

A vehicle is a body the campaign places in `vehicles[]` that a player boards and leaves: a boat, the rider's minecart or a saddled mount. The engine resolves each one's seat (its mark), its `bounds`, and its **medium**, each cut at `bounds`: for a boat, the surface cells of the water a body swims (`World::is_water_surface`) across the boat's footprint; for a cart, the run a push along its `facing` takes from the mark (`compiler::rail::driven_run`) — along the rails through every stretch that does not drive it of at most 45 cells, ending where the rail ends, on a brake, or where such a stretch exceeds that coast (`docs/reference/vehicles-animals-and-targets.md` §6, §11); for a mount, ground at its footprint, out of water, rising at most one block. Its **boarding cells** are the standable cells whose eye reaches its body within the 3-block interaction reach, nearest the mark first; its **leaving cells** are the standable cells within a block past its widest column of a medium cell, a block up or down, and for a mount the cell it stands in.

**A ride is declared.** The route proof rides a leg only where its objective carries `ride` (`Plan::declared_rides`), whether or not a walk crosses it: `nav::vehicle::ride_of` walks the party from the leg's start to the nearest boarding cell it can reach, rides the medium from the mark, and leaves at the leaving cell nearest the leg's end from which the party walks on (then the shorter ride, then cell order; at most 256 leaving cells are tried); a ride moves the vehicle at least one cell, and a cart is left only where its run ends. The path gains a `ride` step in front of the objective's step, at the kind's rate: a boat 5 blocks a second, a mount 4, a cart 2.4 — each a rate the record measured. A declared leg its vehicle does not carry is `DW0311`, naming the vehicle and the miss. A leg with no `ride` is walked and no vehicle is tried for it. Every validate prints `vehicle binding (DW1097–DW1099): <n> vehicle(s) — <kind> <id> medium <m> cell(s) in bounds, board <b> cell(s), leave <l> cell(s), seats <s>; …; <d> objective(s) declare their leg ridden; <r> ride leg(s) on the critical path`, and per branch world `vehicle binding, <branch>: <w> ride leg(s) on its path`. The pacing model does not price a ride.

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1097` | **A vehicle seated outside its medium**: a boat whose footprint is not surface water; a cart off a rail, on a slope, on a powered rail that is powered with a solid block at one end (a launcher runs an empty cart away — the record §11), or whose `facing` does not run along its rail; a mount that cannot stand at its footprint or stands in water. Build tier (exit 3), judged over the assembled world before the route proof's verdict. Prescription: the mark into the medium, or the medium under the mark. |
| `DW1098` | **A medium cell the vehicle reaches from which a body that leaves does not get to the region the party walks** (the walk region rooted at every anchor): the reach graph (`World::body_reach` — walking, falling, jumping, climbing, swimming) is flooded from that cell's leaving cells and, for a boat, from the water under the hull, and must stand a body in the walk region. A rail into a pit is this; a ledge a rider drops from to the party's floor is not. Build tier. Prescription: a way off that stretch, `bounds` cut short of it, or the medium ended before it. |
| `DW1099` | **A vehicle nobody can board, or that cannot carry the party**: a mark outside the vehicle's own `bounds`; no boarding cell; or a declared ride whose leg no walk crosses and whose boardable vehicles seat fewer than four between them (the steering table's seats), because a required ride carries the whole party and the engine never proves a party split. A declared ride beside a walk seats whoever takes it. Build tier. Prescription: the mark inside `bounds`; a floor beside the vehicle; more vehicles at the leg, or a walk. |
