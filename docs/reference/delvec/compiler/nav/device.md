# `delvec::compiler::nav::device`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/nav/device.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1069` | **A door the story addresses is no way's shutter (`compiler::nav::device`; error; exit 1 at validation, exit 3 at build).** spec-0124 §6.1, §7.1. Four shapes under one code, each message opening with its shape. **Shape c** (validation, `nav::device::check_document`): `gating.shut: door` on an edge that is not a `walk`, on a walk with no seam, on a seam whose opening is not one cell wide and two high in a wall (`opening: door`), or on the gate of a `timed_gates[]` entry with `crush`. **Shape d** (validation): an `interact` anchored at a door-shut seam's `anchor/seam-<edge>` that writes `prop` or `release` — the seam's door is the pressable and the gate's opening is its release. **Shape a** (build, `nav::device::check_way_blocks`): a pressable whose block a hand opens (`blockshape::opens_by_hand`) owned by an `interact`, a `use` trigger, a shortcut's unlock or a disarm, whose cells are no door-shut seam's gate region; the message names the pressable, its block and cell, the nearest seam and the two remedies (declare the edge `shut: door` and anchor the step on its seam, or make the pressable a lever, a button or a bell). **Shape b** (build): a door-shut seam whose side it opens to (away from `opens_from`, both sides for `either`) holds no body in the cell beyond the plane or on the cell under it. Binding line, every build: `device binding (DW1069): W way-block(s) among P pressable(s); D door-shut seam(s) among G gated connection(s); n refused`; `validation/devices.json` carries `way_blocks`. Prescription: a door the story opens is the shutter of its way — `gating.shut: door` on the connection and the step an `interact` on `anchor/seam-<edge>`. |
| `DW1071` | **A key's effect is out of sight (`compiler::nav::device`; error; exit 3).** spec-0124 §6.3, §7.3 (`nav::device::check_keys`). For every `interact` with `requires_item`, every world write its completion performs (`plan::effect_writes` over its quest's `on_objective_complete` bundle, nested lists included) passes when some cell of the region written is in sight of the key's cell by `DW0863`'s own predicate (`promise::act_unfound`, within 64 blocks, nine lines, stopped only by a cell that hides what is behind it), or when a `cutscene` in the same completion has a shot (or the cutscene itself) whose `look_at` or anchor subject resolves within two cells of the region or names its gate; a door-shut seam's release at the key's own anchor passes by construction. The message names the objective, the region, the reason sight failed, and the two remedies. Binding line: `key binding (DW1071): K key objective(s) among O; E write(s) judged, s in sight, c shown by a shot, r the key's own door released; n refused`; `validation/devices.json` carries `keys`. |
| `DW1072` | **A door or fence gate closes no opening (`compiler::nav::device`; error; exit 3).** spec-0124 §6.5, §5.4, §7.4 (`nav::device::check_doors`). The population is every `*_door` lower half and every `*_fence_gate` the census's one door reading (`census::doors`) enters over the whole of the blocks as shipped (`assembled::shipped_blocks`) — pieces, the stand-in, props, a door-shut seam's shutter; trapdoors are not entered. Each door `D` is asked two questions of the reach graph, from both side cells (before and behind along `facing`, at its course or the one under it), each the graph's own confined walk (`World::body_flood`) over `D`'s window — its column and its jambs', one cell before and behind, from a course under it to the course over its upper half — in a world that opens nothing (`World::without_gate_use`): **clause 1**, with every door and gate shut, no body passes from one side to the other round the leaf; **clause 2**, with `D` alone open, both sides are rest states and each reaches the other through it. A door no hand opens is a full cube to the occupancy model; its open state is read as the one door shape class (an oak door of the same `facing`, `hinge` and `open`). A room's doors are judged one at a time, `N + 1` configurations. A piece's spatial contract may declare `decorative[]` (`{at, what}`, the door's lower cell or the gate's cell in the piece's frame); a declared door is exempt when no hand opens it, or when no rest state of the reach graph rooted at the spawn has an eye within `metrics::BLOCK_INTERACTION_RANGE` (`4.5`) of its cells — the eye box over the body's footprint (`[0.3, 0.7]` of the cell) from the crouched eye (`metrics::PLAYER_CROUCHED_EYE_HEIGHT`, `1.27`) to the standing eye raised by `metrics::MAX_JUMP_RISE_16` ([`device-logic.md`](../../../device-logic.md) §6). A declared wooden door a player can touch is refused with the nearest rest cell, the eye and the distance; an entry aimed at no door or gate is refused. Binding line: `DW1072 binding: N door(s) and gate(s) in the assembled world (C in bound pieces, the census's N); d declared decorative [cells]; J judged over K configuration(s); n refused`; `validation/devices.json` carries `doors`, one row per door and gate. Prescription: remove the door, join wall to both sides of it at both courses, or declare a decoration no hand opens or none a player can reach. |
