# `delvec::compiler::nav::learned`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/nav/learned.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW1053/DW1054 — a shut way declares how it is learned (`compiler::nav::learned`; error; exit 3)

The rule is spec-0118. Judged at build, from the same call site as `DW1011` and over the same model: the configurations, a gate's faces, the party's reach and `can_be` are `compiler::nav::shutmodel`'s (`Configurations`, `faces_of`, `party_reach` — the reach graph's closure from the route cells, `World::body_reach` (spec-0113), a closure past its budget refusing `DW1042` with no verdict — and `meet`; `can_be` is the graph's rest predicate, `World::rest_kind`), the functions `DW1011` ([`nav.md`](../nav.md)) reads, held together by an agreement test that drives both proofs over generated worlds and asserts they meet the same (gate, configuration, face) triples at a face. **The eye** is the one sight rule, `framing::in_sight`, asked through `nav::learned::eye_meets`: from the player's eye (`cell_center` raised by `metrics::PLAYER_EYE_HEIGHT`, 1.62 — not the 1.5 mob eye `World::has_line_of_sight` uses), nine lines to a target cell — its centre and eight corners inset — a target seen when one line meets no cell outside the target that **hides** (`World::hides`: the block state occludes with a full-cube occlusion shape in the pinned jar, read from `crates/dsl/data/occlusion-1.21.11.tsv` through `dsl::blockshape::hides`), and by nothing else — iron bars, glass, a pane, a fence, a wall, a stair, a slab, a door, a trapdoor, leaves and a campfire are looked through ([`sight-through-blocks.md`](../../../sight-through-blocks.md)). A sealed gate cell hides exactly when the block that seals it does (the world-load seal's bytes, `GateSeal::hiding`; a fill's block, `RegionState::fill_hides`). **Population**: every gated connection (`Edge::shuts()`) whose seam gate `anchor/seam-<edge>` the world resolves — a connection declared `shortcut` **included** (unlike `DW1011`), because the wrong side of a one-way door is the face this rule is about. For each configuration the critical path passes through, each connection shut in it (every region cell solid), and each of its two faces, the triple is a **shut way met** when the party's reach holds a cell of the face (*at a face*; a face no body can be in on one side leaves the pair unstood, as `DW1011` counts it), or when the nearest reach cell on that face's side of the gate's axis — by moves from the route, then by cell — has an eye that reaches a cell of the gate region that does not itself hide (*by the eye alone*: a grille in view; a shutter that hides reads as the wall it stands in). **Junction and branch**: a shortest-path tree over the party's reach graph (its own edges, `Reach::succ` with the open sea joined, so the tree holds exactly the closure's cells) from the configuration's route cells, fewest leaps (flown launches) first and then fewest moves, so a branch is walked cell by cell wherever it can be (those a body can be in) reaches the face's nearest reached cell (or, met by the eye, the seeing cell); the path's last route cell is the junction `J`, the cells after it the branch, whose length (moves) and rise (highest `y` on it minus `J`'s) are written and never judged. The **branch ground** is the ground only the branch leads to: the reach graph's closure from the face's reached cells (`World::body_flood`, unconfined), less the closure of the route cells over the world with a **cut** walled up (the open side, `World::body_flood` over `World::walls_over`: a cut left out of the walk would be crossed in the air), less every cell beside the open side (a cut cell from which one move of the graph, `World::body_edges`, lands on it; any other cell from which one step of the graph's cell relation — an edge that is no flown launch — does), less the route cells — empty on an open floor, which is not a branch, and empty for a way met by the eye alone, which pays for no walk. **The cut** is the narrowest cross-section the branch path passes that leaves ground behind it: first the junction and the branch path's own cells, one body wide; where the open side flows round them, the same with the full declared width of one station of a declared way the path walks — spec-0120's portals (planes included), stair runs, contacts, way places and piece ways, taken through `compiler::nav::width::plan_cross_sections` (each station's cells the cells a body passing any of its columns can be in, its width its column count) — narrowest first, nearer the face first among equals; where none leaves ground, the ground is empty and the cut is the path's. A branch that walks no declared way is cut by its own cells alone: no width is measured where none is declared. **The four words**: `seen` — from `J` the eye reaches some region cell (a bar behind a grille, a fence or a pane is seen); `told` — `QuestsContent::answers_press_at(anchor/seam-<edge>)`, the predicate `DW0429` reads; `rewarded` — a reward anchor resolves to a point in the branch ground or a face-neighbour of it: a `collect`'s `anchor` (and the `holder` it adopts), an `interact`'s `anchor`, a `loot[]` entry's `anchor`, the `anchor` of the wave a `kill` names, an `ambush`'s `at`; a `reach-anchor` is not a reward; `hidden` — judged in **every** configuration the way is shut in, met or not: (a) for every reach cell, in order of moves from the route, no eye line reaches a target — a cell of the gate region that does not itself hide, or a cell a body can be in inside the place on the far side (an eye in one of the way's two places looks for the other; an eye in a third place for the side the party does not reach in that configuration, and where the reach holds both sides or neither, for the side across the gate's axis); (b) the shot — every effect list that opens the seam (`open-gate` of `anchor/seam-<edge>`) carries a `cutscene` whose `look_at`, a `path` waypoint, or a shot's `look_at`, `path` or anchor `subject` names the seam (`nav::learned::opening_lists`, the one document-level answer `DW1060` reads too); (c) the text — `DW1060`'s, at validation ([`telling.md`](../telling.md)). **Binding line**, printed before the verdict on every build that walks: `shut ways (DW1053/DW1054): G gated connection(s); over C configuration(s), M shut way(s) met — A at a face, E by the eye alone — D declared (S seen, T told, R rewarded, H hidden), U undeclared, F declaration(s) not confirmed; I declared and never met shut; K hidden way(s) judged over V configuration(s), X eye line(s) examined`, `F` counting the declared triples not confirmed and each configuration a hidden way met nowhere fails in; ledger `validation/shut-ways.json` (`compiler::nav::ShutWayBinding`): the counts, `inert_ways`, one row per shut way met — `connection`, `gating`, `configuration`, `face` (the node on that side), `openable`, `met` (`at a face` or `by_the_eye_from`), `junction`, `branch_length`, `branch_rise`, `branch_cut` (`{at: "path", width: 1}`, or the station, its `way` and its `width`), `learned`, `confirmed`, and each word's finding (`seen`: `seen_from`/`bar_cell` or `blocked_at`; `told`: `answered_by` or `at`; `rewarded`: `rewarded_by` and its `kind`, or `ground_cells` and the `nearest` reward with its `distance`) — and one `hidden_rows` entry per hidden way and configuration it is shut in (`reach`, `targets`, `eye_lines`, `eye`, `shots`, `text`). A build that judges no gated connection writes it with every count `0`; a refused build writes no tree, so the red rows reach the reader through the message.

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `DW1053` | **A shut way the party meets, learned by no declared means.** A gated connection met shut at a face, or seen by the eye alone (above), whose `gating` names no `learned`. Raised once per connection, at the first configuration and face it is met, every failing connection named (six, then a count). The message names the connection with its class and gating, the configuration (`while \`obj/…\` is next`), the face by the node on that side and whether it is the side the way opens from, how it is met (*the party meets it at* the face, or *the party sees it from* a cell), `J`, the branch's length and rise, and what each word would need there: `seen` — the bar is seen from `J`, or hidden behind the named cells; `told` — the trigger that answers a press at `anchor/seam-<edge>`, or that nothing does; `rewarded` — the reward on the branch, or none within the branch's `N` cell(s) and the nearest; `hidden` — the eye line that sees it (so it cannot be hidden as built), or that nothing in the reach sees it, and whether the opening beat carries a shot of its seam. Repair: write the word that is true, or make one true and write it. The gallery's probes `a-shut-way-nobody-is-told-of` (the site-plan point with `learned` removed from `edge/east-door`, the one-way door met from its wrong side) and `a-shut-way-seen-and-never-walked-to` red it. |
| `DW1054` | **A declaration the world does not confirm.** A shut way met whose connection declares `learned`, and the word does not hold there, or a hidden way, in a configuration it is shut in: `seen`, but from `J` the eye meets a cell that hides before the bar (each blocking cell named); `told`, but no `use` trigger serves `anchor/seam-<edge>` (`QuestsContent::gate_answers`, the predicate `DW0429` reads; the message carries the trigger JSON `DW0429` prescribes, `dsl::trigger::press_answer_prescription` — the gate's own answer at a cell of its bar — and the knocker form beside it, so the two rules name one remedy); `rewarded`, but no pickup, interaction or fight stands within the branch's `N` cell(s) behind the cut (named), the nearest reward anchor and its distance named; `hidden`, but the way is seen — the eye cell, the target and what it is (the gate's block, with *is looked through; a shutter that hides is a block the pinned game draws as a full opaque cube*, or *a cell a body can be in in* the far place); `hidden`, but the beat that opens it carries no shot of its seam — the effect list named, or that no list opens it. Repair, the world's or the word's: open the geometry so the bar is seen, write the trigger, place the reward, make the shutter hide, wall the line of sight or move the eye's ground, add the shot to the opening beat, or change the word to the one that is true. **As a warning**, the same code names a word on a connection never met shut in any judged configuration — a `hidden` one: never shut in any — counted into `I`, never refused: the judged configurations are the critical path's and the closure is branch-blind, so a way met shut only on a branch's own path is not in them, and refusing there would reject a legitimate design for an engine limitation (the rule `DW0891` states for a volume no modelled body reaches). The gallery's probes `a-way-said-told-that-nothing-answers` (`edge/undercroft-crawl` declared `told`, no `use` trigger serving its seam), `a-hidden-way-the-eye-looks-through` and `a-hidden-way-with-no-shot` red it. |
