# `delvec::compiler::assembly`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/assembly.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW0935–DW0938 — a fixed thing that can be hit and hits back (`dsl::validate` + `compiler::assembly`; error)

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DW0936` | **An assembly's hitbox cannot be struck as declared** (spec-0082 §5.3). Build tier (exit 3), `compiler::assembly::judge`. A width outside `(0, 6]` or a height outside `(0, 22]` — vanilla registers an attack on an interaction only within 3.3 blocks of its position toward −X/−Z and 22.6 above (Minecraft Wiki, *Interaction*), so a larger box has a slab no swing lands on; a box that meets none of the cells the spawned parts stand in (the initial clip's first frame, or the rest pose — the player strikes what the player sees), the message naming those cells; or a `strike-assembly` naming an assembly that declares no hitbox. An aimed pattern's box is judged against the spawned parts at every facing a player can draw. The hitbox also enters `eclipse::affordances` at its declared size, so a body on it is `DW0359` and a press body inside it is `DW0878`. |
| `DW0937` | **A strike the critical path performs cannot be reached** (spec-0082 §5.3). Build tier (exit 3), `compiler::assembly::judge`, asked before the hitbox's mark rule. For every `strike-assembly` trigger the exported path performs, some cell the party can walk to — `World::reachable_walkable` from `DW0891`'s roots under the configuration of the next objective step after the strike, less every cell outside the playable region — holds an eye within `strand::STRIKE_REACH` (3.0 blocks) of the hitbox's box, measured by `strand::eye_reaches_box`, the measure `DW0924` uses. Prescription: lower the mark or the hitbox's `offset` toward a floor the party stands on, or give the party footing within reach. |
| `DW0938` | **A blow lands where it was not announced, or its area is not the area its limb comes down on** (spec-0082 §5.4: danger is visible, or the engine refuses it; §5.7 for aim). Build tier (exit 3), `compiler::assembly::judge`, one code and two shapes over every `damage-players` in a step's `on_land` at any depth, at every facing a player can draw. **Shape 1**: every cell of the landing box (turned to the facing) grown to its keep-out (`metrics::keep_out_box(Body::PLAYER, …)`) lies inside `while_in`'s keep-out — a player who never entered the arming region is never struck; a landing `damage-players` with no `in` strikes every player in the world and is refused here too. **Shape 2, both ways, one rule** (`assembly::correspondence`): a walked cell (`lethal::walked_population`, spec-0062's `P`) is **landed on** when a part of the strike clip's last frame, turned to the facing and judged exactly (part box against box, `rig::Transform::meets_box`), meets the footprint of a body standing there in the floor band (`assembly::floor_band_body`: the body's 0.6 width round the cell's centre, from the floor up `FLOOR_BAND` = 1 block). **Forward** — every walked cell of the landing box is landed on, else refused naming them and where the last frame does land (a limb hanging over a body's head has not landed on it); and something comes down within the blow's catch — a strike that lands on no caught cell its first frame did not already stand on is refused (a base standing on the landing cell is not a blow). **Back** — every walked cell the last frame lands on that the strike clip's first frame did not lies in the landing box's keep-out, the stated tolerance (one cell round the box for a player), else refused naming them: a long limb's blow is a long area along where it lands. Cell footprints are exact (`rig::Transform::cells`: separating-axis, never the hull). An aimed pattern's `damage-players` inside another effect's list cannot be turned and is refused; so is an empty `pattern`. Refusals at a turned facing name it (`facing k of n`, its turn and root yaw). **Not judged**, by spec-0016 §3's standing ruling: the wind-up's length, `hold` and `amount` — a one-frame wind-up, a hold of 0 and a blow that kills an unhurt player build with no finding; `validation/assembly.json` states them beside the caught cells, and per step and facing the root yaw, the cells the limb comes down on and a stand cell; the critical path carries the bot's `witness-strike` pair (struck on the stand cell, spared outside the arming region) before the first step that strikes the assembly. |

### DW0968–DW0970 — a strike locks where the player stands (`dsl::validate` + `compiler::assembly`; error)

This module's rows of a section whose prose is on the [`delvewright_dsl::assembly` page](../../dsl/assembly.md#dw0968dw0970--a-strike-locks-where-the-player-stands-dslvalidate--compilerassembly-error).

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DW0968` | **A locked strike can lock onto a cell it cannot strike** (spec-0094 §5.1). Build tier (exit 3), `compiler::assembly::judge` over `assembly::lock_plan`, per locked step. For every cell of `lock.within` in the walked population (`lethal::walked_population`), the bearing from the mark cell's centre to the cell's centre; per candidate clip — the step's `strike`, then each of `lock.reaches` — the turn that points the clip's blow (`assembly::aim_bearing`: the horizontal centroid of the cells its last frame stands in and its first does not) at the cell; the first candidate is taken whose last frame, at that turn, comes down on the cell (`struck_cells` of the last frame less the first, spec-0082 §5.4) with every cell it comes down on caught only from inside `while_in` (shape 1). Refused, naming the cells and why for the first three: a standable cell no candidate takes; a lock region whose keep-out (`metrics::keep_out_box`) leaves `while_in`'s; a lock region with no standable cell. `DW0936`'s spawn-pose rule is asked at every turn a proved cell takes (the `interaction` does not turn). Prescription: add a clip to `lock.reaches` that comes down there (`delvec rig describe` prints every clip's footprint), shrink the lock region to the cells the poses reach, or widen `while_in`. |
