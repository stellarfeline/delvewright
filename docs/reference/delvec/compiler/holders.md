# `delvec::compiler::holders`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/holders.rs`: the holder census (spec-0105, spec-0111), the holder entities a piece carries, the stance pass, and the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## The census

Every build reads the **edited assembled model** — the cells every later proof reads — and collects every cell whose block is one of the twelve container kinds of `delvewright_dsl::blocks::CONTAINER_KINDS_1_21_11`, and every **holder entity** the placed pieces carry (`placed_holders`: each template's `entities`, read by `nbtread::entities`, kept when the kind is one of `delvewright_dsl::holders::ENTITY_HOLDERS_1_21_11`, rotated and translated with the piece — a frame at its `blockPos` with its `Facing` turned, a stand at its foot cell with its yaw turned). To those it adds each container a declaration has the compiler place at runtime: a `collect`'s own chest (neither `holder` nor `dropped_by`), and a `use` / `strike` trigger's or an `interact` objective's container `prop`. Each row carries its **kind** (`container`, `item-frame`, `glow-item-frame`, `armor-stand`), its **slot vocabulary** (`container.<n>`; `contents`; `armor.head`, `armor.chest`, `armor.legs`, `armor.feet`, `weapon.mainhand`, `weapon.offhand`) with the slots a claim fills marked, and whether it is **frozen** (an entity holder no fill claims) and **exempt**.

**Exempt** (spec-0105 §9.5): a holder whose contents show from outside — every frame and stand, and a brewing stand that no declaration claims or that `loot` / `collect` fill only in its bottle slots (`container.0`–`container.2`, drawn as `has_bottle_N`). An exempt row is never a decoration cell, takes no place in §9's arrangement (neither scenery nor a holder that matters among scenery), and owes no notice. A brewing stand filled in its ingredient or fuel slot, or claimed by a device, is an ordinary container. Each cell is attributed to its **place** — on a site-plan campaign the place whose play space holds it (else whose footprint column holds it, highest floor at or under the cell), otherwise the area whose placed piece holds it; a cell in no place is `(outside every place)` — and to the claims made on it:

| Claim | From | Cell |
|---|---|---|
| `loot` | a `loot[]` entry | its anchor's cell |
| `collect` | a `collect` adopting `holder`; a `collect`'s own chest | the adopted cell; the `anchor` cell |
| `device` | a `trapped-chest` trap, or any trap's dispenser (`dispenser` in the trap anchor's metadata); a `use` / `strike` trigger or an `interact` objective whose cell holds a container or whose `prop` is one | the `at` / `anchor` cell |
| `decoration` | nothing | the remainder |

A cell may carry `loot` and `device` together (the real chest among false trapped chests). Rows are in cell order (ADR-0006).

**The binding line**, on every build, after the `holder binding` line (`holder binding: F fill(s) (L loot, C adopting collect; by kind: <kind>: n, …), J judged against the assembled world, D deferred …`):

```
holder census: N holder(s) in the assembled world (<kind>: n, …); L loot, C collect, V device, D decoration, E empty (frozen), X exempt (contents show from outside); notice <declared|none>; by place: <place>: n, …; arrangement: M mixed place(s), K over the cap of 2 decoration cell(s) per place. stance: F filled holder(s) and H holder(s) judged, U unsupported, R unreachable.[ S place(s) still stand in their stand-in, whose containers this build cannot see (<place>, …); <verdict>.]
```

Then `holder edits: K runtime edit(s) examined against H holder entit(y/ies), 0 reaching a holder (DW1038).` The census is taken once the world model exists (after the relight), so the stance pass can stand a body; its line is printed before any of its verdicts.

**The stand-in deferral.** The census judges what the build can see, as the container fills do (`loot::split_owed_by_detail`): on a site-plan campaign, a place still standing in its stand-in (`Blockout::massed`) is massing nobody furnished, and its containers are owed by its detail. While any such place remains, `DW1018`'s notice-with-no-scenery arm is deferred to the build that details them, and the line names the places; a scenery container the build does see is judged as ever. An `areas[]` campaign, or one with every place detailed, defers nothing and prints no deferral clause.

**The ledger** `validation/holders.json`, on every build: `census_input_sha256` (SHA-256 over every `(cell, state)` of the model the census read, in its order, then every holder entity's kind and cell), `holders` (N), `cells` (container rows), `loot`, `collect`, `device`, `decoration`, `empty`, `exempt`, `by_kind`, `by_block`, `by_place`, `max_scenery_per_place`, `mixed_places`, `over_cap_places`, `stand_ins` (the places whose containers this build cannot see, in plan order; empty when none), `stance` (`{unsupported, unreachable}`), `notice` (`{text, style, sound}` with the line's plain English text and its namespaced sound event or `null`; or `null`) and `rows[]` of `{cell, kind, block, slots[{slot, filled}], frozen, exempt, place, claims[{kind, id}]}`.

## Holder entities: owned, summoned, taken (spec-0111)

`own_holders` derives, once, each holder entity's tag id (`dw_holder_<id>`: the claiming anchor's stem, or `at_<x>_<y>_<z>` for a holder no fill claims), its fills and its claims; emission, the PackTest and the critical path read that value. The build ships every template with an empty `entities` list (`admit::structure::as_placed`) and summons each holder from its own body function `holder_<id>`, called from `setup_finish` — `execute unless entity @e[tag=dw_holder_<id>] run summon …` with the NBT the engine owns (the piece's scene properties, rotated; `Tags:["dw_fixture","dw_holder","dw_holder_<id>"]`; a frame's `Pos` and `block_pos` (the body loads where it hangs), its `Fixed` and `Invulnerable` `0b` while it starts holding an item and `1b` otherwise, `ItemDropChance:1.0f`; a stand's `Marker:0b`, `Invulnerable:1b`, `ShowArms` forced on when a hand slot is filled, and `DisabledSlots` with putting disabled on all six slots and taking disabled on every slot no fill claims) — then `item replace entity @e[tag=dw_holder_<id>,limit=1] <slot> with <item>[components] <count>` for every `loot` stack. A `collect` adopting a holder entity fills its first slot (`contents`, `armor.head`) at activation and unfreezes a frame. `tick` carries, when a frame holder exists, `execute as @e[type=#<ns>:frames,tag=dw_holder] unless items entity @s contents * run data merge entity @s {Fixed:1b,Invulnerable:1b}` (the `#<ns>:frames` entity-type tag names the two frame kinds): an emptied frame freezes the next tick. Every campaign's setup writes `gamerule entity_drops true`.

**The stance pass** (`check_stance`): every holder is kept by the game — a frame's backing block (`cell − facing`) solid in the edited model and its own cell clear, a stand's foot cell standable — and every filled holder has a standable cell in the party's walk region from whose eye its body box (a frame's 12/16 × 12/16 × 1/16 plate on its face, a stand's 0.5 × 1.975 column, halved when `Small`) is within `INTERACTION_REACH` (3.0) and in sight (`nav::stance_cells`, the generalised `sees_body`). A holder `collect`'s critical-path step carries the stance cell nearest the holder.

**The arrangement rule** (spec-0105 §9). A place is the neighbourhood a player searches. `MAX_SCENERY_PER_PLACE` is **2**, authored from the *Oblivion* Construction Set wiki's dungeon practice (one to three containers per ordinary room, one or two of them junk); a double chest is two cells and counts two.

**The gallery.** The primary holds eleven holders: seven container cells — the hall's five all claimed (`loot/pedestal-case`, `loot/ferryman-stores`, `loot/gallery-end-stores` — a glass bottle in the long gallery's brewing stand —, `obj/take-the-emeralds`, `trap/chest`), an empty brewing stand beside the filled one (exempt), and the long hall's end chest, the one scenery container, spoken for by `containers.decoration_notice` — and four holder entities the hall carries: a glow frame (`loot/map-frame`, a named map in `contents`), an armour stand (`loot/sword-stand`, a helmet in `armor.head` and a sword in `weapon.mainhand`), a plain frame `obj/take-the-seal` takes from on the critical path, and an empty frame, frozen. The site-plan overlay's loft program marks an armour stand with a `pose` and `show_arms` (`holder` on a `mark`). Refusal probes: `a-barrel-nobody-spoke-for` and `a-notice-over-a-stocked-town` (`DW1018`), `a-loot-chest-with-nothing-in-it`, `a-notice-with-nothing-to-say` and `a-chest-conjured-at-runtime` (`DW1019`), `a-key-among-empty-chests` and `a-storeroom-of-nothing` (`DW1024`), `a-sword-on-the-stands-chest` and `a-stand-fill-with-no-slot` (`DW1035`), `a-second-stack-in-a-frame` and `a-chest-fill-aimed-at-a-frame` (`DW0431`), `a-frame-nobody-can-reach` and `a-stand-on-air` (`DW1037`), `a-wall-cleared-from-under-a-frame` (`DW1038`). The second `DW1018` probe reaches the end chest's unanchored cell with a stage-7 batch framed on the anchor standing on top of it.

## The item-holder class

Spec-0105 §10: `loot[]` and `collect` target an **item holder**, of which the container is one family, and a holder qualifies only if a player in adventure mode can take its item. Read from the pinned 1.21.11 server jar (bundled `server-1.21.11.jar` sha256 `ec47239a…fcebc`, `javap -c -p`, names through `server.txt` sha1 `5621e925…`). The adventure gate on a block is not on the use path — `ServerPlayerGameMode.useItemOn` checks only `GameType.SPECTATOR` before the block's `useItemOn` / `useWithoutItem` — but in `Player.blockActionRestricted` (breaking and placing) and `Abilities.mayBuild`.

| Holder | How a player takes the item | Adventure | Read at | In the engine |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the twelve containers | open the menu | allowed | `ServerPlayerGameMode.useItemOn`, `BaseContainerBlockEntity.createMenu` | `loot` / `collect` / census |
| `item_frame`, `glow_item_frame` | attack: the item pops out | allowed — `ServerGamePacketListenerImpl$1.onAttack`, `BlockAttachedEntity.skipAttackInteraction` (spawn protection only), `ItemFrame.hurtServer` → `shouldDamageDropItem` | those methods | holder entity, spec-0111 |
| `armor_stand` | right-click the slot, empty hand | allowed — `ArmorStand.interactAt` → `swapItem` reads the marker flag and `DisabledSlots` only; breaking is `mayBuild`-gated in `hurtServer` | those methods | holder entity, spec-0111 |
| `chiseled_bookshelf` | right-click an occupied slot | allowed — `ChiseledBookShelfBlock.useWithoutItem`; books only | that method | follow-up F3 |
| `shelf` | right-click a slot, main hand | allowed — `ShelfBlock.useItemOn` → `swapSingleItem`; not admitted by `admit/allowlist.rs` | that method | follow-up F3 |
| `jukebox` | right-click: the disc is ejected | allowed — `JukeboxBlock.useWithoutItem` → `popOutTheItem`; not admitted | that method | follow-up F3 |
| `decorated_pot` | an empty-hand click only wobbles; the stack returns when the pot breaks | breaking by hand is refused without `can_break`; a projectile shatters it (`DecoratedPotBlock.onProjectileHit`: `Projectile.mayInteract`, no game-mode check, and `mayBreak`: `#minecraft:impact_projectiles` and `projectiles_can_break_blocks`) | those methods | **excluded**; follow-up F4 only if the party's impact projectile or `can_break` tool is proven structurally |
| `lectern` | the *Take Book* button | **refused** — `LecternMenu.clickMenuButton` case 3 returns `Player.mayBuild()` | that method, offset 91 | **excluded** |
| `campfire` | none — no `useWithoutItem`; a stack leaves only cooked | — | `CampfireBlock` | **excluded** |
| `item_display` | none — `Display` overrides no interaction and is not pickable | — | `Display`, `Display$ItemDisplay` | **excluded** |

## Diagnostics

### DW1037 / DW1038 — a holder the party cannot take from (spec-0111)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1037` | A holder the party cannot take from (spec-0111 §6.3). Build-tier (exit 3), judged on the census after `DW1018` / `DW1024`. Two arms, one message listing every holder that fails: **UNSUPPORTED** — a frame whose backing block is not solid or whose own cell is solid (the game drops it within five seconds, `ItemFrame.survives` every 100 ticks), or a stand whose foot cell is not standable — naming the cell, the block and what the game would do; **UNREACHABLE** — a filled holder no standable, walk-reachable cell sees within `INTERACTION_REACH` (3.0), naming the holder, its slots and the nearest walkable cell. Remedies, at the piece: move the holder, open the sightline, or stand it on (hang it from) the solid block it needs. A campaign whose holders all pass prints `0 unsupported, 0 unreachable` on the census line with its denominators. |
| `DW1038` | A runtime edit reaches a holder (spec-0111 §6.4). Build-tier (exit 3), `check_runtime_edits`: a `set-block`, `fill-region` or `clear-region` effect at any root and depth whose cells include a holder entity's cell or the block it hangs from or stands on. The edit would pop the frame or drop the stand mid-game, silently. The message names the effect, its path, the holder and the cell they share; the remedy is to move the holder or shrink the region. A stage-7 `morph`, `carve` or `fill` is in the edited model already and is judged by `DW1037`. |

### DW101x / DW102x — the container class (spec-0105)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1018` | The containers built and the containers declared disagree (an exempt holder — spec-0105 §9.5 — is never scenery here). Build-tier (exit 3), judged on the census after `DW0431` / `DW0438` have proven every fill names a real container. Two arms. **Scenery with no notice:** a container no `loot`, `collect`, trap, trigger or objective claims, and no `containers.decoration_notice` — a player would open it to an empty grid with nothing having told them the place holds nothing. The message lists every decoration cell with its block, cell, place and slots, and per cell names the anchor standing on it (a `loot` entry on that anchor fills it) or says no anchor does (the piece or program of its place lands it). Remedies: fill each one, replace it with furniture that is not a container, or declare the notice. **A notice with nothing to speak for:** every container is claimed, so the notice would tell the player something false about the world (`game-writing.md` §8 L2); the remedy is to delete it. While a place still stands in its stand-in this arm is deferred to the build that details it, stated on the census line. A campaign whose every container is claimed and which declares no notice passes with `D 0`. |
| `DW1024` | Scenery containers arranged as a storeroom, or a container that matters standing among them (spec-0105 §9; an exempt holder takes no part, §9.5). Build-tier (exit 3), judged on the census after `DW1018`. Two arms, one message listing every offending place: **MIXED** — a place holding a `loot` / `collect` / `device` cell and a decoration cell; a player looking for what matters opens every one; remedies: fill every scenery container there, replace it with furniture that is not a container, or move the one that matters to a place where every container pays off (a trap among loot is allowed). **STOREROOM** — a place holding more than `MAX_SCENERY_PER_PLACE` (2) decoration cells; remedies: fill some, or replace the surplus in the place's piece or program. A census with no decoration passes with `0 mixed place(s), 0 over the cap`. |
