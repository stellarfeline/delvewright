# `delvec::compiler::approach`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/approach.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW1025 — a body the party talks to faces them (`compiler::approach`; error; exit 3)

A still body keeps the yaw it was given: its summon writes its home anchor's declared `facing` (`watching::npc_home`), and a walk leaves it on the walk's arrival turn (`nav::apply_arrival_yaw`). Neither knows which way the party comes from. **Every NPC a player can talk to watches by default**: an NPC with a dialogue tree that declares no `watch` turns to the nearest player within 8 blocks (`dsl::DEFAULT_WATCH_WITHIN`, the look distance the game's own `LookAtPlayerGoal` is constructed with at 22 of its 37 three-argument sites in the pinned server jar; `dsl::body_watch_sites` resolves it, so the emitter, `DW0997`, the PackTests and the bot read one answer). An NPC whose scene needs a body that does not turn declares `"watch": "none"` and holds its facing. DW1025 judges two shapes.

**A talk on a walked route** (`approach::check`, after the branch routes). Per configuration — the exported critical path and every realised branch's own path, each with the legs its route proof walked — and per `talk-to` step on it. **The approach**: the leg delivering the party to the step (the last leg whose `to_step` is at or before it and whose last cell completes the step), read back from its last cell to the first cell at least the interaction reach (3 blocks, `crosshair::INTERACTION_REACH`) horizontally from the body's feet — the party's last walked cell before it comes into reach to talk; the bearing from the body's feet to that cell is the direction the party arrives from. The cells inside reach are not read: the route ends on the snapped station, often the body's own cell, and its one step into that cell is the search's tie-break, not a direction anybody walks. A leg that never leaves reach is read at its cell farthest from the body, one block or more away. **The body's yaw at that beat**: the home summon's, when the step's station is the body's home cell, and the arrival yaw of every `move-npc` whose destination mark's own cell is the station (`nav::MovePlan::mark_cell`); each is judged. A yaw within 90° of the approach bearing faces the party (the front half-plane, closed; the threshold is authored, as the least reading of "not with its back to them"). A body whose watch (declared or default) is `nearest` and whose `within` reaches the approach cell from every stance is counted as drawn and not judged; a class watch turns only to that class, so it is judged by its yaw; a body declaring `"none"` is counted as held and judged by the second shape. One refusal per body, objective, stance and yaw, at the first configuration that shows it.

Every build that walks prints `approach binding (DW1025): <talks> talk beat(s) over <n> configuration(s): <j> judged by facing (<y> yaw(s), <r> refused), <w> drawn by a watch, <h> held by declaration (judged from every cell), <u> reached by no walked leg, <p> with no resolved body`, zeroes included; `u` counts talks a ride or no leg delivers the party to, and `p` talks whose station neither the home cell nor any walk's destination resolves.

**A held facing, from every approach** (`approach::check_held`, after the world block). Per NPC declaring `"watch": "none"`, at every stance it holds a yaw the compiler fixed — its home summon, then each `move-npc` arrival — every cell of the walked population `P` (`lethal::walked_population`, the population the watchers are judged over) from which a player can talk to it: the player's eye (feet plus 1.62) within the interaction reach (3 blocks) of the nearest point of the body's interaction hitbox (`emit::NPC_HITBOX_WIDTH` 1.0 wide, `emit::NPC_HITBOX_HEIGHT` 2.0 tall, on the body's feet; `approach::within_interaction_reach`). Cells less than a block from the body horizontally (its own column) have no bearing and are not judged; a cell inside a killing volume holds no live player and is not judged. A stance any judged cell lies more than 90° behind is refused. Every build prints `held-facing binding (DW1025): <c> cell(s) judged over <b> body(ies) declaring `watch: "none"` at <s> stance(s): <k> cell(s) behind a body, <r> stance(s) refused, <n> stance(s) no walked cell reaches`, zeroes included.

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1025` | **A body the party talks to has its back to them.** Either shape. *On a walked route*: at a `talk-to` step of some configuration, the bearing from the body's feet to the party's last walked cell before it comes into reach to talk is more than 90° off the yaw the body holds at that beat, and no `nearest` watch reaches the approach. Names the body, the objective, the configuration, the body's cell and yaw with where the yaw comes from (the home anchor's facing, or the walk whose arrival turn it is), the approach cell and its bearing, and the angle between them. Prescription: declare `watch: { "who": "nearest", "within": N }` on the npc, `N` the approach distance's ceiling and never less than the interaction reach (3), so the body turns to the party as they arrive. *A held facing*: a body declaring `"watch": "none"` holds a yaw some cell a player can talk to it from lies more than 90° behind. Names the body, the stance's cell and yaw with its source, how many of the judged cells lie behind it, and the one most behind with its bearing and angle. Prescription: when one cardinal facing faces every judged cell, declare the anchor's facing that way in its piece's metadata (`delvec prefab anchor … --facing <f>`), naming it; when none does, set the body's `offset` (and the cast's mark with it) so a wall or a counter is at its back; or remove `"watch": "none"`, so the body watches by default. Build tier (exit 3). |
