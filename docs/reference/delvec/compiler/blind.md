# `delvec::compiler::blind`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/blind.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW0943 — a blinding beside a drop (`compiler::blind`; error; exit 3)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW0943` | **A blinding grant whose reach meets a killing volume or a fatal drop** (spec-0085 §6). A `give-effect` of a blinding effect (`dsl::perception::BLINDING`: `blindness`, which forbids the sprint, and `darkness`, which does not — the two whose screen hides the floor; `nausea` hides nothing) is judged wherever it is written. Its **standing set** `S` is the standable cells of its `in` box; else, for a grant addressed to the party (a stated `party`, or a root whose default is `@a`), the walked population `P` (`DW0891`'s) — every player is blinded wherever they stand; else, for a grant addressed to the actor, where the actor stands, by root — the cells within an `approach` trigger's `range`; within interaction reach of a `use`/`strike` trigger or a trapped chest; the plate or wire and the cells beside it for a pressure-plate or tripwire trap; a `reach-anchor` completion's footprint (`reach::ReachJudgement`); the respawn seats for `on_death` and a dialogue `on_respawn`; the seat for a `set-checkpoint`'s `on_respawn`; and `P` everywhere else (a shop, an `on_kill`, a non-reach objective, an `on_caught`), the largest set there is, so a fallback refuses more and never less. A grant at a timeline's `at_ticks > 0` without an `in` box is widened first by the moves a seeing body makes in that delay, at the sprint. Its **reach** `n` is `ceil(seconds × speed)` body moves at walking speed (4.317) for blindness and sprinting speed (5.612, `dsl::metrics::SPRINT_SPEED_BLOCKS_PER_SECOND`, cited from the wiki's *Sprinting*) for darkness. The **blind reach** `R` floods `S` by `n` moves of `World::body_moves` — walk, survivable fall, jump — over `World::without_exclusions`, the counterfactual `DW0891` measures over (over the world the router walks the keep-out is already impassable and the check would bind to nothing; `tests/v35_perception.rs::the_reach_is_judged_over_the_lethality_free_world` hands it that world and watches the rim grant go green). A grant is **caught** when a cell of `R` lies in a killing volume's keep-out, or a standing cell of `R` has beside it a column a body steps off into a fall it does not survive (`World::fatal_step_off`: nothing arrests it within 22 blocks, or lava does). An `in` box over no standable cell is refused with a count of zero. A caught cell is one in a killing volume's keep-out, or a standing cell beside which a body steps off into a fall it does not survive or into lava (a pool let into the floor included). Asked after `DW0891` at both entry points (`emit::build` and the world-edits replay) and before the route proofs. Prescription: shorten `seconds`, draw `in` farther from the hazard, use `nausea`, or move the volume as `DW0891` says — never remove a cell from the walk. **Not caught, stated:** a fall that hurts and does not kill; a player already blind from their own potion; momentum a sprint carried into the blinding; a sprint-jump, faster than the sprint the reach is taken at. |

**Binding.** Every build that assembles a world prints `blind-reach binding: G blinding grant(s) examined; standing sets of … cell(s); reaches of … move(s); C caught.` — zeroes included — before the verdict is taken, and `validation/lethal-gate.json` (when the campaign declares a volume) carries `blind_reach` with per grant `standing`, `reach_moves`, `reached` and `caught` (the cells).
