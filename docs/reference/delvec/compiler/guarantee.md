# `delvec::compiler::guarantee`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/guarantee.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW0889 — an anchor outside what the area guarantees (`compiler::guarantee`; advisory)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW0889` | **This campaign names an anchor, and the area that would provide it does not guarantee it.** An area binding a `prefab_pool` seats a **subset** of that pool's members, and the subset is exactly three things: the `entry`-role member, placed at the area origin with rotation `none` before any growth; one carrier for each anchor the campaign makes the solver guarantee (`plan::required_anchors_for_area` then `solver::required_carriers`); and `filler_count` `connector`-role pieces drawn **with replacement** by weight from the area's PRNG stream. Membership in the pool buys a piece nothing: a `room` or `terminal` member nothing requires is never seated, and an anchor it declares does not exist in the built world. **The guaranteed set is therefore the entry member's anchors plus everything the forced carriers declare**, and this code names every anchor of the pool's vocabulary that this campaign's documents carry and that set does not contain. `compiler::guarantee::check`, bound in the one validation funnel every subcommand goes through (`main::validate_loaded`), **validation tier, exit 0**. **Why it is answerable here**: the whole verdict is a fact about declarations — the pool's members, their roles, their `anchors` maps — so no `.nbt` is opened, no piece is placed and no PRNG is drawn; on `compiler::seating`'s precedent it is reported at the step the documents already decided it, rather than at the build, which is five authoring steps after the anchors are chosen. **Severity: advisory (warning, exit 0), deliberately.** The guaranteed set is a *sufficient* condition and not a necessary one: an anchor carried only by `connector` members is seated whenever the filler draw picks that connector, and shipping content rests on exactly that, so an error here would refuse campaigns that build green — the over-refusal shape. The sharp end is unchanged and still the build's: `DW0302` when the pool has no member that could supply a required anchor, `DW0303` when the `pieces` budget cannot fit the forced carriers, `DW0360` when an effect's anchor resolves to no position in the assembled world, `DW0142`/`DW0431`/`DW0447` at their own sites. What was missing was never a refusal — it was the answer arriving in time. **Its quantifier**, which is part of what it says: names this campaign carries that *some* member declares and *no* guaranteed member declares. It says nothing about a name **no** member declares (`DW0302`'s, at the use site) and nothing about a name **two placed pieces** answer to (`DW0498`'s and `DW0305`'s, and unknowable without the settled draw). **How the names are collected**: by serialising the stage documents and taking every string in them, not by walking the verbs that may carry an anchor. A typed walk here would be the twelfth hand-rolled enumeration of *where an anchor can be named* — the shape `CLAUDE.md` names, right the day it is written and silently narrower than the DSL every day after — and it would be wrong first for the verbs `required_anchors_for_area` already does not reach: a `loot` fill, a trap payload, a shop, a lethal volume, a `shortcut`, and an environment trigger in a campaign with more than one area. Serialising cannot forget a field. It over-reports rather than under-reports (a name is collected wherever it appears, so on a multi-area campaign one area's name is offered to every pool whose vocabulary holds it), the surplus is bounded by intersecting with the pool's own vocabulary, and the message says *this campaign names it*, never *this area resolves it*. **Prescription, and every branch of it is reachable from the campaign**: bind a name the area guarantees; or require this one where the solver must seat its carrier (an objective, an NPC stand, a wave spawn, a lane waypoint, or an anchor-bearing effect in this area) — which is the same mechanism, used deliberately; or bind the area to that piece with `prefab` instead of `prefab_pool`, which guarantees everything it declares. Rebuilding the library is **not** among them. **Measured on the shipped content library** (`delvec prefab anchors --json`, this revision): `pool/cave-shore`, `pool/stone-keep` and `pool/vertical-keep` each guarantee 2 anchor names — `spawn` and `anchor/exit`, both on the entry piece — of the 10 their members declare between them; `pool/island` guarantees 11 of 53; 17 of 83 across 42 members in 4 pools. **Binding**: `anchor-guarantee binding:` states pool areas of areas, members examined, the vocabulary they declare, how many of it is guaranteed, how many names the campaign carries and how many were reported — on every `validate`/`analyze`/`build`, including the run that reports nothing. |

**`DW0498` and this code are a pair, and they say different things.** `DW0498`
is about **ambiguity** and needs the settled draw: the pool seated one
anchor-bearing piece twice, so a name has two carriers. `DW0889` is about
**absence** and needs no draw at all: the layout is not required to seat the
piece that carries the name. One area can legitimately raise both about one
anchor — the gallery's `area/annex` does — and neither restates the other. Neither
replaces the other: nothing `DW0498` asserts about a
settled draw is knowable at validation, and nothing `DW0889` asserts about a
guarantee is improved by waiting for one.
