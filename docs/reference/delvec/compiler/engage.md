# `delvec::compiler::engage`

this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW0920 — a fight its body will not take up (`compiler::engage`; error)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW0920` | **A body vanilla's own AI will not engage the party is staged as a fight the party must win.** The mirror of `DW0496`: that rule refuses a fight the sun settles, this one a fight the body walks away from. **Fires when all four hold:** (1) the entity is `minecraft:drowned`; (2) the party is meant to fight it — `DW0496`'s population exactly (`daylight::collect_staged`: a wave a `kill` objective adjudicates, or an actor the party can damage); (3) the level is **bright for as long as the fight is live**; (4) no cell within the stack's aggro radius, on ground walk-reachable from its seated cells, puts a body's feet in water. **Why the drowned, read from the pinned jar:** `Drowned.okTarget(t)` is `!level.isBrightOutside() \|\| t.isInWater()`; it is the predicate on the drowned's player-targeting goal and the gate on its melee goal, and `DrownedGoToWaterGoal` (priority 1, gated on the same call) walks it toward water instead. No vanilla tag names the bodies whose targeting reads the hour: the pinned jar was scanned for every caller of `Level.isBrightOutside()` — thirteen classes, and `Drowned` is the only target gate among them (the others are the fox, the wandering trader, the drowned's own movement goals and the sun-avoiding and village-strolling goals) — so the species list is one id carrying that measurement. A spider's neutrality reads the light at its own position, not the declared hour, and is not this rule's. **The hour table** (`engage::bright_outside`): `isBrightOutside` is `skyDarken < 4`, `skyDarken = (int)(15 − gameplay/sky_light_level)`, the overworld `minecraft:day` timeline multiplying the base 15 by 1.0 from tick 133 to 11867 and falling linearly to 0.26666668 at 13670, rain alpha-blending toward 4.0 at 0.3125 and thunder at 0.52734375. So `day`, `noon` and `dusk` are bright in `clear` and `rain`, and every hour is dark in `thunder`, as are `night`, `midnight` and `dawn`. `dusk` in `rain` is bright by five thousandths of a light level (sky light 11.005, `skyDarken` 3), so the table was taken twice with nothing shared: once from the jar's arithmetic, once from the running game by `tools/maintenance/probe-drowned-engagement.py`, which on the pinned server summons a bare-handed drowned beside a villager in a roofed cell under each of the eighteen states and reads whether it was struck, plus the tightest bright state on a flooded floor and on waterlogged bottom slabs — 20 of 20 cases agree. **The clock, dated against the fight.** The states are `DW0496`'s own clock (`daylight::Clock::sky_for`): every state the body can stand in from the beat that seats it until the fight is decided — here its `kill` objectives, whether or not a rest re-seats the wave, since a body met again after the party has won is no longer a fight it must win; an actor runs to the end of the delve. Vesperhold's cuts to noon, night and dawn are all in quests after the choir, so the choir's span holds dusk in rain alone. **Wet footing** (`engage::wet_footing_within_reach`): a standable cell whose support is waterlogged with its top below a source's surface (8⁄9 of a block — a waterlogged bottom slab), or a flooded cell a body can walk or drop into from a reachable standable neighbour, within the stack's `follow_range` (else `nav::DEFAULT_FOLLOW_RANGE`) of a seated cell, over an unbounded walk — `DW0496`'s radius and walk. One wet cell silences it, so the rule under-fires by construction. **Binding**: `engagement binding: N staged fight(s) examined, M of a species whose targeting reads the hour, K of those bright while the fight is live, J of those with water for the party's feet within reach; R refused (DW0920).` on every build that seats waves. Build tier (exit 3), run right after `DW0496`. **Prescription**: put the fight's floor under water — a floor of waterlogged bottom slabs is ground the route proofs walk and stands a body in water (measured: a drowned crossed 14 blocks of it to its target in about 20 s) — or stage a species that fights on land, or declare a dark hour or thunder. Never wall the body away from its own water to keep it alive: that is what turned vesperhold's choir toward the wall. |
