# `delvec::compiler::plan::trigger`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/plan/trigger.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW0429 — a sealed gate answers a press (`compiler::pressable`; error; exit 3)

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW0429` | **A sealed gate with no answer** (spec-0117 §6). Every `close-gate` target and every shortcut `gate` owes an **answer trigger**: a `use` trigger with `serves: {gate: <that anchor>}` whose `at` resolves either to a cell **inside** the region that holds a block with an outline while sealed — the gate's own answer, whose body is that block under a click box fitted to it — or to a cell **face-adjacent** to the region holding its own `prop` (a knocker); for a shortcut, the knocker stands on the door's sealed side. A shortcut's own leaf is pressed from both sides: its answer speaks to a presser on the sealed side (`audience: presser` required; the side is a gate term derived from `wrongside`), and the far side hears the shortcut's `answers.not_yet` from the same leaf. Refused, naming the gate and the cell: a gate with no answer trigger (a push on a sealed door is otherwise silent); an answer anchored at a cell of the region the prefab leaves as air (no block to press); a knocker neither inside nor face-adjacent to the region, or on a shortcut's far side; a shortcut's own answer addressed to the party. The compiler never writes the words. Build tier (exit 3), `compiler::pressable::check`, where regions and anchors resolve. Prescription: declare the answer trigger at a cell of the door that holds its block (recommended: the thing the player pushes on is the thing that answers), or, where nothing reads as the door, a knocker — a bell or a button with its `prop` — beside it on the side that owes the line. **Binding.** Every build prints `press proof (DW0429/DW1059/DW1052/DW0896): P pressable(s) placed — O pressed on their own, B boxed acting on their own, U boxed unobservable, S boxed struck; G sealed gate(s) answered (A by their own block, K by a knocker); T answer trigger(s) synthesized; R reconciliation pair(s) in tick` and writes `validation/press-bodies.json`. |
