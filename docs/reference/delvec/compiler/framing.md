# `delvec::compiler::framing`

The reference page for `crates/delvec/src/compiler/framing.rs` and its `framing/shot.rs`: the diagnostics-catalog row of every DW code
this module declares. The catalog's shared rules are in [`compiler.md` §5](../../compiler.md#5-diagnostics-catalog).

## Diagnostics

### DW1033–DW1034 — a shot frames its subject (`compiler::framing`; error; exit 3)

Asked in the world block right after the cutscene clip, served-distance and pan proofs (`DW0308`, `DW0956`, `DW0347`), so a camera inside a wall is named for that first (`emit::routes::prove_world`). Every shot of every cutscene is judged at every driver tick against **the proof frame** — the client the game ships: vertical field of view 70° on an 854 × 480 window, 60° at a tick the lens's cell holds water or lava, entity distance 1.0, `entity-broadcast-range-percentage` 100 — over the poses the client draws: the keyframes `ExpandedShot::frames` emits and the linear tween between them (`ExpandedShot::client_poses`). A body subject is asked first whether it is there to be seen (`DW1034`), then whether the frame shows it (`DW1033`). Every refusal is printed, the first per judged subject; the build stops on the first.

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `DW1033` | **A shot does not show its subject** (spec-0110 §6.1): at some judged tick the shot's `subject` or `subject_b` is **out of frame** — its reference point is behind the near plane or outside the action-safe rectangle, `|x|, |y| ≤ 0.93` in normalised device coordinates (EBU R 95) — or **occluded** — all nine sight lines from the lens to its sight box (the centre and the eight corners inset 0.3) cross a cell `World::blocks_camera` holds for, the subject's own box excluded. The lines are the one sight rule, `framing::in_sight`, which a review frame's manifest (`occluded`, through `framing::resolve_targets`) and `DW0863` ask too. The message names the cutscene, the shot, the subject, the tick, the lens and its aim, which failed and, for occlusion, the centre line's first blocking cell. Remedies: the camera `path`, `look_at`, `bearing`/`dist` on a styled shot, or a hard cut into two shots. Never fires on `"landscape"`. |
| `DW1034` | **A body subject is not there to be seen** (spec-0110 §6.2): an `npc`, `actor` or `assembly` subject that is **not present** at the shot's first tick — no placing ordered before the cutscene on every path, or an unconditional removal ordered between (the one ordering rule, `dsl::ordering::Order`, which `DW0195` and `DW0198` share; a flag- or state-gated effect and a reaction bundle count for nothing; an assembly is never placed at setup), or an unconditional removal that runs **while the shot plays** (`Order::removed_while`): a cutscene does not hold the list it stands in, so a removal later in that list runs at the cutscene's first driver tick, and one in a later step of the same `sequence` runs that step's `at_ticks` after it; the remedy named is the cutscene's own `then`, which runs when the camera returns — **not sent** at some tick — the horizontal distance from the lens to the body exceeds its type's tracking range, `clientTrackingRange × 16` from `crates/delvec/data/entity-bodies-1.21.11.json`, capped by the served `view_distance × 16` — or, for an NPC or actor, **not drawn** — the lens is at or beyond `size × 64` blocks from it, `size` the mean extent of its worn type's box. An assembly never fails the drawn arm: the engine writes its `view_range`. |

**Binding.** Every build with a cutscene prints, before the verdict, `shot subjects: S shot(s) in C cutscene(s), S declare a subject (L landscape, M marks, B bodies, A assemblies, P places, R regions); T tick(s) judged, O out of frame, K occluded, clear lines Q of 9·T; bodies: N present, sent, drawn — farthest F blocks, hidden below entity distance s`, and writes `validation/shot-subjects.json`; a cutscene-less campaign prints and writes neither.
