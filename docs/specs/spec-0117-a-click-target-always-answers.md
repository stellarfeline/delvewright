# spec-0117: A click target always answers — a pressable stands for the whole delve, every press outside its window is answered to the presser, and a repeatable press that serves an objective stands down when the objective settles

- **Status**: Draft
- **Ground**: written against engine `cd9b7eb0f9ce75f3cc57ad423e0b2e596c2dc0ed` (`origin/main`), read only — `Objective::Interact` (`requires_item`, `missing_item_hint`, `prop`, `marker`) in `crates/dsl/src/quest/objective.rs`; `EnvTrigger` (`once`, `audience`, `prop`), `TriggerAudience`, `attributes_its_actor`, `DW0427`, `DW0428`, `DW0429` and `press_answer_checks` in `crates/dsl/src/trigger.rs`; `DW0437` in `crates/dsl/src/quest/check.rs`; `QuestsContent::answers_press_at` in `crates/dsl/src/quest/mod.rs`; `Verb::Narrate`, `NarrateStyle::Actionbar` and `Verb::CloseGate::sealed_hint` in `crates/dsl/src/quest/verb.rs`; `TextKind::Refusal` and `key_kind` in `crates/dsl/src/l10n.rs`; `activation_commands`, `completion_cleanup`, `interact_entity_tag` and `reward_fns` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/emit/objective.rs`; the activation driver, the `interact` tick lines (completion, `missing_item_hint`, the unconditional reset) and `interact_objectives` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/emit/functions.rs`; `trigger_poll_guards`, `click_trigger_poll`, `env_trigger_tick`, `env_trigger_setup`, `env_trigger_fns` and `press_dispatch_fn` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/emit/trigger.rs`; `Body`, `trigger_body`, `trigger_props` and `interact_block` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/pressable.rs`; `collect_press_answers` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/plan/trigger.rs`; `activation_prop` and the activation-prop region events in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/plan/path/region.rs`; `path_triggers` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/plan/path/triggers.rs`; the `interact` and press-answer rows of `docs/reference/compiler.md`; the `verb_interact`, `verb_interact_arming` and `verb_interact_held` templates in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/emit/packtest/objective.rs`; `crates/delvec/data/commands-1.21.11.json` and `sounds-1.21.11.json`. The campaign the finding came from was read at the content repository's `campaign/stranding` head, counted only.
- **What it is for**: a player who presses a thing and is told what happened. In a playtest, a prop that an objective would later open was clicked before its step and said nothing; a puzzle solved by several presses went on accepting presses after it was solved and punished one of them. The general form of both: every click target answers every press — *not yet* before its step, and no change to settled state after. The engine today summons an `interact`'s body when its objective opens and kills it when the objective completes, spends a click that arrives outside the window in silence, has no field for a not-yet or a done answer, and never stands a repeatable trigger down because an objective it feeds has completed. The engine already has the whole mechanism for a press answer — a `use` trigger addressed to its presser, a `narrate` on the action bar, an advancement that observes without consuming, and a refusal for a sealed body left silent — built for sealed gates and doors. This spec extends that one mechanism to the two pressables that still lack it.
- **Research**: `docs/reference/press-answers.md` is the record; its synthesis is §2. Each statement below is marked **cited** (a line of the tree at the ground revision, a reading of pinned data, a published source on that page, or a constitution rule) or **authored** (this spec chooses).
- **Numbers**: spec `0117`. Two diagnostics: `DW1051` (a press that would be silent in a state a player can press it in, §6) and `DW1052` (an answer declared for a state the press never has, §6). `DW0437` is retired into `DW1052` (§6); `DW0427` widens what it judges under its own code (§6). The surface change is stage 5 and takes the unreleased `dsl_version` minor other unreleased surface changes share; this spec states no version literal.
- **Non-goals**: an engine-written default wording or default cue for a refused press (§3, `DW0429`'s rule); an answer addressed to the party (§3); an answer on a left-click (`strike`, `strike-npc`, `strike-assembly` — vanilla cannot name the presser, §6); an objective's state as a term of the one gate (§11); any change to what a press *does* while its window is open; the NPC dialogue hitbox, the bonfire, the shop and the trap disarm (each already answers every press through its dialog or its own reply); a journal or log of refusals (spec-0104 §8 lists what is open; a refusal is the moment, not the record).

## 1. What the tree does today, re-verified

Each claim the finding's record made is re-read at the ground revision; none has changed. One fact the record did not name is added last, because it decides the shape of this spec.

- **Cited** (`emit/functions.rs`, the activation driver; `emit/objective.rs`, `activation_commands`): an `interact`'s `minecraft:interaction` body — or, for a prop vanilla reports the use of, the prop block alone — is placed by `activate_<obj>` the tick the objective's `pending_guard` first holds, once, behind the `#act_<obj>` sentinel. **Cited** (`completion_cleanup`): `complete_<obj>` runs `kill @e[tag=dw_i_<obj>]`. So the body exists exactly while the objective is pending; a prop block placed at activation stays as scenery. **Cited** (`plan/path/region.rs`): the region model registers that prop as a write sealed from load and forced from the completion step, because "activation has no step of its own".
- **Cited** (`emit/functions.rs`, the `interact` tick lines): the completion line runs under `pending_guard` and the hand guard; the `missing_item_hint` line runs under `pending_guard` and the negated hand guard; the reset `scoreboard players reset @s dw.i_<obj>` runs unconditionally. A click that arrives while the objective is not pending is spent and nothing is said. For a hand-pressed prop the `i_<obj>` advancement fires on `default_block_use` at the cell in every state, so a lever the piece authored is flipped before its step and spent in the same silence.
- **Cited** (`quest/objective.rs`, `l10n.rs`): `missing_item_hint` is the one authored answer an `interact` carries, inventoried as `obj.<quest>.<obj>.missing_item_hint`, kind `Refusal`; no field answers a press before the step or after it.
- **Cited** (`emit/trigger.rs`, `trigger_poll_guards`): a trigger's dispatch gate is its `once` sentinel, its `forbids_flags`, its `requires_flags` and its `requires_state`, and nothing else; no term reads an objective. A `once: false` trigger whose writes feed an objective's gate fires after that objective completes exactly as before.
- **Cited** (the campaign, counted): 30 objectives, 16 `interact` (3 with `requires_item`, all 3 with a `missing_item_hint`); 41 triggers, 14 `use`, every one `once: false`, 4 gated by flags, 10 addressed to the presser, 6 of them answers to sealed things. The repeated-press shapes are seven repeatable strike triggers each counting into a datum and standing down on a flag they set themselves, and three repeatable, ungated `use` triggers each setting a datum. The puzzle's own stand-down read a flag set later than the solve; the engine had no way to say *this press belongs to that objective*.
- **Cited — what the record did not say** (`compiler.md`, the press-answer rows; `trigger.rs`, `DW0429`; `plan/trigger.rs`, `collect_press_answers`): a press answer is already "not a mechanism — it is an ordinary trigger": `EnvTrigger { on: use, audience: presser, once: false, effects: [narrate { style: actionbar, text }] }`, authored, or synthesized by the compiler from a `close-gate`'s `sealed_hint`; dispatched by a `player_interacted_with_entity` advancement whose reward revokes itself so the object answers every press; gated through `trigger_poll_guards`; emitted, inventoried, proven and claimed (`DW0811`) by the same code every authored click uses. A sealed body the campaign leaves silent is refused (`DW0429`) and the compiler bakes no default, "one rule for the whole pressable class". `NarrateStyle::Actionbar` is documented as "the channel a *reply* uses".

## 2. What practice does

**Cited** (`press-answers.md` §1–§3): feedback within a reasonable time is how a user learns the outcome of a prior interaction and decides the next step (Nielsen Norman #1); an error says in plain language what is wrong and what to do (#9; `game-writing.md` F2); essential information is conveyed neither by sound alone nor by text alone (Game Accessibility Guidelines); the pinned game's own refused use — a locked container pressed without its key — plays a sound and writes one line on the action bar, and `minecraft:block.chest.locked` is that sound in the pinned registry.

**Authored — the synthesis.** A refused press is answered on the action bar, to the presser, on every press, with a line the author wrote and a sound the engine plays; the engine never writes the words; a pressable that would be silent in a state a player can press it in is refused at build, as a sealed body is.

## 3. The decision: one pressable class, one press-answer mechanism, three states

**Authored — the object class.** The thing a player right-clicks is a **pressable** — `compiler::pressable` is already the one authority for its body (`Body::Block`, `Point`, `Region`, `Rides`), for an `interact` objective and for a `use` or `strike` trigger alike. A pressable is in one of three states at any tick:

| State | An `interact` | A trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **open** — the press acts | its `pending_guard` holds (quest active, every `after` complete, its gate, not complete) | its own gate holds and, if `once`, it has not fired; with `serves` (§4), also the served objective is pending |
| **settled** — the act is done | `dw.o_<obj>` is 1, or its quest is complete | `once` and fired; or `serves` and the served objective is settled |
| **shut** — not open and not settled | any other tick | any other tick |

An `interact` with `requires_item` has a fourth answer inside **open**: the hand lacks the item (today's `missing_item_hint`).

**Authored — the answer is the existing press-answer trigger, synthesized from a declared wording.** Exactly as `sealed_hint` is lowered by `collect_press_answers` into a presser `use` trigger riding the seal's body, each answer declared here is lowered into a presser `use` trigger riding the pressable's own body, gated to the one state it answers, carrying `narrate { style: actionbar, text }` under `Audience::Solo`. Nothing is added to the emitter but the gate derivation; the dispatch, the l10n path, the `env_trigger_<id>` PackTest, the `DW0811` claim and the `dw-` id namespace (`DW0428`) are the ones the seal answer already has. Two consequences follow and are taken: the body of an `interact` must exist in every state a player can press it in, so it stands from `setup_finish` to the end of the delve as every trigger body does (§5.1); and the states a press can be in are what decide which answers are owed (§6).

**Authored — no baked default, so silence is refused.** `DW0429`'s reasoning is adopted whole: a default wording or a default-only cue would be the compiler making a design statement about this door on the author's behalf. A pressable that would be silent in a state a player can press it in is `DW1051`, the sibling of `DW0429` over the two pressables it did not cover. The engine's half — the body, the dispatch, the state, the sound — is supplied; the words are the campaign's. This is a tightening against the engine before this spec, in those words: an `interact` that answers nothing before or after its step, which nothing refused, is refused.

**Rejected, authored.**

- *A default line from compiler chrome, hidden by a switch.* The quest chrome (`New objective:`) names a thing the engine owns; the words a shut door says are the campaign's tone, which `DW0429` already refuses to invent.
- *An answer that is an effect bundle.* It would let a not-yet answer write a flag or a done answer move the world — the very writes the finding forbids after a step settles — and would need a refusal for writing verbs that the string form makes unnecessary. A reply is one `Refusal` line; a beat is a trigger the author writes.
- *A party-addressed answer.* A reply to a press tells the one who pressed (`trigger.rs`: "broadcasting it tells four players about a door three of them are nowhere near").
- *Refusing a `once: false` trigger that feeds an objective and is not stood down.* A bell that can be rung forever and tallies its rings for an ending is a legitimate design; which of two readers of a datum is "the" objective a press serves is a candidate, not a match (CLAUDE.md, a resolve-by-name over a non-unique scope). The author states it (§4 `serves`), and the skill says when to.
- *Objective state as a term of the one gate* (`requires_flags` / `forbids_flags` / `requires_state` gaining an objective term). The general form, and the right home in the long run; it touches every gate consumer and every flow proof, and this finding needs one binding, not a fourth gate term. Recorded open (§11).

## 4. The surface

Stage 5.

```json
{
  "quests": [{
    "objectives": [
      { "type": "interact", "id": "obj/the-slate", "anchor": "anchor/slate",
        "prop": { "block": "minecraft:lectern[facing=south]" },
        "title": "Read the slate",
        "answers": { "not_yet": "The slate is blank until the keeper has spoken.",
                     "done": "You have read what the slate says." } },
      { "type": "interact", "id": "obj/the-door", "anchor": "anchor/door",
        "requires_item": "minecraft:tripwire_hook", "after": ["obj/the-slate"],
        "answers": { "not_yet": "The door is barred from within.",
                     "missing_item": "Locked. The keeper's key opens it.",
                     "done": "The door stands open." } }
    ]
  }],
  "triggers": [
    { "id": "trigger/the-left-lever", "at": "anchor/lever-left", "on": { "on": "use" },
      "prop": { "block": "minecraft:lever[face=wall,facing=south]" },
      "once": false, "serves": "obj/the-bolt",
      "effects": [{ "type": "add-state", "state": "state/levers", "amount": 1 }],
      "answers": { "not_yet": "The lever is stiff with rust.",
                   "done": "The bolt is already drawn." } }
  ]
}
```

**`answers`** is an object carried by every pressable the engine can name a presser for — the `interact` objective and the `use` trigger — holding one `Refusal` line per state the pressable has: `not_yet` (shut), `done` (settled) and, on an `interact` with `requires_item`, `missing_item` (open, hand empty). Each line is optional in the schema and owed by `DW1051` where the state exists (§6). Keys in the l10n inventory: `obj.<quest>.<obj>.answers.not_yet` / `.missing_item` / `.done` and `trigger.<id>.answers.not_yet` / `.done`, kind `Refusal`, each with its `situation` (the objective's or trigger's, as the title's rows already carry). **`missing_item_hint` is removed**: its one meaning is `answers.missing_item`, and a second field for the same answer class is the defect CLAUDE.md names. A document carrying `missing_item_hint` is refused as an unknown field (`deny_unknown_fields`); the content is changed, with no shim.

**`serves`** is an optional `ObjectiveId` on every `triggers[]` entry, naming the objective this press is part of. It binds the trigger's window to the objective's: the trigger is open only while its own gate holds **and** the objective is pending; it is settled when the objective is; it is shut otherwise. The reference resolves like `after` does, to an objective of any quest; an unknown id is the existing unknown-objective refusal. `serves` is admitted on every trigger kind, because standing down at a step's settlement is a property of the trigger, not of how it is pressed; the **answers** of a `serves` trigger are admitted only where the presser can be named (§6).

**What is not changed.** `once`, `audience`, the three gate terms and `prop` keep their meaning; a presser `use` trigger the author writes as an answer to a sealed body is unchanged, and `answers_press_at` still discharges `DW0429`.

## 5. Emission

### 5.1 The body stands for the whole delve

**Authored.** An `interact`'s body — the `minecraft:interaction` column, or the hand-pressed prop block that is its own detector — is placed at `setup_finish`, beside the trigger bodies, and is never killed; `activation_commands` places the `collect` chest and nothing for an `interact`; `completion_cleanup` kills nothing for an `interact`. The `#act_<obj>` sentinel and `activate_<obj>` remain for the `collect` chest. **The region model** (`plan/path/region.rs`) registers the interact prop as a write forced from step 0, which is what it now is; the unforced-from-load arm is kept for the `collect` chest alone. **One cell, one hitbox**, the rule `env_trigger_setup` and the NPC hitbox already keep: two pressables whose bodies resolve to one cell share one body wearing both tags, resolved in `compiler::pressable` and nowhere else, so two `interact` objectives on one lever in sequence build, and `DW0896` (two bodies whose lifetimes overlap on one cell) reads the shared body as one. **Cited** (`compiler.md`, `interact` row): the kill at completion existed "so a finished objective is not clickable"; under this spec a finished objective is clickable and answers `done`, which is the point.

**The cost, stated.** Every campaign's bytes move (`setup_finish` gains the summons, `complete_<obj>` loses the kill, the region events change); `gallery/baseline/` is regenerated once; the `obj_activate_<obj>` and `v04_interact_cleanup` PackTests change what they assert (the body is present before activation and after completion). No byte-identity claim is made for any existing document.

### 5.2 The answers, lowered

**Authored.** For each declared answer the compiler synthesizes one presser `use` trigger, `trigger/dw-answer-<owner>-<state>` (`<owner>` the objective's or trigger's safe id; `<state>` one of `not-yet`, `missing-item`, `done`), riding the owner's body: `Body::Rides` for a hitbox (the body gains the answer's `dw_trig_…` tag at summon), or the block's `default_block_use` criterion (`block_use_criterion`) for a hand-pressed prop, through `press_dispatch_fn`, exactly as a seal's answer is lowered today. The answer's effects are `narrate { style: actionbar, text }` under `Audience::Solo`, followed by `scoreboard players set #ans_<owner> dw.sys <n>` (1 not-yet, 2 missing-item, 3 done) — the trace write every trigger already makes on `#trig_<id>` (`emit/trigger.rs`: "the write is what makes trigger dispatch observable at all"), which is what a PackTest reads, since an action-bar line leaves no game state. **Every answer plays the engine's refusal cue** — `CUE_REFUSED`, `minecraft:block.chest.locked`, the sound the pinned game pairs with its own refused use — through `cue_line`, beside `CUE_ANNOUNCE`, `CUE_COMPLETE` and `CUE_CAMPAIGN`, and `validation/cues.json` states it for the bot. **The gates** are derived, never typed: the interact's `not_yet` carries the negation of its `pending_guard` and the negation of its settled term; `done` carries the settled term; `missing_item` carries the `pending_guard` and the negated hand guard, replacing the tick line that carried the same today (one click, one line, as before). A trigger's `not_yet` carries the negation of `trigger_poll_guards`' flag, state and `serves` terms with the once term not fired; `done` carries the once-fired term or the served objective's settled term. **One authority**: `pending_guard` and `trigger_poll_guards` are read, not restated; the implementation lands an agreement test that drives an objective through its states and asserts, per state, that exactly one of {completion, `not_yet`, `missing_item`, `done`} can dispatch.

### 5.3 `serves`

**Authored.** `trigger_poll_guards` gains, for a trigger with `serves`, the served objective's `pending_guard` terms as further space-terminated fragments, so every dispatch route — the tick poll, `press_<id>`, `step_<id>`, the assembly's strike clause — gates identically. `path_triggers`' `enabled` and `Flow`'s link liveness read the same fact: a `serves` trigger is performable only while its objective is pending on that walk; the agreement test of §5.2 covers it. After the objective settles the trigger never dispatches again, whatever `once` says, so no write it would have made reaches settled state.

### 5.4 Nothing else moves

The bot's `/trigger dw.i_<obj>` path, the completion marker channel, the announcement chrome, the journal and the compass (spec-0104), `takes_item` (spec-0114), the harness's `interact` and `trigger` steps: unchanged. A bot never presses outside a window, so it never reads an answer; the PackTests do.

## 6. Diagnostics

**`DW1051` — a press that would be silent** (`dsl::trigger`, beside `DW0429`, in `press_answer_checks`; error; validation tier, exit 1). Quantifier: every `interact` objective and every `use` trigger of the primary document. Owed and missing: `not_yet` on every `interact`; `done` on every `interact`; `missing_item` on an `interact` with `requires_item`; `not_yet` on a `use` trigger with any gate term or a `serves`; `done` on a `use` trigger with `once: true` or a `serves`. Path: the pressable's `answers`. Message: the pressable, the state, what a player would meet there (a press that does nothing and says nothing), and the remedy — write the line, in the campaign's own words; the answer's l10n key. Binding line: `press answers (DW1051): P pressable(s) — A answer(s) owed, D declared, R refused; S sealed bodies judged by DW0429`. A `use` trigger with no gate, no `once` and no `serves` has only the open state and owes nothing; it is counted.

**`DW1052` — an answer for a state the press never has** (same module; error; validation tier). `missing_item` without `requires_item` (today's `DW0437`, retired into this code); `not_yet` on a `use` trigger with no gate term and no `serves`; `done` on a `use` trigger with `once: false` and no `serves`. An inert declaration is refused, never ignored (spec-0093 §7's rule). Remedy: drop the line, or give the trigger the gate, `once` or `serves` that creates the state.

**`DW0427`, widened under its own code.** `answers` on a trigger whose press vanilla cannot attribute — `strike`, `strike-npc`, `strike-assembly`, `approach`, `step` — is refused with the message `audience: presser` already gets: a reply needs a presser, and a left-click has none. `serves` on those kinds is admitted (it is a gate, not a reply); a `serves` `strike-assembly` stands down and says nothing, which is recorded as the gap it is (§11).

**`DW0437`** is retired: declared nowhere, listed nowhere; `check-dw-codes.py` holds it bidirectionally. Its test moves to `DW1052`.

The probe for `DW0437` is renamed to the `DW1052` probe above or deleted, whichever the coverage gate already holds.

## 8. Demo level

The row for `docs/demo-levels.md`: **Three Levers and a Bell** — one room off a jetty: a bell on a post that an objective opens only after the keeper has spoken (`not_yet`: the rope is tied up; `done`: the bell has been rung), a door with `requires_item` (`missing_item`: locked, the keeper's key; `done`: it stands open), and three levers whose presses count into a datum the bolt objective reads, each `once: false` with `serves` and both answers, so that pressing a fourth time after the bolt draws says *the bolt is already drawn* and moves nothing. A player presses every target in every state and reads one action-bar line and hears one cue each time; a second player standing beside them reads nothing. The look is what no machine decides: whether the line reads at the crosshair before it fades, whether the cue reads as a refusal and not a click, and whether the lever's own flip beside the line confuses or confirms.

**Reused surfaces, by name**: `compiler::pressable` (the body), `collect_press_answers` and `press_dispatch_fn` (the dispatch), `pending_guard` and `trigger_poll_guards` (the gates), `NarrateStyle::Actionbar` (the channel), `TextKind::Refusal` and `key_kind` (the words), `cue_line` and `validation/cues.json` (the sound), `DW0427`, `DW0428`, `DW0429`, `DW0811` (the rules). Nothing in this spec is a second authority for any of them.

**Ordering.** The implementation bases on `main` once both branches have landed there; it shares `emit/objective.rs`, `emit/trigger.rs` and `emit/functions.rs` with the click-box protrusion work and follows it.

## 11. What stays open

- **Objective state as a gate term.** `serves` is one binding of a general fact; the one gate reading an objective's completion (as `after` does, for objectives only) would let any gate consumer say it, and would make `serves` a special case. Proposed, not done.
- **A `strike` that stands down and says nothing.** A `serves` `strike-assembly` is disarmed at settlement and answers no one, because vanilla names no left-clicker (`DW0427`). The assembly's own clip is the author's answer there.
- **A party-visible refusal.** A lever's flip is seen by everyone; its action-bar line by one. Whether a shut lever should also fail to flip is a question for the demo level.
- **Vanilla's `lock` component** as a body for a locked container (the pinned game's own refused use, with its own line and sound) is a different carrier for the same class and is not taken here.
