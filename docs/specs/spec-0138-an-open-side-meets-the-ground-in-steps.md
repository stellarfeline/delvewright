# spec-0138: An open side meets the ground in steps — where a place stands higher or lower than the land beside a side it walls off with nothing, the whole's terrain is stepped up or down to it at the place's declared slope, with a seeded noise, and a side declared `sheer` keeps its drop

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Ground**: written against `feature/festival-day` at `af68169c4` (`delvec 1.16.0`, `dsl 0.75.0`), read for: `Sides`, `SideKind`, `Sides::is_open` and `is_declared_open` in `crates/dsl/src/siteplan/mod.rs`; `PlacedBox::open_sides`, `declared_open_sides` and `side_open` in `siteplan/place.rs`; `Site::new`, `is_open_ring_column`, `fixed_top`, `ground_height`, `terrain_height`, `is_fixed`, `owner_among`, `fixed_cells` and `aloft_in_earth` in `siteplan/claim.rs`; `Ground::of`, `top`, `column_kind`, `column_blocks`, `fill_block`, `ground_block` and `trees` in `siteplan/ground.rs`; `lay_fill` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/blockout.rs`; `open_side_ways` in `compiler/nav/width.rs`; the `DW0990`, `DW1057`, `DW1065` and `DW0800` rows of the diagnostics catalogue; `gallery/overlays/site-plan/site-plan.json`.
- **What it is for**: a player who walks to the edge of a meadow and finds it ends in a two-block wall of dirt, or looks across a beach at a terrace standing on a straight cliff. A place whose side stands no wall is meant to read as part of the land, and where its floor is not at the height of the ground beside it the edge is a sheer face the creator never drew. This spec makes the land meet such a side in steps by default, and gives the creator one word to keep the face where a pit or a terrace is what they meant.
- **Research**: `docs/reference/terrain-grade.md`, sources S1–S7. Every practice below is **[cited]** from it or from the code named in *Ground*; every rule is **[authored]** here.
- **Numbers**: spec `0138`; `dsl_version` `0.76.0`; `DW1108`, used (§7). `DW1109` was handed and is not used: §10 names the rule that would carry it.
- **Non-goals**: smoothing the terrain anywhere else (the heightmap is the creator's); a slope made of slabs or stairs; a ramp a body walks without jumping; any change to a cell a place owns, to a piece, to a seam, or to the ground between two places that meet (§10); judging whether a body can get up or down the bank (the reach graph's, as for all ground).
- **Rulings taken** (amendments made while the spec was built, in its own words): the slope is the creator's, declared per place as a rise per run (§3, §4); a bank carries a seeded noise so it does not read as a regular cone (§4); two places that meet at different heights with no whole column between them get no grade and no refusal — the places draw their join (§6, §7, §10, settled); a cut beside a declared fluid is refused and the creator declares the side `sheer` (§7, settled).

## 1. What stands there now **[cited]**

A ground place's claim is its footprint grown by a one-cell ring. On a side that stands no wall — `sides.<face>` absent or `"open"` — the ring column holds the place's own ground at the floor course and nothing over it, and its fixed ground stops under the floor course (`Site::fixed_top`: `min(ground height, floor − 2)`). The next column out is nobody's: `lay_fill` lays the site's terrain there at `Ground::top(x, z)`, whatever the place's floor is.

So with the place's surface `P = floor − 1` (the `y` of its floor course) and the terrain's surface `t` in the column beyond the ring, the edge is a face `|P − t|` blocks high, standing at the ring's outer edge. No rule reads it. `DW0990`'s crack shape judges the plot's edge inside the ring, and passes a face that is solid down to the lower surface. `DW1065` refuses it only on a side declared `open` in words, as *built shut*, with the remedy *give the side ground beyond it* — which nothing in the plan can do.

## 2. Where the grade lives **[authored, on cited practice]**

**In the whole's terrain, beyond the ring. The place does not move and is not written.**

**[cited]** (`terrain-grade.md` §2): the pinned game adapts the ground to a rigid piece and never the piece to the ground — `Beardifier` is a term in the terrain's own density, taken over the columns around a piece's box; (§3) a map tool edits terrain as one surface height per column.

**[authored]** The grade is a bound on `Ground::top`. Every reader of the terrain already asks that one function — the claim bottoms, the fill, the fixed ground's blocks, the trees, the handout, the daylight survey — so the graded ground is the ground, for all of them, by construction. `Ground::of` is given the placed boxes (which the packing derives without reading the terrain) and resolves the graded tops once; the ungraded height is private to the module that grades (`siteplan::grade`) and has no other reader.

**Rejected: in the place's box.** The box's cells are the piece's, and a bound place's owned cells are a hole in what the whole writes: the engine cannot grade there, and a piece that graded its own edge would need the box enlarged by the bank, which makes every place's size depend on the terrain around it. **Rejected: in the ring.** The ring is one cell and holds the place's paving on an open side; one cell lands a drop of one, which is already no cliff. **Rejected: lowering or raising the place.** The floor is the plan's declaration (`floor`), and the seams' rises are derived from it.

## 3. The surface **[authored]**

```json
{ "node": "node/quarry", "extent": [12, 9], "floor": {"y": 58}, "ceiling": {"open": 8},
  "sides": { "north": "wall", "east": "sheer", "south": "sheer" } }
{ "node": "node/castle", "extent": [40, 40], "floor": {"y": 120}, "ceiling": {"open": 30},
  "grade": { "rise": 5, "run": 1 } }
```

`boxes[].sides.<face>` takes a third value, `"sheer"`: **the place stands no wall on this side, and the ground beside it is left as the terrain has it.** It is the opt-out, per side. The default is unchanged in spelling and gains the grade: a side absent or `"open"` is graded.

`boxes[].grade` is **the slope of the place's bank**: `{"rise": r, "run": n}`, `r` blocks of height per `n` columns across, `r` in `1..=5` and `n` in `1..=4`, both whole. Absent, it is `{"rise": 1, "run": 2}`. A value outside its range, a missing key or any other key is `DW0100`, refused where the plan is read. The bounds are **[authored]**: the steepest bank, five blocks a column, is a mountain under a castle (thirty blocks in six columns); the gentlest, one block in four columns, is a lawn; between them every ratio a creator writes as two small integers. The slope acts on every graded side of the place; a side that should not be banked is `sheer`.

**The place owns the slope; the side owns whether there is a bank.** The slope is the character of the ground a place stands in — the mountain a castle crowns, the lawn a manor sits on — and is one answer for the whole place: a castle on a crag is not gentle on one side and sheer-steep on the next, and where it is, the side that drops is `sheer` and the cliff is drawn. **Rejected: a slope per side** (`sides.east: {"rise": 5, "run": 1}`). It makes the side's value two answers — whether a wall stands and how steep the land is — and leaves `open` (a way) and a slope unable to be written together.

| `sides.<face>` | a wall stands | judged as a way (`DW1057`, `DW1065`) | the ground beyond is graded |
|---|---|---|---|
| absent | no | no | **yes** |
| `"open"` | no | yes | **yes** |
| `"sheer"` | no | no | no |
| `"wall"` | yes | — | no |

A `sheer` side is in every other respect an undeclared one: `Sides::is_open` holds for it, the leak proof and `DW0836` read it as a declared opening, and a face two places share with neither walling it is a seamless border whichever of the three unwalled values each wrote.

**The side owns it.** The grade acts on one side of one place, and the side already has exactly one declaration saying how the place's edge meets what is beside it. A fourth answer to that question is a fourth value of that field. **Rejected: `grade: false` on the box.** A quay is sheer at the water and graded at its back; the box is the wrong grain. **Rejected: a second per-side map (`boxes[].grade`).** Two fields for one side, which can disagree (`grade` on a `wall`). **Rejected: `sheer` beside `open` on one side.** A side judged as a way owes ground at its walk plane in the column beyond the ring (`DW1065`), which is the grade's first column (§4); a sheer edge with that ground is not sheer, and without it is refused. The two differ only where they contradict.


## 4. The shape **[authored, on cited practice]**

**Which sides.** A **graded side** is a side of a ground place (`base: "ground"`), on a site whose `fill` is `open` with a terrain that resolved, declared neither `wall` nor `sheer`.

**Edge columns.** A place's edge columns are its ring columns that stand on graded sides only (a corner column, on both of its sides — `PlacedBox::is_open_ring_column`, read over graded sides) and that no other ground place's shell footprint covers. A ring column two places share is the seam's and is never an edge column.

**Whole columns.** A region column in no ground place's shell footprint. These are the only columns the grade changes.

**The step count.** For a place `i` with surface `Pᵢ`, slope `rᵢ` per `nᵢ`, and a column `c`, let `q` be the least squared distance `Δx² + Δz²` from `c` to an edge column of `i`. Then `kᵢ(c)` is the least integer `k ≥ 0` with `q ≤ (1 + nᵢ·k)²`: how many runs out the column stands. Its **allowance** is `aᵢ(c) = rᵢ · kᵢ(c)`. Integers only; the square root is an integer one (`⌈√q⌉`), and `k = ⌈(⌈√q⌉ − 1) / n⌉`.

**The bank.** Place `i`'s **bank** is the set of whole columns joined to its edge, 4-adjacent, through columns its own bound moves: a whole column 4-adjacent to an edge column of `i`, or to a column of `i`'s bank, is in the bank when `t(c)` lies outside `[Pᵢ − aᵢ(c), Pᵢ + aᵢ(c)]`. A bank ends at the first column the terrain already meets and never reaches past it: a place at the foot of a mountain does not carve the mountain beyond the ground that meets it, and two graded places on the same level ground do not bound each other at any distance. **[authored]** — amended while built: the bound written over every column of the region carved every slope steeper than the default within sight of a gentle place (on the town's island, 8,004 columns lowered by places nowhere near them), and refused two places on flat ground twenty-five columns apart.

**The bound.** `lo(c) = max over i of (Pᵢ − aᵢ(c))`, `hi(c) = min over i of (Pᵢ + aᵢ(c))`, over the places whose bank holds `c`. In a whole column the natural surface `t(c)` settles to

- `min(lo + ν(c), hi)` where `t < lo` (raised),
- `max(hi − ν(c), lo)` where `t > hi` (lowered),
- `t` where `lo ≤ t ≤ hi`, and `lo` where `lo > hi` (refused, §7).

`ν(c)` is the bank's noise, below. In a column no bank holds, and everywhere else, `g(c) = t(c)`.

What that draws, along a straight side with a drop of `H` on flat ground, at the default slope (`r = 1`, `n = 2`), before the noise:

| columns beyond the ring | 1 | 2–3 | 4–5 | … | `2H − 2`, `2H − 1` | `2H` on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| allowed difference from `P` | 0 | 1 | 2 | … | `H − 1` | `H` or more |
| surface | `P` | `P ∓ 1` | `P ∓ 2` | … | `P ∓ (H − 1)` | the terrain's own |

At `5` per `1` the allowance is `0, 5, 10, …` and a drop of 30 is met in six columns.

- **A shoulder, then treads `n` deep, each `r` high.** The first column beyond the ring stands at the place's own height whatever the slope (`k = 0` there, so `lo = hi = P` for that place alone): the place's edge is not also the top of a step, and a side declared `open` has the ground `DW1065` asks for. **[authored]**: no source read gives a run per block (`terrain-grade.md` §6). The default, one block per two columns, is the least run on which a body that has jumped one block (**[cited]** `body-movement.md` §4: a full block is jumped, never stepped) stands on a tread before the next rise; a steeper slope is a bank the creator declares is not walked.
- **The noise.** **[cited]** (`terrain-grade.md` §7): the pinned game's own terrain is a shape plus noise — `overworld/sloped_cheese` adds `jaggedness × jagged` noise to the shaped depth, the jaggedness itself a spline of the terrain's parameters, and `base_3d_noise` over it — so a natural slope is never a regular surface, and its roughness grows with the shape it roughens. **[authored]**: `ν(c)` is value noise over a three-column lattice (`Ground::bank_noise`), drawn from the world's `seed` and the column's coordinates alone — no state, no iteration order — in `0..=r`, `r` the rise of the bank that set that side of the bound (the gentlest where two agree). The amplitude follows from the slope and is not a field: one step of the declared rise, carried only **back toward the places that bound the column**, never past the bound's other side. So the noise never stands a column outside `[lo, hi]` — the declared slope is the bank's envelope —, the shoulder (`lo = hi = P`) never moves, and `DW1108`, which is judged on `lo` and `hi` before the noise, is the same with and without it. A column the bound did not move is not touched by the noise.
- **Both ways, by one rule.** A place above the ground has the ground raised to it (`lo`); a place below has it cut down to it (`hi`). **[cited]** (`terrain-grade.md` §2): the game's beard adds ground under a piece's plane and removes it over, from one function. Where the terrain crosses the place's height along one side, each column takes the branch it needs.
- **The least change.** A column whose natural height is inside the bound is not touched, so ground that already meets the side is byte for byte what it was, and the grade ends where the terrain comes within reach — at the default slope after `2H − 1` columns for a drop of `H`: the shoulder alone for a drop of 1, nothing for a drop of 0. Where the bound meets the terrain, the noise lifts some toe columns and not others, so the bank's edge is not a straight line.
- **A corner is rounded.** **[cited]** (`terrain-grade.md` §2): the game measures from the box by straight-line distance, both axes counting only past a corner. Here `q` is that distance squared, so two graded sides meeting at a corner share a quarter-round bank, and no two 4-adjacent columns' bounds differ by more than the rise.
- **At the end of a graded side that meets a `wall` or `sheer` side**, the corner column is not an edge column; the bank turns the end of the side by the same distance and runs out along the neighbouring side's foot. It writes no cell of that side's ring (§5).
- **The blocks are the terrain's.** **[cited]** (`terrain-grade.md` §3): moved ground is re-skinned from its new surface. A regraded column is a terrain column at its new height: `Ground::column_blocks` gives it the `surface` and `below` of the first stratum it meets at that height, steepness and patch read over the graded surface, exactly as an untouched column gets its own. A bank between a sand band and a grass band is sand where it is low and grass where it is high. Every graded column is solid from the region's bottom to its surface, so a `surface` that falls (sand, gravel) stands as it does anywhere on the terrain.
- **Trees.** `Ground::trees` reads the graded surface, and already keeps every trunk four cells clear of every ring; a tree further out stands on the bank as on any slope.

## 5. What it never writes **[authored]**

- **A cell of any ground place's shell footprint** — play space, ring, the ground under either. Ownership (`Site::owner`), every frame, every void, every handed ground column and every fixed ground cell are what they are without the grade. No piece is invalidated.
- **Beside a `wall` side, for that side.** A wall side has no edge columns; it is the source of no grade.
- **Beside a `sheer` side, for that side.** The same.
- **For an aloft place.** It stands on no ground and has no graded side, whatever its `sides` say. The terrain under it may be regraded for a ground place nearby; `DW0990`'s third shape (*an aloft place stands in the earth*) reads the graded surface like any terrain, and where the column it names was raised by a grade its remedies name the side and `sheer`.
- **On a `solid` fill.** There is no terrain surface; every place is carved from rock.
- **Outside the region.** The terrain ends there and so does the grade.

## 6. How it meets what else is there **[authored]**

**Two places that meet.** Two places **meet** where their shells overlap or touch, diagonally included, so no whole column stands between them: two places a seam joins (they share a ring column), and two places packed side by side. The grade does nothing between them, and **no shape of `DW1108` is raised between two places that meet**: the step between their floors is the seam's (`DW0830`, `DW0831`) or the two pieces', drawn by the places, as now. Each place's bank still runs out on its own sides; where the two banks reach one whole column past the end of the join and cannot share it, the column stands at the higher bound. **Settled**: a place pair at different heights with no open ground between them gets no automatic grade; the places draw their join.

**A way across the side.** A side declared `open` is judged as a way: its ring run, and one approach cell in the column beyond. The shoulder is that column's ground. So a declared-open side over a drop, refused today as built shut, stands open. This pair is tested together (criterion 9): the grade supplies the remedy `DW1065` names, and `DW1065` still refuses when a declared volume takes the shoulder away.

**A declared volume.** `volumes[]` override the fill in their regions, the graded fill as any. A `ground`, `massif` or `clearance` the creator declared inside a bank stands as declared, and the count of regraded columns a volume covers is printed (§8). The grade does not refuse a volume for being in its way.

**Water.** The grade lays land and never a fluid. A bank raised into a declared volume of water ends at the water as a shore does. A **cut** is different: **[cited]** (`terrain-grade.md` §4, §5) removing ground beside standing water opens a cell a source runs into, and "unsafe changes near to water" is a named failure of generated settlements. A cut that would remove a cell beside or below a cell of a declared fluid volume is refused (`DW1108`, §7), and the creator declares that side `sheer` (**settled**). A cell a solid volume fills is not removed, and a cell inside the fluid volume is the volume's. Fluid inside a place is that place's to contain; the build's existing fluid and seepage proofs (`DW0318`, `DW0851`) judge the graded world as built and are not relaxed.

**Another place's foot.** The ground beside every ring column of a ground place `j` — its walls, its sheer sides and its graded edge alike — is `j`'s setting, and the bank of a place `i` that does not meet `j` may not bury it, undercut it, or move `j`'s shoulder. Formally: in a whole column 4-adjacent to a ring column of `j` that no other ground place's shell covers, the surface the banks of `j` and of every place not meeting `j` hold it to (before the noise) must equal what `j`'s own bank alone holds it to (the natural surface, where `j`'s bank does not reach it). Otherwise the grade has no room (`DW1108`). A place's bank against its own wall is its own business and passes.

**Contract spaces and reach.** A piece's contract, its spaces, its parts and its open-side cells are inside the frame, which the grade does not enter. `nodes[].reached` does not enter the rule: a place built to be seen is graded like one that is walked. The whole-world proofs — the reach graph, the leak proof (which already reads every unwalled side as a declared opening), the relight survey, the boundary — read the graded world because it is the world. The allocation's `sides` rows gain two derived facts for the place's author, `meets` (`"graded"` or `"sheer"`) and `drop` (the least and greatest natural surface minus `P` over the columns one beyond the ring), so a piece is drawn knowing whether its edge ends in a bank or a face.

## 7. What is refused **[authored]**

**`DW1108` — a grade has no room to land.** Raised at validation, from the plan and the terrain alone, error tier. Two shapes of one claim, that the bound of §4 can be met inside what the whole may write:

1. **Two settings meet.** For two places that do not meet (§6): a whole column where their banks give `lo(c) > hi(c)`; or a column at the foot of one that the other's bank would change (§6). The message names both places and sides, the first column, the two heights, the whole columns between the two shells and the columns the greatest difference over the pair's columns needs at the bank's slope (`n · ⌈difference / r⌉`). Remedies, each reachable: move the places apart by the stated number of columns; declare one side `sheer`; join them with a place that carries the rise (a stair, a ramp).
2. **A cut beside water.** A regraded column lowered from `t` to `g` where a cell it removes (`y` in `g + 1 ..= t`) is beside or below a cell of a declared volume whose block is a fluid. The message names the side, the column, the volume and the cell. Remedies: declare the side `sheer`; move the volume or the place.

One refusal per pair of places, and one per side and volume, each with its count of columns and the first six.

A grade that lands is never refused for its size, for the height it climbs, or for what a body can do on it.

## 8. What is printed **[authored]**

Every `delvec validate` and `delvec build` of a campaign with a site plan prints, zeroes included:

`grade: U unwalled side(s) of G ground place(s) — S graded, H sheer; E edge column(s); C column(s) regraded of W whole column(s) (R raised, L lowered); greatest drop met D; V regraded column(s) under a declared volume`

On a `solid` fill: `grade: a solid fill has no terrain; 0 of U unwalled side(s) graded`; on a terrain that did not resolve (a plan already refused): `grade: the terrain did not resolve; 0 of U unwalled side(s) graded`. `U`, `S` and `H` count the sides of ground places only, and `U = S + H` on an open fill. `D` is the greatest `|drop|` over the graded sides' rows. A build writes `validation/grade.json`: those counts; `terrain_sha256`, over the rows `x z natural graded` of every regraded column in `x`-then-`z` order; and one row per unwalled side of a ground place in plan order — `place`, `face`, `meets`, `surface`, `edge_columns`, `raised`, `lowered`, `drop` (least and greatest), `reach` (the furthest regraded column's step count). A regraded column is attributed to the place whose bound set its height, the first in plan order where two agree, and to the side of that place's nearest edge column (a corner edge column counted on its first graded side in face order).

## 10. What it leaves out, by name **[authored]**

**Two places that meet at different heights.** There is no ground between them to grade; the face is two floors the plan declared, joined or not by a seam. A cliff there is drawn by the two pieces or carried by a third place. **Settled**: such a pair gets no automatic grade and no refusal; the places draw their join (§6).

**A grade that stops.** No limit on the height is set. A place thirty blocks over a valley at the default slope is graded by a bank fifty-nine columns wide unless its side is `sheer` or its `grade` is steeper; the `grade:` line states the greatest drop met so it is seen. The game's own reach is fixed at 12 cells (`terrain-grade.md` §2). **Settled**: the slope is the creator's (`boxes[].grade`, §3); a cap on the bank's width is not a field, and the first map that needs one is the falsifier.

**A `sheer` or a `grade` that changes nothing.** `sheer` on an aloft place, on a `solid` site, or on a side with no drop beyond it draws exactly what an absent key draws; `grade` on a place with no graded side likewise. A `sheer` on a ground place is counted in `H` (an aloft place's sides are not counted), and neither is refused; `DW1109` would carry a rule that it bind to a drop, and is not used, because a creator may declare an edge before the terrain under it is drawn.

**A sky-open place deeper than its declared headroom.** Terrain standing over a place's ring above its claim is what the fill lays today and is outside this spec.

**Slabs and stairs on the bank.** The terrain is full blocks; a walked ramp is a place.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The surface.** `delvec schema --stage all` exports `sheer` beside `wall` and `open` at each of the 4 keys of `boxes[].sides`, and `boxes[].grade` as `{rise, run}`, at `dsl_version` 0.76.0; a plan carrying them round-trips; `Sides::is_open` holds for `sheer` and `is_declared_open` does not; a `rise` outside `1..=5`, a `run` outside `1..=4` or a missing key is refused; every document the repository holds parses. *Judge*: `crates/dsl/tests/it/grade.rs::a_side_may_be_sheer`; `version_sites.py verify`. *Gallery*: `node/dais`, `node/crag`.
2. **A raised side steps down.** For one place over flat terrain at each drop `H` in 1..=6 and each of the slopes 1/2, 5/1, 1/4 and 3/2: the column `d` beyond the ring on a straight side stands at `P` for `d = 1`; while `a = r·⌈(d − 1)/n⌉ < H`, between `P − a` and `min(P − a + r, P)` (the bound, and the noise carrying it at most one rise back toward `P`); and at the natural height beyond; the regraded count along the side is, per edge column, the number of such `d` — `2H − 1` at the default slope. Thirty blocks at 5/1 are met in six columns. *Judge*: `grade.rs::a_raised_side_steps_down_to_the_ground`. *Denominator*: the columns examined, asserted as a number (1,338 over the slopes and drops). *Gallery*: the yard and the causeway (the default), `node/crag` (5/1).
3. **A lowered side steps up.** The mirror of 2 with the signs exchanged, and a heightmap terrain that crosses `P` along one side, where each column takes its own branch, a column already inside the bound is unchanged, and a column no bank reaches is unchanged. *Judge*: `grade.rs::a_lowered_side_steps_up_to_the_ground`, `grade.rs::ground_that_already_meets_the_side_is_left`. *Denominator*: as 2. *Gallery*: none — recorded debt (§9).
4. **A corner is rounded and a wall end is turned.** Over generated plans (one place, every one of the 256 combinations of the four values on four sides, drops −4..=4): every column's height equals the definition of §4 computed a second way (the edge columns, the bank's flood and the step count re-derived by counting runs against the exact squared distance, then `settle` with the noise); no two 4-adjacent columns' bounds differ by more than the rise; a corner between a graded and an ungraded side is not an edge column (the edge-column count is equal to the second route's). *Judge*: `grade.rs::a_corner_is_rounded_and_a_wall_end_is_turned`. *Denominator*: plans generated and columns compared, both asserted. *Gallery*: the yard's corners and wall ends.
5. **No cell of any place is written.** For the same generated plans and for a two-place plan: `Site::owner` over every cell of every claim, `fixed_cells`, every frame and every allocation's `ground` are equal with the terrain graded and with every side rewritten `sheer`; a `wall` side, a `sheer` side and an aloft place each regrade 0 columns as the only place of a plan. *Judge*: `grade.rs::the_grade_writes_no_cell_of_any_place`. *Denominator*: claim cells compared. *Gallery*: `node/dais`; perturbation — one `sheer` removed moves the emitted terrain and no byte of any piece.
6. **A regraded column is skinned as terrain.** Over a fill with two strata by `y` band and one by `steep`: each regraded column's `surface` and `below` are those of the first stratum its graded height meets; the column is solid from the region's bottom to its surface. *Judge*: `grade.rs::a_regraded_column_is_skinned_as_terrain`. *Denominator*: regraded columns examined.
7. **The built ground is the graded ground.** For three plans over the blockout fixture (two declared-open sides, one raised and one lowered; the raised place at 5/1; the raised place unwalled on three sides): in every whole column no volume covers, the assembled world's highest terrain block stands at `Ground::top`, and `Ground::trees`, the fixed ground's blocks, the handout's ground columns and the daylight survey's ground read the same height (one agreement test over each consumer). *Judge*: `crates/delvec/tests/it/grade.rs::the_built_ground_is_the_graded_ground`. *Denominator*: columns compared per plan, none 0, every regraded column among them. **Recorded debt**: the gallery overlay is not among the plans (its pieces are built by the gallery's own generator, outside the test library); its built ground is judged by the build's own observers only.
8. **`DW1108`.** Refused: two raised places whose facing graded sides stand closer than the difference needs; a bank that would move another place's shoulder; a bank that would change the ground at another place's wall foot; a cut removing a cell beside a declared `minecraft:water` volume; one removing a cell below one. Passing: each of the first three with the stated columns added; the first pair with one side `sheer`; two places that meet; a place's bank against its own wall; a bank raised into the water volume; a `ground` volume inside a bank. *Judge*: `grade.rs::dw1108_a_grade_has_no_room_to_land` (5 refused shapes, 7 passing); the gallery probes `a-crag-with-no-room-for-a-gentle-bank` and `a-cut-beside-a-pond`, each refused with `DW1108`.
9. **A declared-open side over a drop stands open.** A place five above and one five below the terrain (the blockout fixture's loft and tunnel), each with a side declared `open`: the clear-width ledger's `open side` rows are whole at the `ring` and `approach` stations and `DW1065` is silent. Perturbation: a `clearance` volume declared over the raised side's shoulder columns, and a `massif` over the lowered side's, each bring `DW1065` back for that side alone. *Judge*: `crates/delvec/tests/it/grade.rs::a_declared_open_side_over_a_drop_stands_open`. *Denominator*: open-side ways judged, stations per way. *Gallery*: the `open` sides of the yard and the causeway, over the falling terrain, present in `validation/clear-width.json` as whole.
10. **Nothing moves where nothing is regraded.** A campaign with no site plan, one on a `solid` fill, one on an `open` fill whose every side is `wall`, and one whose unwalled sides all stand at the terrain's height each emit, file by file, the bytes the test holds from the base revision, and print `C = 0`. **A loosening, declared in these words**: the comparison is made with this revision's `dsl_version` string read as the base's and without `manifest.json` (the index of the input documents, whose `dsl_version` lines the version change moved), because five emitted files state the `dsl_version` and nothing else of them differs; the world is the in-process build's output, as `emit::build` returns it. With the engine changed and the gallery's documents untouched, `gallery-baseline.py` moves the `dsl_version` sites, the new `validation/grade.json`, and the site-plan overlay's rows the default grades (the yard's three `open` sides and the causeway's two, over its falling terrain); with the two places added, every further moved row is attributed to one of them. For `foot-of-the-mountain` the count changes and is declared: the `grade:` line before and after the meadow's east side is graded is written in the commit body, with 3 regraded columns per edge column where the drop is 2. *Judge*: `crates/delvec/tests/it/grade.rs::a_campaign_with_nothing_to_grade_emits_what_it_emitted`; the gallery baseline's diff. *Denominator*: 3 of the 4 fixture campaigns, stated; the baseline's rows, stated. **Recorded debt**: the fourth — a campaign whose unwalled sides all stand at the terrain's height — has no fixture that builds at the base revision (on the blockout fixture an unwalled side at the grade lets a body walk off the region, `DW0322`, at the base revision as at this one); the zero it would show is asserted at the plan level by criterion 4's drop-0 plans.
11. **The line and the ledger.** The `grade:` line is printed for every campaign with a site plan, zeroes included, and `U = S + H`, `C = R + L`, and the ledger's per-side `raised` and `lowered` sum to `R` and `L`; `terrain_sha256` is recomputed by the test from the rows. Perturbation: one heightmap pixel inside a bank changed moves the hash and one side's counts; a pixel outside every bound moves neither. *Judge*: `grade.rs::the_ledger_counts_what_was_regraded`. *Gallery*: `check-gallery-coverage.py` reports 0 in neither state with `sheer`, `PlanBox.grade`, `Slope.rise` and `Slope.run` bound on the `site-plan` overlay. **Recorded debt**: the gate does not yet refuse a `grade.json` whose `C` is 0 on that overlay — it reads the primary's build only, and the overlay's build is the baseline job's.
12. **Codes and pages.** `check-dw-codes.py`, `check-diagnostic-messages.py`, `check-capability-ownership.py`, `check-skill-page.py`, `check-stated-counts.py`, `check-doc-dupes.py`, `check-unsanctioned-identifiers.py` and `version_sites.py verify` are green; `compiler.md` carries `DW1108` and no row for `DW1109`.
