# spec-0135: A body carries another — a boat, a minecart and a saddled mob are one vehicle class a campaign places, a player boards and leaves, a route may ride, and the bot proves the ride by riding

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Ground**: written against engine `ee036a0cc` (`main`, `delvec 1.16.0`, `dsl 0.70.0`), read whole for: `Actor` in `crates/dsl/src/actor.rs`; `Wave` and the equipment shape in `crates/dsl/src/wave.rs` and `equipment.rs`; the body tables `crates/delvec/data/entity-bodies-1.21.11.json` and `crates/dsl/data/entity-slots-1.21.11.json` (spec-0067); `Footprint`, `entity_dims`, `neighbors_fp` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/nav/world/body.rs`; `RestKind`, `body_reach` in `nav/world/reach.rs`; `verify_boundary_safety` in `nav/horizon.rs`; the route leg and `RouteBinding` in `nav/route/leg.rs`; `nav/lane.rs` (`DW0386`); `plan::body_point` and `zone_box`; the harness `critical-path.ts`, `executor/transport.ts`, `executor/walk.ts`, `navigation.ts`, `movement.ts`; mineflayer `4.37.1` (`lib/plugins/entities.js`: `mount`, `dismount`, `moveVehicle`; `physics.js`: the `player_input` packet; minecraft-data `1.21.11` `packet_vehicle_move`); `docs/reference/compiler.md` (`actors[]`, `waves[]`, `teleport`, `boundary`, the contract), `delvec/compiler/nav.md`, `body-movement.md`, spec-0038, spec-0066, spec-0067, spec-0083, spec-0099, spec-0113, spec-0134 §10; the vanilla record `docs/reference/vehicles-animals-and-targets.md` §4–§6, established for this spec.
- **What it is for**: the same festival-day script: a capsized cargo of crates in the bay fetched by rowing, two boats at the dock, enemy ships berthed, and a pig race for fun. The engine has no body that carries another: a piece ships no entity (`DW0730`), nav refuses water (spec-0134 §10 opens it for a swimmer), and the harness has no leg but a walk and a link. Ruled: riding a mob, rowing a boat and sitting in a minecart are one class — a body that carries another. This spec gives the class a surface, says what each kind traverses and how nav models it, how the harness drives each, and what is refused.
- **Research**: `docs/reference/vehicles-animals-and-targets.md` §4 (a saddled pig under a rider), §5 (a boat), §6 (a minecart), measured on the pinned server by `validation/festival-probe.mjs` with the harness's own mineflayer. Every rule below is **[cited]** from it or the record named, or **[authored]** here.
- **Numbers**: spec `0135`; `dsl_version` `0.74.0` (after spec-0134's 0.73.0 — §4 rides the water relation it opens); `DW1096`–`DW1099`, all four used (§8).
- **Non-goals**: a vehicle for an NPC or an actor (a body carrying a non-player is another spec); a vehicle a player breaks or crafts; a boat on land or ice; a minecart that is not the plain rider's cart (a chest, hopper or furnace cart carries no rider); a mob the game does not steer (a llama, a dolphin); the pig's boost; a horse's jump; breath under a capsized boat; the race's scoring (it is not scored); any campaign's dock.

## 1. The class, and why one **[authored, on cited facts]**

**[cited]** (the record §4–§6): a saddled pig, an oak boat and a minecart are each mounted by a right-click of a standing player and by `ride <player> mount <vehicle>`; each is left by the sneak input and by `ride <player> dismount`; in each the rider's position on the server is the vehicle's seat; a `tp` of the vehicle carries the rider; `execute as <player> on vehicle` reaches the carrier; and in none does the rider's forward input move the vehicle on this server — the pig, the boat and the minecart under a rider are moved by what their kind is moved by (§3). **[authored]** What they share is everything the engine must know: where it is placed, how a body gets on and off, where the body is while on, that a command can seat and unseat a body, and that the vehicle's own medium decides where it can go. What differs is the medium and the driver, which is a property of the kind. So the engine has one class, **`vehicles[]`**, whose kind is derived from the entity, and every proof below quantifies over the class with the kind's medium plugged in.

## 2. The surface **[authored]**

Stage 5 gains:

```json
"vehicles": [
  {"id": "vehicle/dock-boat", "entity": "minecraft:oak_boat", "at": {"anchor": "anchor/dock-slip", "offset": [0, 0, 1]}, "facing": "south",
   "bounds": {"anchor": "anchor/bay", "extent": [40, 4, 30]}, "name": "The Gull"},
  {"id": "vehicle/race-pig", "entity": "minecraft:pig", "at": {"anchor": "anchor/church-gate"}, "bounds": {"anchor": "anchor/main-street", "extent": [60, 6, 20]}}
]
```

- `id` is `vehicle/<kebab>` (`DW0110`/`DW0111`). `entity` is an id of the pinned entity registry (`DW0173`) whose **kind** the engine derives from the registry tables: **boat** — every `*_boat` and `*_raft` (a `*_chest_boat` seats one and carries cargo; it is refused, §8); **minecart** — `minecraft:minecart` alone; **mount** — a living entity whose `entity-slots` row draws a `saddle` and whose kind the pinned game steers under a rider (`pig`, `strider`, `horse`, `donkey`, `mule`, `skeleton_horse`, `zombie_horse`, `camel`), read from a data file `crates/delvec/data/entity-steering-1.21.11.json` the maintenance regenerator writes from the pinned jar (`Saddleable` and the kind's ridden input, with the steering item where the kind needs one: a carrot on a stick for a pig, a warped fungus on a stick for a strider). Any other entity is `DW1096`.
- `at` is a mark (spec-0066, `Plan::body_point`, `DW0897`), `facing` the ordinary yaw word; `name` a name tag (`DW0983`'s one-body rule holds: a vehicle is one body).
- `bounds` is the region the vehicle lives in — the anchor-centred box every region is — and **required**: it is the cut of the medium graph (§4) and the re-seat rule (§3). A mark outside its bounds is `DW1099`.
- A mount takes `equipment` in the wave mob's shape, the saddle filled by the engine (`DW0898` holds any other piece to what the body draws); a horse family mount is summoned tamed; a mount is summoned `NoAI`-free, `Silent`, `PersistenceRequired`, with its `movement_speed` as the registry's default unless `attributes` says otherwise, and with `Invulnerable`, as every vehicle is: a ride the party can break is a route the party can lose.
- A campaign may seat a vehicle at load (every entry, in `setup_finish`, by its own `vehicle_seat_<id>` function) and by the verb **`seat-vehicle {vehicle}`** at a beat, which runs the same function; a `despawn-vehicle {vehicle}` removes it (the removal `removal_lines` performs). The two verbs are the vehicle's and take any bundle.

## 3. What each kind traverses, and what moves it **[cited, with the authored rule after each]**

**A boat.** [cited, §5] A boat summoned over water floats at the water's level; a rider's forward input does not move it; a boat's position **reported by the rider's client** (`vehicle_move`) is what the server takes, accepted whole at two, five, ten and twenty blocks a second; a `Motion` written to the boat moves it with its rider; a dismount in open water stands the body on the boat. [authored] The medium is **water**: the connected body of water cells of §4 at the boat's footprint (`entity_dims`, 1.375 wide → the 2 × 2 column footprint `Footprint::for_dims` already derives), on the surface only — a boat does not dive — and never a land cell, ice included. The driver is the rider's client (§6).

**A minecart.** [cited, §6] A minecart on a rail with a rider is moved by the rails and its own momentum: pushed to speed it runs the powered rails at about ten blocks a second and coasts the plain rail at that speed for the forty-five blocks the rail had, stopping only where the rail ends; a rider's forward input nudges it a fifth of a block in three seconds; a `tp` carries the rider; a dismount stands the body beside the rail. [authored] The medium is **rails**: the rail cells of the assembled world (`rail`, `powered_rail`, `detector_rail`, `activator_rail`) joined by their shapes, read from the assembled block map; a powered rail is a driver where its `powered` state is `true` in the assembled world (the piece powers it: a redstone block beneath, a lever — the engine reads the state, never infers power), a brake where `false`. The driver is the rails: the ride leg must start on or against a powered rail the cart is pushed from (a `seat-vehicle` mark on a powered rail with a solid block behind it, the vanilla launcher), and the leg's rail run is driven where every stretch of unpowered rail between powered ones is at most the measured coast of the record (§6) and ends where a rail ends or an unpowered stretch exceeds it. A slope is a rail cell one higher or lower, as the shape says.

**A mount.** [cited, §4] A saddled pig is mounted and left as the others; under a rider holding a carrot on a stick it is not moved by the rider's input, and with the hand emptied it wanders on its own under the rider; its position reported by the rider's client is what the server takes: a flat run whole, a one-block step passed, a slab and a block passed when the report rises with them, a two-block wall refused at its face. [authored] The medium is **ground**: the walk relation at the mount's footprint and height (`neighbors_fp` with `entity_dims` — a pig's 0.9 × 0.9 fits a one-wide way, a horse's 1.4 needs two), rises of at most one block as the record measured, no jump, no climbable, no water deeper than the kind's wading (a mount that enters water dismounts its rider in vanilla — the medium stops at the shore); a mount's steering item is held for the whole leg, because a mount with an empty hand walks where its own AI takes it. The driver is the rider's client (§6) and the steering item: a ride on a pig owes a carrot on a stick obtainable before it on every path that rides (the `nav::movement_items` walk, as spec-0134's shot owes a bow), else `DW1096`.

**Re-seat.** [authored] A vehicle with no rider outside its `bounds`, or missing, is re-seated at its mark by `vehicle_reseat_<id>` on a 100-tick replace-mode `schedule` (zero per-tick cost), so a boat rowed out to sea and abandoned, a cart run off its rail, a pig walked away, is back at the dock; a vehicle with a rider is never touched by it. A rider carried past the campaign's `boundary` (stage 1) is returned by it as any player is — **[cited]** (`compiler.md`, `teleport`; the record §5: a `tp` of a player dismounts them) — and the vehicle then re-seats.

## 4. How nav models a ride **[authored, on the cited model]**

**[cited]** (spec-0083): the route proof takes a link — a `teleport` a `once: false` trigger hosts — as a way between two cells when no walk crosses, with a stand cell and a landing. **[cited]** (spec-0134 §10): a connected body of water is in the route relation for a swimmer.

**[authored]** A vehicle is a **way** of the route proof, keyed to the vehicle: from any **boarding cell** — a standable cell of the walk region from which a body's eye reaches the vehicle's hitbox within the interaction reach (`metrics::PLAYER_EYE_HEIGHT`, three blocks; the sight rule) — through the **medium graph** inside `bounds` from the vehicle's seat, to any **leaving cell** — a standable cell within the kind's dismount reach of a medium cell (the record: beside the pig, beside the rail, on the boat; and for a boat, the water itself, where the swimmer relation of spec-0134 §10 takes over). A leg the walk cannot cross and a ride can is proven as a **ride leg** (`LegRoute` gains a `ride: {vehicle, board, medium_cells, leave}`), priced at the kind's rate from the record (a boat at the reported rate the harness drives, a minecart at the measured ten blocks a second, a mount at its `movement_speed`), and exported on the step as a `ride` (§6). A leg the walk crosses is walked; the ride is never preferred. The medium graph is computed once per vehicle per region state (a tide moves a boat's medium as spec-0038 moves the swimmer's) and cut at `bounds`.

**Proofs over the class**, each quantifying over every vehicle whether or not a leg rides it:

- **The seat is in its medium** (`DW1097`): the mark's cell is water for a boat, a rail for a minecart, standable ground for a mount, in the assembled world at every region state the seat is live in.
- **A way off from everywhere** (`DW1098`): from every medium cell the vehicle can reach inside `bounds`, a leaving cell exists from which the proven walk region is reachable by the reach graph (`World::body_reach`, the movement `DW0921` floods — walking, falling, jumping, climbing, swimming), so a rider who leaves anywhere is never stranded; for a boat this is the swimmer's climb-out `horizon.rs` already judges over the water body, named with the vehicle. A rail run that ends in a pit is this; a bay whose far shore is a cliff is this.
- **The party can board** (`DW1099`): a boarding cell exists; and, for a ride leg no walk crosses, the vehicles boardable at that leg's start carry seats for four (a boat seats two, a minecart and a mount one — the kind's seat count is a registry fact the data file carries), because a required ride must carry the whole party and the engine never proves a party split.
- The ordinary proofs read a rider as a body at the seat: `DW0314` accepts a ride step's waypoint on its medium; `keep_out` volumes are read along the medium cells as along a walk; the aggro spheres of a wave the medium passes are priced as on a walk; a `lethal_volumes[]` region the medium crosses is `DW0311`'s lethal cause as on a walk.

## 5. The player's acts, and what the engine reads **[cited, authored]**

**[cited]** (the record §4–§6): mounting is a right-click on the body; leaving is the sneak input; the server's `RootVehicle` on the player names the carrier; `execute … on vehicle` resolves to it; a rider in a box is counted by the box selector at the seat. **[authored]** No objective kind is added: a `reach-anchor` whose box a rider enters completes for the rider as for a walker; an `interact` or a `collect` is performed after leaving. A gate may read the ride: `requires_state` over a derived datum (spec-0134 §1's `counts`) gains `of: {"riders": "vehicle/<id>"}` — the count of players whose root vehicle is that vehicle, written by the census tick through `execute as @a on vehicle if entity @s[tag=dw_vehicle_<id>]` — so a beat can hold until the party is aboard, and a `body-enters` trigger over it fires when the first player boards. The stakes of a ride are the kind's: a rider is a body at the seat, and nothing is granted or forgiven for sitting down.

## 6. How the harness drives each kind **[authored, on cited facts]**

**[cited]** (the record §4–§6; mineflayer `entities.js`, `physics.js`): mineflayer's `mount` is the right-click; its `dismount` sends the jump bit on this version's input packet, which does not dismount — the sneak bit does; `moveVehicle` sends the input bits, which move none of the three kinds; the client-reported `vehicle_move` is what moves a boat and a mount; the rails move a cart.

**[authored]** `critical-path.json` and every `world-path-<k>.json` gain the step kind **`ride`** (`STEP_ACTIONS`; the contract's format number moves in both constants, numbered by the planner): `{"action": "ride", "vehicle": "vehicle/<id>", "kind": "boat" | "minecart" | "mount", "board": [x, y, z], "medium": [[x, y, z], …], "rate": blocksPerSecond, "leave": [x, y, z], "steering_item"?: "minecraft:carrot_on_a_stick"}`. The executor's `ride`:

1. walks to `board`, looks at the vehicle's body (the crosshair rule of `crosshair.ts`, so an occluded vehicle fails by name), and mounts; the step fails when the server's `RootVehicle` — read on the opped channel, never the client's belief, which the record shows stale after a dismount — is not the vehicle within the grace;
2. drives by kind: a **boat** or a **mount** by reporting the vehicle's position along `medium` at `rate` (a `vehicle_move` per tick, yaw along the tangent; for a mount the steering item is equipped first), the way a vanilla client drives — navigation along a proven route at a stated rate, never a physics the harness computes; a **minecart** by nothing: the rails drive it, and the executor waits with the same grace a transport's landing has (`TRANSPORT_NEAR`, `TRANSPORT_NEAR_Y`);
3. asserts arrival by the **server's** position of the rider within the landing tolerance of the last medium cell, then leaves by the sneak input, asserts `RootVehicle` is none and the body is at `leave` within the tolerance, and walks on.

The run report carries `rides[]`: per step the vehicle, the kind, the medium cells, the distance the server saw the vehicle move against the distance reported, the final seat, and the leaving cell reached. A reported move the server did not take — a boat the server left behind, a mount at a step it refused — is a red by name, which is what makes the ride a proof and not a replay.

The engine states, on every validate, `vehicle binding (DW1097–DW1099): <n> vehicle(s) — <kind> <id> medium <cells> cell(s) in bounds, board <k> cell(s), leave <m> cell(s), seats <s>; <r> ride leg(s) on the critical path and <w> on branch paths`.

## 7. PackTest and the gallery **[authored, under spec-0039]**

Per vehicle the build emits `vehicle_<id>_seated` (after `setup_finish`, exactly one body with `dw_vehicle_<id>` stands within a block of the mark, in its medium) and `vehicle_<id>_reseats` (the body teleported outside `bounds`, 100 ticks stepped, the body at the mark again); per ride leg `ride_<k>_boards` (a dummy player seated by `ride … mount` from the board cell, `RootVehicle` read back, `ride … dismount`, the body at a leaving cell) — the command seating the record §4–§6 measured, proving the seat, the read and the leave on every pull request while the client drive is the bot's. The gallery primary binds one boat on a pool the walk cannot cross (the water leg spec-0134 §13 adds gains a boat beside the swim, and the route takes the walk where it exists, so the overlay removes the walk and the boat is the ride leg), one minecart on a short powered line of the long hall, one pig with a carrot in the class kit; probes `a-chest-boat-with-a-rider`, `a-llama-under-a-saddle`, `a-boat-on-the-floor`, `a-rail-into-a-pit`, `a-boat-nobody-can-reach`, `a-boat-for-four`. The gallery bot rides each kind in its run and the run report's `rides[]` is read by the coverage gate for three kinds.

## 8. The rules **[authored]**

| code | module | refuses |
|---|---|---|
| `DW1096` | `dsl::vehicle` | an entity that carries no rider — not a boat, the rider's minecart or a steerable saddled kind (a chest boat, a hopper cart, a llama); or a mount whose steering item no path puts in a hand before a ride |
| `DW1097` | `compiler::nav` | a vehicle seated outside its medium: a boat on a floor, a cart off a rail, a mount in water or in a wall |
| `DW1098` | `compiler::nav` | a medium cell the vehicle can reach from which no leaving cell reaches the proven walk region |
| `DW1099` | `compiler::nav` | a mark outside its bounds; no boarding cell; or a ride leg no walk crosses whose boardable seats are fewer than four |

Reused: `DW0110`/`DW0111` (id), `DW0173` (entity), `DW0897` (mark), `DW0898` (equipment), `DW0983` (name), `DW0311` (a leg no walk and no ride crosses, naming the ride it tried), `DW0314`, `DW0322`, `DW0921`, `DW1043`.

## 10. What it leaves out, by name **[authored]**

A **chest boat** and a **cargo cart** (no rider). A **llama**, a **dolphin**, anything the game does not steer. A **second rider** on a boat as a separate body in the proof (the leg proves one body; the seat count admits the party). The **pig's boost** and a **horse's jump** (the ride leg never needs either; a jump is a rise the medium refuses). A vehicle for an **NPC** or an **actor**. **Ice**, a boat's land speed, a cart on a **slope** faster than the flat rate (the rate is a floor for the harness, and the walk proof prices a ride by it). A **current**. A vehicle the player **breaks** (it cannot) or **crafts**. The race's **scoring**.

## 11. Acceptance criteria

1. **The surface.** `delvec schema --stage quest-expansion` exports `vehicles[]` with `id`, `entity`, `at`, `facing?`, `bounds`, `name?`, `equipment?`, `attributes?`, and the verbs `seat-vehicle` and `despawn-vehicle`, at `dsl_version` 0.74.0; the steering table exists with its pin and lists the eight kinds with their items and seats; every document the repository holds parses.
2. **The kind.** `crates/dsl/tests/it/vehicle.rs`: a boat, a raft, a minecart and each steerable kind resolve their kind; a chest boat, a hopper cart, a llama and a zombie are `DW1096`; a pig with no carrot obtainable before its ride leg is `DW1096` and green with one in the kit.
3. **The seat.** `crates/delvec/tests/it/vehicle.rs`: each kind's summon carries `Invulnerable`, the tag, the saddle for a mount (`DW0898` on any other piece), `Tame` for the horse family; `vehicle_seat_<id>` runs in `setup_finish` and from `seat-vehicle`; `vehicle_reseat_<id>` is scheduled at 100 ticks in replace mode and teleports only a riderless body outside `bounds`; `despawn-vehicle` emits the removal lines. `DW1097` on a boat over stone, a cart over grass, a pig over water; green on each in its medium.
4. **The medium and the ride leg.** `vehicle.rs`: a pool the walk cannot cross is a ride leg by boat with its medium cells, board and leave; the same pool with a bridge is walked and the vehicle binding counts zero ride legs; a rail line from a powered launcher to its end is a ride leg of the cart, its driven run cut where an unpowered stretch exceeds the coast; a paddock with a slab path is a ride leg of the pig and with a full step is not (`DW0311` naming the ride it tried). Perturbation: shrinking `bounds` to exclude the far shore turns the boat leg to `DW0311` with no other change.
5. **A way off and a way on.** `vehicle.rs`: a rail into a pit with no way up is `DW1098` naming the cell; a bay whose far shore is a two-block cliff is `DW1098` through the swimmer's climb-out, naming the vehicle; a boat behind a fence no cell reaches is `DW1099`; a two-seat boat on a leg no walk crosses is `DW1099` and two boats are green; a mark outside `bounds` is `DW1099`.
6. **The reader.** `vehicle.rs`: a census `of: {riders}` emits the `on vehicle` store line; a `body-enters` over it fires on the first rider.
7. **The harness.** `harness/test/critical-path.test.ts`: a `ride` step parses with its fields per kind and is refused without `board`, `medium`, `leave`, or a `rate` at or under zero; the executor's `ride` on a mock bot mounts, drives a boat and a mount by reported moves along `medium` at `rate`, waits for a cart, reads the server's `RootVehicle` and position for every verdict, leaves by the sneak bit, and names the vehicle the server left behind; `rides[]` is in the run report; the format constant moved in both languages.
8. **PackTest.** The gallery build emits the three templates per vehicle and per ride leg of §7 and they run green in `tier 2`.
9. **The gallery.** The three kinds are bound as §7 states, the six probes are refused with their named codes, the coverage gate reports every unit bound, and the gallery bot's run report carries three rides each with a server-seen distance equal to the reported one within the tolerance.
10. **Codes and pages.** Every code of §8 is asserted by a test and sits on its module's page; `check-dw-codes.py`, `check-diagnostic-messages.py`, `check-capability-ownership.py`, `check-effect-roots.py`, `check-gallery-coverage.py`, `check-live-commands.py` and `check-skill-page.py` are green.
