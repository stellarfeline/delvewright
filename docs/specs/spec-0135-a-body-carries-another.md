# spec-0135: A body carries another — a boat, a minecart and a saddled mob are one vehicle class a campaign places, a player boards and leaves, a route may ride, and the bot proves the ride by riding

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Ground**: written against engine `ee036a0cc` (`main`, `delvec 1.16.0`, `dsl 0.70.0`), read whole for: `Actor` in `crates/dsl/src/actor.rs`; `Wave` and the equipment shape in `crates/dsl/src/wave.rs` and `equipment.rs`; the body tables `crates/delvec/data/entity-bodies-1.21.11.json` and `crates/dsl/data/entity-slots-1.21.11.json` (spec-0067); `Footprint`, `entity_dims`, `neighbors_fp` in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/nav/world/body.rs`; `RestKind`, `body_reach` in `nav/world/reach.rs`; `verify_boundary_safety` in `nav/horizon.rs`; the route leg and `RouteBinding` in `nav/route/leg.rs`; `nav/lane.rs` (`DW0386`); `plan::body_point` and `zone_box`; the harness `critical-path.ts`, `executor/transport.ts`, `executor/walk.ts`, `navigation.ts`, `movement.ts`; mineflayer `4.37.1` (`lib/plugins/entities.js`: `mount`, `dismount`, `moveVehicle`; `physics.js`: the `player_input` packet; minecraft-data `1.21.11` `packet_vehicle_move`); `docs/reference/compiler.md` (`actors[]`, `waves[]`, `teleport`, `boundary`, the contract), `delvec/compiler/nav.md`, `body-movement.md`, spec-0038, spec-0066, spec-0067, spec-0083, spec-0099, spec-0113, spec-0134 §10; the vanilla record `docs/reference/vehicles-animals-and-targets.md` §4–§6, §11 and §12, established for this spec.
- **What it is for**: the same festival-day script: a capsized cargo of crates in the bay fetched by rowing, two boats at the dock, enemy ships berthed, and a pig race for fun. The engine has no body that carries another: a piece ships no entity (`DW0730`), nav refuses water (spec-0134 §10 opens it for a swimmer), and the harness has no leg but a walk and a link. Ruled: riding a mob, rowing a boat and sitting in a minecart are one class — a body that carries another. This spec gives the class a surface, says what each kind traverses and how nav models it, how a campaign declares that a leg is ridden, how the harness drives each, and what is refused.
- **Research**: `docs/reference/vehicles-animals-and-targets.md` §4 (a saddled pig under a rider), §5 (a boat), §6 (a minecart), §11 (a cart at rest and pushed once, a mount that waits), measured on the pinned server by `validation/festival-probe.mjs` with the harness's own mineflayer. Every rule below is **[cited]** from it or the record named, or **[authored]** here.
- **Numbers**: spec `0135`; `dsl_version` `0.74.0` (after spec-0134's 0.73.0 — §4 rides the water relation it opens); `DW1096`–`DW1099`, all four used (§8).
- **Non-goals**: a vehicle for an NPC or an actor (a body carrying a non-player is another spec); a vehicle a player breaks or crafts; a boat on land or ice; a minecart that is not the plain rider's cart (a chest, hopper or furnace cart carries no rider); a mob the game does not steer (a llama, a dolphin); the pig's boost; a horse's jump; breath under a capsized boat; the race's scoring (it is not scored); any campaign's dock.

## 1. The class, and why one **[authored, on cited facts]**

**[cited]** (the record §4–§6): a saddled pig, an oak boat and a minecart are each mounted by a right-click of a standing player and by `ride <player> mount <vehicle>`; each is left by the sneak input and by `ride <player> dismount`; in each the rider's position on the server is the vehicle's seat; a `tp` of the vehicle carries the rider; `execute as <player> on vehicle` reaches the carrier; and in none does the rider's forward input move the vehicle on this server — the pig, the boat and the minecart under a rider are moved by what their kind is moved by (§3). **[authored]** What they share is everything the engine must know: where it is placed, how a body gets on and off, where the body is while on, that a command can seat and unseat a body, and that the vehicle's own medium decides where it can go. What differs is the medium and the driver, which is a property of the kind. So the engine has one class, **`vehicles[]`**, whose kind is derived from the entity, and every proof below quantifies over the class with the kind's medium plugged in.

## 2. The surface **[authored]**

Stage 5 gains:

```json
"vehicles": [
  {"id": "vehicle/dock-boat", "entity": "minecraft:oak_boat", "at": {"anchor": "anchor/dock-slip", "offset": [0, 0, 1]}, "facing": "south",
   "bounds": {"anchor": "anchor/bay", "extent": [40, 4, 30]}, "name": "The Gull"},
  {"id": "vehicle/race-pig", "entity": "minecraft:pig", "at": {"anchor": "anchor/church-gate"}, "bounds": {"anchor": "anchor/main-street", "extent": [60, 6, 20]}}
]
```

- `id` is `vehicle/<kebab>` (`DW0110`/`DW0111`). `entity` is an id of the pinned entity registry (`DW0173`) whose **kind** the engine derives from the registry tables: **boat** — every `*_boat` and `*_raft` (a `*_chest_boat` seats one and carries cargo; it is refused, §8); **minecart** — `minecraft:minecart` alone; **mount** — a living entity whose `entity-slots` row draws a `saddle` and whose kind the pinned game steers under a rider on the ground — the rows the table carries, never a list written here — read from a data file `crates/dsl/data/entity-steering-1.21.11.json` the maintenance regenerator writes from the pinned jar (`Saddleable` and the kind's ridden input, with the steering item where the kind needs one: a carrot on a stick for a pig, a warped fungus on a stick for a strider). Any other entity is `DW1096`.
- `at` is a mark (spec-0066, `Plan::body_point`, `DW0897`), `facing` the ordinary yaw word — and, for a minecart, the way along its rail the engine pushes it when a body boards (§3); `name` a name tag (`DW0983`'s one-body rule holds: a vehicle is one body).
- `bounds` is the region the vehicle lives in — the anchor-centred box every region is — and **required**: it is the cut of the medium graph (§4) and the re-seat rule (§3). A mark outside its bounds is `DW1099`.
- A mount takes `equipment` in the wave mob's shape, the saddle filled by the engine (`DW0898` holds any other piece to what the body draws); a horse family mount is summoned tamed; a mount is summoned `NoAI`-free, `Silent`, `PersistenceRequired`, with its `movement_speed` as the registry's default unless `attributes` says otherwise, and with `Invulnerable`, as every vehicle is: a ride the party can break is a route the party can lose. A mount nobody rides is held at its mark by a `movement_speed` modifier that takes its speed to nothing, which boarding removes (§3).
- A campaign may seat a vehicle at load (every entry, in `setup_finish`, by its own `vehicle_seat_<id>` function) and by the verb **`seat-vehicle {vehicle}`** at a beat, which runs the same function; a `despawn-vehicle {vehicle}` removes it (the removal `removal_lines` performs) and ends its re-seat (§3) until a `seat-vehicle` seats it again: the re-seat would otherwise put back within 100 ticks what the story took away. The two verbs are the vehicle's and take any bundle.
- **An objective of any kind may carry `ride: "vehicle/<id>"`**: the leg to that objective is ridden on that vehicle (§4). The declaration lives on the objective because a leg is not an object a campaign writes — a leg is the way to an objective, and the objective is the object the way belongs to — and it is one field of the objective class, the same on every kind. A `ride` naming no declared vehicle is `DW0112`.

## 3. What each kind traverses, and what moves it **[cited, with the authored rule after each]**

**A boat.** [cited, §5] A boat summoned over water floats at the water's level; a rider's forward input does not move it; a boat's position **reported by the rider's client** (`vehicle_move`) is what the server takes, accepted whole at two, five, ten and twenty blocks a second; a `Motion` written to the boat moves it with its rider; a dismount in open water stands the body on the boat. [authored] The medium is **water**: the surface cells of the water a body swims (spec-0134 §10's relation, `World::is_water_surface` — never lava, a lethal volume or water the swimmer is refused) at the boat's footprint, which is the registry's — `Footprint::for_dims` over `entity_dims` — and never a number written here; on the surface only — a boat does not dive — and never a land cell, ice included. The driver is the rider's client (§6), at five blocks a second.

**A minecart.** [cited, §6, §11] A minecart on a rail with a rider is moved by the rails and its own momentum; a rider's forward input nudges it a fifth of a block in three seconds and the rider's client does not move it; a `tp` carries the rider; a dismount stands the body beside the rail. A powered rail that is powered, with a solid block at one end — the vanilla launcher — runs a cart away **whether or not anybody sits in it**: an empty cart summoned on one came to rest eight blocks on. A cart on level plain rail stays where it is, empty or ridden. One push of 0.3 blocks a tick carried a ridden cart 57 cells of plain rail in 23.5 seconds, and the push can be the boarding itself: the reward of a `minecraft:started_riding` advancement writes it to the rider's vehicle. [authored] The medium is **rails**: the rail cells of the assembled world (`rail`, `powered_rail`, `detector_rail`, `activator_rail`) joined by their shapes, read from the assembled block map; a powered rail is a driver where its `powered` state is `true` in the assembled world (the engine reads the state, never infers power), a brake where `false`. The cart is seated **where it rests**: a level rail that does not launch it, running the way the cart's `facing` points, with the rail going on or a solid block standing behind it (else `DW1097`) — a cart on a launcher would not be at its mark when a body came for it, and a cart at the open end of its line is rolled off its rail by the first body that walks into it, where the boarding push dies on the floor ([cited, §11]). The driver is the engine's one push and the rails: boarding pushes the cart along `facing`, and its run goes from the mark along the rails through every stretch of rail that does not drive it of at most 45 cells — inside both coasts the record measured — and ends where the rail ends, on a brake, or where such a stretch exceeds the coast. A slope is a rail cell one higher or lower, as the shape says. A ride on a cart is waited for at 2.4 blocks a second, the slowest whole run the record holds.

**A mount.** [cited, §4, §11] A saddled pig is mounted and left as the others; under a rider holding a carrot on a stick it is not moved by the rider's input, and with the hand emptied it wanders on its own under the rider; its position reported by the rider's client is what the server takes, at the mount's own speed and at a speed of nothing alike: a flat run whole, a one-block step passed, a slab and a block passed when the report rises with them, a two-block wall refused at its face. A riderless pig whose speed is nothing does not leave where it stands. [authored] The medium is **ground**: the walk relation at the mount's footprint and height (`neighbors_fp` with `entity_dims` — a pig's 0.9 × 0.9 fits a one-wide way, a horse's 1.4 needs two), **a rise of at most one block** — the one limit, the step the record measured taken and the wall it measured refused — no jump, no climbable, no water (a mount that enters water dismounts its rider in vanilla — the medium stops at the shore); a mount's steering item is held for the whole leg, because a mount with an empty hand walks where its own AI takes it. The driver is the rider's client (§6), at four blocks a second — the reported rate the record measured taken whole; a reported move does not read `movement_speed`, so the attribute sets how the mount walks under a player's hand and never the proof's rate — and the steering item: a ride on a pig owes a carrot on a stick held by the party before it on the path — in **every** class's kit, or from a `give-item` an earlier step runs — else `DW1096`. It is the one rule spec-0134's shot owes its bow by (`MovementItems::first_held`): both owe the item on every path, and a player who chose the class without it is a path.

**Re-seat.** [authored] A vehicle with no rider that is away from its mark — more than a block and a half — or missing, is re-seated at its mark by `vehicle_reseat_<id>` on a 100-tick replace-mode `schedule` (zero per-tick cost), so a boat rowed out and abandoned, a cart at the end of its line, a pig walked away, is back where it is boarded; a vehicle with a rider is never touched by it. The rule is the mark and not `bounds`, because every proof of a ride starts at the mark: a vehicle left elsewhere inside its bounds would be one the next rider's proof did not describe. `despawn-vehicle` ends it (§2). A rider carried past the campaign's `boundary` (stage 1) is returned by it as any player is — **[cited]** (`compiler.md`, `teleport`; the record §5: a `tp` of a player dismounts them) — and the vehicle then re-seats.

## 4. How nav models a ride **[authored, on the cited model]**

**[cited]** (spec-0083): the route proof takes a link — a `teleport` a `once: false` trigger hosts — as a way between two cells when no walk crosses, with a stand cell and a landing. **[cited]** (spec-0134 §10): a connected body of water is in the route relation for a swimmer. **[cited]** (CLAUDE.md, *A check confirms intent*): a check judges what the author declared and never infers an undeclared intent.

**[authored] A ride is declared, never inferred.** A party swims water and walks a mount's ground, so a leg "no walk crosses and a ride can" all but never arises, and a vehicle the proof took only there would be a boat nobody is proven to row. The campaign says it: an objective's `ride` (§2) declares that the leg to it is ridden on that vehicle, and the proof then routes that leg through the vehicle's medium **whether or not a walk crosses it**. A leg with no `ride` is walked, and no vehicle is tried for it; a vehicle no objective rides is still judged by the proofs over the class below, and is the party's to use or leave.

The ride of a declared leg: from the leg's start the party walks — round the cells the vehicle's body stands in, never through them, because a body that walks into a vehicle shoves it off its mark ([cited, §11]) — to the **boarding cell** nearest the mark that it can walk to — a standable cell from which a body's eye reaches the vehicle's hitbox within the interaction reach (`metrics::PLAYER_EYE_HEIGHT`, three blocks; the sight rule) — rides the **medium graph** inside `bounds` from the mark, and leaves at a **leaving cell** — a standable cell beside a medium cell, within the vehicle's own edge and a block up or down (the record: beside the pig, beside the rail, off the boat's side) — from which it walks on to the leg's end. A ride takes the party **as near the leg's end as its medium reaches**: among the leaving cells that walk on, the one nearest the end wins, then the shorter ride; the rest of the leg is walked. A ride moves the vehicle — at least one medium cell past the mark — and a cart is left only where its run ends, because the rails take it there. **A ride keeps out of every completion volume armed on its leg, its own objective's included**, by the hitbox rule a walk is held to (`World::meets_keep_out_fp`, the rule the bot's `volumeReachesCell` mirrors): the ride is its own step in front of the one that performs the objective, so no cell it boards from, passes or leaves at may be one a body touches such a volume from. A ride therefore ends short of its `reach-anchor` and the party walks in, and no effect of that objective's completion — a `despawn-vehicle` among them — can fire under a rider: the case does not arise. A cart cannot stop short of its run, so a run through such a volume carries no ride. A ride is never left on the goal's own cell, where what the party came for stands. The ride is spliced into the path as a `ride` step in front of the step that performs the objective (`LinkTakes::rides`), exported with the kind's rate (§3, §6). The medium graph is computed per leg over the leg's own region state (a tide moves a boat's medium as spec-0038 moves the swimmer's) and cut at `bounds`.

A declared leg its vehicle does not carry is **`DW0311`**, naming the vehicle and which thing failed — the party walks to no boarding cell from the leg's start; its medium inside `bounds` is its mark alone; its mark or a cart's run lies in a volume the leg keeps out of; the party walks to the leg's end from no leaving cell — and saying whether the party could have walked the leg (a mark outside its medium or its bounds is `DW1097`/`DW1099`'s to name). **A leg declared ridden on a vehicle the path has taken away** is `DW0311` at the plan: the last `seat-vehicle` or `despawn-vehicle` of that vehicle the path certainly fires before the objective's step is a despawn. A seat the path cannot date seats it for every step; a despawn it cannot date removes nothing.

**Proofs over the class**, each quantifying over every vehicle whether or not a leg rides it:

- **The seat is in its medium** (`DW1097`): the mark's cell is surface water across the boat's footprint; a level rail the cart rests on, running the way it faces; standable ground for a mount at its footprint, out of water — in the assembled world.
- **A way off from everywhere** (`DW1098`): from every medium cell the vehicle can reach inside `bounds`, a body that leaves there gets to the region the party walks by the reach graph (`World::body_reach`, the movement `DW0921` floods — walking, falling, jumping, climbing, swimming), flooded from the leaving cells and, for a boat, from the water under the hull, where a rider who leaves in open water is. A rail run that ends in a pit is this; a bay no swimmer climbs out of is this. A ledge a rider drops from to the floor the party walks is not.
- **The party can board** (`DW1099`): the mark is inside `bounds`; a boarding cell exists; and, for a declared ride whose leg **no walk crosses**, the vehicles boardable at that leg's start that carry it seat four between them (the kind's seat count is the table's fact, never a number written here: the seats a party takes by clicking, which the record §12 measured for a camel at two where the jar's `canAddPassenger` admits three), because a required ride must carry the whole party and the engine never proves a party split. A declared ride beside a walk seats whoever takes it; the rest walk.
- The ordinary proofs read a rider as a body at the seat: `DW0314` accepts a ride step's waypoint on its medium; `keep_out` volumes are read along the medium cells as along a walk; a `lethal_volumes[]` region the medium crosses is `DW0311`'s lethal cause as on a walk.

**Debt, recorded.** The pacing model does not price a ride: the step carries its rate and its cells, and no budget reads them.

## 5. The player's acts, and what the engine reads **[cited, authored]**

**[cited]** (the record §4–§6): mounting is a right-click on the body; leaving is the sneak input; the server's `RootVehicle` on the player names the carrier; `execute … on vehicle` resolves to it; a rider in a box is counted by the box selector at the seat. **[authored]** No objective kind is added: a `reach-anchor` whose box a rider enters completes for the rider as for a walker; an `interact` or a `collect` is performed after leaving. A gate may read the ride: `requires_state` over a derived datum (spec-0134 §1's `counts`) gains `of: {"riders": "vehicle/<id>"}` — the count of players inside the datum's `region` whose vehicle is that vehicle's body, written by the census tick in two lines: the datum set to nothing, then one added for every such player through `execute as @a[<box>] on vehicle if entity @s[tag=dw_vehicle_<id>]` — so a beat can hold until the party is aboard, and a `body-enters` trigger over it fires when the first player boards. A vehicle nobody declared is `DW0112`. Nothing is proven statically for it (a rider is placed by boarding, and no seat walks to the box). **A ridden objective may wait for its own rider**: an objective that declares `ride` and whose `requires_state` reads a `riders` census of that same vehicle at one or more completes under the rider and nowhere else, so its ride goes **into** its completion volume — the one exception to §4's keep-out — and the `ride` step carries the census as a row `{state, objective, riders: <the vehicle's tag>, region, count}`. The bot, aboard at the ride's end, is the body it counts: it waits one census period, reads the datum on the server, and fails the ride by name when the game does not count it, or when the gate needs more riders than one bot is. A gate on a `riders` census anywhere else — another vehicle's, or a step that is not a ridden objective's — carries no row and is not driven by the bot: stated, a debt. The stakes of a ride are the kind's: a rider is a body at the seat, and nothing is granted or forgiven for sitting down.

## 6. How the harness drives each kind **[authored, on cited facts]**

**[cited]** (the record §4–§6; mineflayer `entities.js`, `physics.js`): mineflayer's `mount` is the right-click; its `dismount` sends the jump bit on this version's input packet, which does not dismount — the sneak bit does; `moveVehicle` sends the input bits, which move none of the three kinds; the client-reported `vehicle_move` is what moves a boat and a mount; the rails move a cart.

**[authored]** `critical-path.json` and every `world-path-<k>.json` gain the step kind **`ride`** (`STEP_ACTIONS`; the contract's format number moves in both constants, numbered by the planner): `{"action": "ride", "vehicle": "vehicle/<id>", "kind": "boat" | "minecart" | "mount", "board": [x, y, z], "medium": [[x, y, z], …], "rate": blocksPerSecond, "leave": [x, y, z], "body": [[x, y, z], …], "steering_item"?: "minecraft:carrot_on_a_stick"}` — `body` the cells the vehicle stands in at its mark, which the bot's walk to `board` walls as the proof's did. The executor's `ride`:

1. walks to `board`, looks at the vehicle's body (the crosshair rule of `crosshair.ts`, so an occluded vehicle fails by name), and mounts; the step fails when the server's `RootVehicle` — read on the opped channel, never the client's belief, which the record shows stale after a dismount — is not the vehicle within the grace;
2. drives by kind: a **boat** or a **mount** by reporting the vehicle's position along `medium` at `rate` (a `vehicle_move` per tick, yaw along the tangent; for a mount the steering item is equipped first), the way a vanilla client drives — navigation along a proven route at a stated rate, never a physics the harness computes; a **minecart** by nothing: the engine's push at boarding and the rails drive it (§3), and the executor waits, for the run's length at the step's rate, with the same grace a transport's landing has (`TRANSPORT_NEAR`, `TRANSPORT_NEAR_Y`);
3. asserts arrival by the **server's** position of the rider within the landing tolerance of the last medium cell, then leaves by the sneak input, asserts `RootVehicle` is none and the body is at `leave` within the tolerance, and walks on.

The run report carries `rides[]`: per step the vehicle, the kind, the medium cells, the distance the server saw the vehicle move against the distance reported, the final seat, and the leaving cell reached. A reported move the server did not take — a boat the server left behind, a mount at a step it refused — is a red by name, which is what makes the ride a proof and not a replay.

The engine states, on every validate, `vehicle binding (DW1097–DW1099): <n> vehicle(s) — <kind> <id> medium <cells> cell(s) in bounds, board <k> cell(s), leave <m> cell(s), seats <s>; <d> objective(s) declare their leg ridden; <r> ride leg(s) on the critical path`, and for every branch world `vehicle binding, <branch>: <w> ride leg(s) on its path`.

## 7. PackTest and the gallery **[authored, under spec-0039]**

Per vehicle the build emits `vehicle_<id>_seated` (after `setup_finish`, exactly one body with `dw_vehicle_<id>` stands within a block of the mark, in its medium) and `vehicle_<id>_reseats` (the body teleported outside `bounds`, 100 ticks stepped, the body at the mark again). No template is emitted per ride leg: that `ride … mount` seats a body, that `RootVehicle` reads the carrier and that `ride … dismount` stands it beside the vehicle are vanilla behaviour, established once in the record §4–§6 and never a recurring check (CLAUDE.md, *A check guards only what our toolchain owns*); whether the content's ride is boarded, ridden and left is the bot's. The gallery primary binds the three kinds in the yard behind the long hall's end room: a boat moored on a basin, a minecart on a level rail line with a stop at each end, a saddled pig in the paddock with a carrot on a stick in every class's kit, a mare and a donkey; five objectives each declare their leg ridden, one of every objective kind — a `kill`, a `collect`, an `interact`, a `talk-to` and a `reach-anchor` — and a `riders` census of the boat with a `body-enters` trigger over it. Probes: `a-chest-boat-with-a-rider`, `a-llama-under-a-saddle` (`DW1096`), `a-boat-on-the-floor`, `a-cart-facing-across-its-rail` (`DW1097`), `a-boat-moored-outside-its-bounds` (`DW1099`), `a-ride-that-goes-nowhere` (`DW0311`), `a-ride-on-no-vehicle` (`DW0112`). `DW1098` and the seats arm of `DW1099` need a place nobody gets off and a leg no walk crosses, which the gallery's yard does not hold and a probe's one edit cannot build; they are proven by `crates/delvec/tests/it/vehicle.rs`. This is a loosening: the probes `a-rail-into-a-pit`, `a-boat-nobody-can-reach` and `a-boat-for-four` are not written. The gallery bot rides each kind in its run and the run report's `rides[]` is read by the gallery bot job for three kinds; the coverage gate reads no run report.

## 8. The rules **[authored]**

| code | module | refuses |
|---|---|---|
| `DW1096` | `dsl::vehicle` | an entity that carries no rider — not a boat, the rider's minecart or a steerable saddled kind (a chest boat, a hopper cart, a llama); or a mount whose steering item no path puts in a hand before a ride |
| `DW1097` | `compiler::nav` | a vehicle seated outside its medium: a boat on a floor; a cart off a rail, on a launcher or a slope, facing across its rail, or at the open end of its line; a mount in water or in a wall |

## 10. What it leaves out, by name **[authored]**

A ride the proof **infers** (§4: every ride is declared). A ride **priced** by the pacing model (§4's debt). A **chest boat** and a **cargo cart** (no rider). A **llama**, a **dolphin**, anything the game does not steer. A **second rider** on a boat as a separate body in the proof (the leg proves one body; the seat count admits the party). The **pig's boost** and a **horse's jump** (the ride leg never needs either; a jump is a rise the medium refuses). A vehicle for an **NPC** or an **actor**. **Ice**, a boat's land speed, a cart on a **slope** faster than the flat rate (the rate is a floor the harness waits by). A **current**. A vehicle the player **breaks** (it cannot) or **crafts**. The race's **scoring**.

## 11. Acceptance criteria
