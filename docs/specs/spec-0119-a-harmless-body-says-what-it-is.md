- **What it is for**: a player who hits a thing that looks like a monster and learns what it is. In a playtest a hostile-looking body that could not be harmed and did not fight stood beside the route; a player struck it, it did nothing and said nothing, and the player could not tell a statue from a broken fight. The general form: a body of a kind the game itself calls a monster, that cannot be harmed and does not fight, standing where a walking player can reach it, answers the blow — and the right-click — with a line the campaign wrote, to that player alone. The engine today stages such bodies as `NoAI`, `Invulnerable` puppets (every NPC; every actor not `vulnerable`), gives an NPC a `minecraft:interaction` hitbox for its dialogue and an actor none, answers a right-click on an NPC with its dialogue, and answers a blow on either with nothing — and holds, in `DW0427`, that a blow *cannot* be answered to its striker because vanilla names no left-clicker. That claim was measured false for the carrier the compiler uses (§1, `docs/reference/click-attribution.md`). This spec puts the line on the body class and dispatches it by the measured primitive.
- **Research**: `docs/reference/click-attribution.md` is the record of the vanilla finding, with its instrument; `docs/reference/press-answers.md` (spec-0117's record) is the record of how a refused press is answered. Each statement below is marked **cited** (a line of the tree at the ground revision, a reading of pinned data, a measurement on that record, or a constitution rule), **ruled** (the ruling this spec executes, at the scope it was given) or **authored** (this spec chooses).
- **Numbers**: spec `0119`. Two diagnostics, both used: `DW1055` (a harmless monster-kind body in reach with no line, §6) and `DW1056` (a line on a body that cannot show it, §6). `DW0427` shrinks under its own code (§6). The surface change is stages 2 and 5 and takes the unreleased `dsl_version` minor other unreleased surface changes share; this spec states no version literal.
- **Non-goals**: an engine-written wording (ruled: "the engine never writes the words"); a line to the party (a blow is one player's, §3); a line owed on a kind the game does not call a monster (§3 — admitted when declared, never owed); a line on a body that can be harmed or that fights (§3); a flinch, a hurt animation or a knockback on the struck body (vanilla has none for an interaction entity and refuses `damage` on an invulnerable body — the line and the cue are the whole answer); a ranged hit (an arrow passes through an interaction entity and writes nothing — spec-0082 §3.4, cited — so the ruling's *melee* reach is the reach this spec has); whether a player can dodge or should fight anything (a design judgement the playtest makes, never the engine); an `approach` trigger's attribution (no carrier entity exists for it, §1).

## 1. What the tree does today, re-verified

- **Cited** (`emit/npc.rs`, the body summon; `combat.rs`, `non_combatants`): every NPC body is `Invulnerable:1b` — the skinned `minecraft:mannequin` and the plain `base_entity` alike — "so an NPC is never a fight, whatever it is wearing". **Cited** (`actor.rs`, `combat.rs` `hostile_actors`): an actor's puppet is `NoAI`, knockback-immune and `Invulnerable` unless `vulnerable`, "so it never attacks"; only an `unleash-actor` beat "replaces the puppet with a real-AI twin that swings back, and nothing short of it can damage a player". Hostility is "never inferred from the species: the pinned entity registry is a membership set with no mob-category data (`DW0469`'s rule)".
- **Cited** (`registry.rs`, `removed_on_peaceful`; `crates/dsl/data/peaceful-despawn-1.21.11.json`, 38 kinds, regenerated from the pinned jar by `tools/maintenance/extract-peaceful-despawn.py`): the engine does hold one per-kind fact the game states about itself — which kinds it discards on peaceful. `minecraft:zombie`, `minecraft:husk`, `minecraft:spider` and `minecraft:warden` are in it; `minecraft:villager`, `minecraft:mannequin`, `minecraft:bat`, `minecraft:iron_golem`, `minecraft:wolf` and `minecraft:polar_bear` are not.
- **Cited** (`emit/npc.rs`): an NPC's hitbox is a `minecraft:interaction` `{width:1.0f,height:2.0f,response:1b,Invulnerable:1b}` on the body's cell, tagged `dw_npc_<n>`; it is the dialogue's carrier, it is teleported along the walk with the body (`tp @e[tag=dw_npc_<safe>]`), and it is removed with the body by `despawn-npc`. A `strike` at the NPC's stand anchor or a `strike-npc` rides this hitbox as its sole carrier (`npc_hitbox_trigger_tags`, "one cell, one hitbox"); a `use` trigger on an NPC's anchor is `DW0350`. **Cited** (`emit/actor.rs`): an actor's puppet has no hitbox; a `strike` anchored where it stands summons the trigger's own box (`pressable::trigger_body`, `Body::Point`).
- **Cited** (`crosshair.rs`): vanilla picks an entity by ray out to `player.entity_interaction_range` (`INTERACTION_REACH` = 3.0 blocks) and returns the box the ray meets first, with no pick tolerance for any body a delve stages. **Cited** (`eclipse.rs`, `DW0359`): a body larger than the box it stands in is picked instead of the box — the warden-bodied giant whose affordance "is never reached". **Cited** (Minecraft Wiki, *Attribute*, `minecraft:entity_interaction_range`): the same attribute bounds the player's attack reach; a blow and a right-click share one reach.
- **Cited** (`holders.rs`, `check_stance`; `nav/route/leg.rs`, `stance_cells`; `emit/mod.rs`): the engine has one model of *a body can act on this from where it walks*: the standable cells of `world.reachable_walkable_rooted(anchor_starts(plan))` whose eye is within `INTERACTION_REACH` of a box and sees it along a clear segment. `DW1037` refuses a filled holder with no such cell; `DW1018` ranges over the same set.
- **Cited** (`cast.rs`, `station`; `nav::MovePlan::mark_cell`; `crosshair.rs`'s use of both): where a body stands at each beat is declared, not guessed — an NPC's home mark and every `move-npc` destination through the cast ledger, an actor's `anchor`+`offset` and every `move-actor` destination.
- **Cited** (`trigger.rs`, `TriggerAudience`, `attributes_its_actor`, `DW0427`; `compiler.md`, the press-answer section): a press is answered to its presser by an advancement whose reward runs a function as `@s`; `minecraft:player_interacted_with_entity` is that criterion for a right-click, a positional selector for a step; "Vanilla offers no such criterion for a left-click", whose record is "a UUID no command can become", so `audience: presser` on `strike`, `strike-npc`, `strike-assembly` and `approach` is refused.
- **Cited — measured** (`docs/reference/click-attribution.md` §3; `tools/maintenance/probe-click-attribution.py`, two runs on the pinned jar, seven acts, zero disagreements, bot in adventure mode): a left-click on a `minecraft:interaction` entity — `Invulnerable` or not, alone or on one cell with a puppet — fires `minecraft:player_hurt_entity` with the interaction as `this`, granted to the attacking player, and the reward runs as that player; the entity's `attack.player` names the same player. A left-click on an `Invulnerable` living body fires nothing. A right-click on an interaction fires `player_interacted_with_entity` as the clicker; on a living zombie, nothing. The tree's claim is false for the one carrier the compiler dispatches every `strike`-class trigger from (`trigger_carrier_tag`), and true of the body itself.

## 2. The ruling, and what each word binds to

**Ruled.** A body of a hostile-looking kind that cannot be harmed and does not fight, standing within melee reach of a walked route, carries a line the campaign writes; without it the build is refused. The line is shown only when a player tries to interact with the body — attacks it or right-clicks it — to that player. The engine never writes the words.

**Authored — the binding of each term to a fact the tree already holds**, so no term is a new judgement:

| The ruling says | The engine reads |
|---|---|
| a body | a stage-2 `Npc` or a stage-5 `Actor` — the two bodies the engine stages, the class `watch` and `traversal` already belong to |
| of a hostile-looking kind | the kind of the body that ships (`nav::npc_body_entity`: the `base_entity`, or `minecraft:mannequin` when a `skin` is declared; an actor's `entity` likewise) is one the pinned game discards on peaceful (`registry::removed_on_peaceful`) — the game's own statement of which kinds are monsters, the one per-kind category the tree holds from the jar |
| that cannot be harmed and does not fight | the body is **inert**: an NPC is inert for its whole life; an actor not `vulnerable` is inert while its puppet stands — before any `unleash-actor`, and for ever if none names it. A `vulnerable` puppet can be harmed; an unleashed twin fights; neither is inert |
| standing within melee reach of a walked route | at some station the body is declared to stand on (its home mark, every `move-npc` or `move-actor` destination), `nav::stance_cells(world, body_box, INTERACTION_REACH, reachable_walkable_rooted(anchor_starts))` is non-empty, the body box being `entity_dims` of the body that ships over that station's cell, as `eclipse.rs` sizes it |
| a line the campaign writes | one `Refusal` line per press the body has, authored in the campaign's document, inventoried and transcreated as every other line (§4) |
| when a player attacks it or right-clicks it, to that player | the two presses an interaction-carried body attributes to its presser: `player_hurt_entity` for the blow (§1, measured), `player_interacted_with_entity` for the touch (§1, cited) — each dispatched as the player who pressed |

**Authored — what the binding leaves outside the ruling, named.** A kind the game keeps on peaceful but a campaign stages as a menace (an iron golem, a polar bear, a wolf, a skinned mannequin in armour) is not owed a line: the engine has no reading of *looks hostile* beyond the game's own and does not invent one (`DW0469`'s rule). The campaign may still write the line (§4), and it is admitted and counted, never owed. A body no walking player can reach owes nothing and is counted as out of reach.

## 3. The decision: the line belongs to the body, and the body's hitbox carries it

**Authored — the object class.** The capability acts on the body, so it is declared on the body: `answers` on `npcs[]` and on `actors[]`, one type, as `watch` is one type on both (CLAUDE.md: "a capability belongs to the object class it acts on, not to the verb that first needed it"). A body has two presses — a blow and a touch — and `answers` holds one line per press. The line is a `Refusal` (`l10n.rs`: "says what is wrong and what fixes it"), because a blow that moves nothing is an action that failed, and F2's shape — the statement, then at most one line of flavour — is what a struck statue should say.

**Authored — the answer is spec-0117's answer, dispatched by the measured criterion.** Each declared line is lowered into the presser `use` trigger spec-0117 §5.2 synthesizes for every press answer (`trigger/dw-answer-<body>-<press>`, in the reserved `dw-` namespace of `DW0428`), riding the body's hitbox (`Body::Rides` on the hitbox's tag), carrying `narrate { style: actionbar, text }` under `Audience::Solo`, the refusal cue (`CUE_REFUSED`) and the trace write (`#ans_<body> dw.sys`, 1 struck, 2 touched). The one change to the family is the criterion: the `struck` answer's advancement is `minecraft:player_hurt_entity` keyed on the hitbox's tag, in place of `player_interacted_with_entity`. Nothing else is new — not the dispatch function, not the l10n path, not the `env_trigger_<id>` PackTest, not the `DW0811` claim.

**Authored — every inert body that answers wears a hitbox that encloses it.** An NPC has one; an actor gains one, of the same class, when it declares `answers` or is owed them (§6): summoned by the same `spawn-actor` line as the puppet, carrying the puppet's `dw_actor_<id>` tag so the walk's `tp` moves both, removed by `despawn-actor` with the puppet, and removed by `unleash-actor` before the twin stands — the twin must take the blows the box would otherwise take. The box is sized to enclose the body that ships: `entity_dims` plus the click-box margin (`compiler::clickbox`), since the ray picks the first box it meets and a body larger than its box is picked instead (§1, `DW0359`'s giant). Today's NPC constant `1.0 × 2.0` is the instance of that rule for bodies up to that size; the rule replaces the constant. **Cited** (`crosshair.rs`, the pick): a box that encloses the body is met first from every azimuth, so a blow on an inert body lands on its box, which is what makes the blow attributable at all.

**Authored — no baked default, so silence is refused.** `DW0429`'s reasoning is adopted whole, as spec-0117 adopts it: the engine supplies the body, the box, the dispatch and the cue; the words are the campaign's; a body owed a line and given none is `DW1055`. This is a tightening against the engine before this spec, in those words: a monster-kind statue in reach that said nothing, which nothing refused, is refused.

**Rejected, authored.**

- *A `looks_hostile` flag the author sets.* An opt-out secured by a property the defect can supply (CLAUDE.md, vacuity): the body whose author forgot it is exactly the body that stands silent. The kind decides (§2), and the author may add a line, never withhold one.
- *Answering the body's own `HurtTime` or the hitbox's `attack` record by polling and selecting the nearest player.* The "downstream folklore" `DW0427` already names; and now unnecessary, since the criterion names the striker.
- *A hitbox on a `vulnerable` puppet or an unleashed twin.* The box would take every blow meant for the body and the fight would be unhittable (`DW0359`'s geometry, in the other direction). A body that can be harmed has no answer box and no line (`DW1056`).
- *A struck line on a body a `strike`/`strike-npc` trigger rides.* The blow already has an answer there — the trigger's act — and spec-0117's `answers` on that trigger (admissible on a `strike` once §6's correction lands) cover its shut and settled states. Two dispatches on one blow is the collision `npc_hitbox_trigger_tags` exists to end.
- *A touched line on an NPC.* Its right-click is its dialogue (`DW0350`'s rule); the dialogue is the answer.

## 4. The surface

Stage 2 (`npcs[]`) and stage 5 (`actors[]`).

```json
{
  "npcs": [
    { "id": "npc/gate-ghoul", "name": "The Gate Ghoul", "role": "flavor",
      "area": "area/yard", "anchor": "anchor/gate-left", "base_entity": "minecraft:husk",
      "persona": { "...": "..." },
      "answers": { "struck": "Dry as a husk. Your blade finds nothing to cut." } }
  ],
  "actors": [
    { "id": "actor/sentinel", "entity": "minecraft:zombie", "anchor": "anchor/plinth",
      "equipment": { "head": "minecraft:iron_helmet" },
      "answers": { "struck": "Stone under the iron. It does not stir.",
                   "touched": "Cold. Whoever carved this did not mean it to wake." } }
  ]
}
```

**`answers`** is an optional object on every `npcs[]` and `actors[]` entry, holding one `Refusal` line per press the body has: `struck` (a blow) and `touched` (a right-click). Each line is optional in the schema; which are owed is `DW1055`'s rule and which cannot show is `DW1056`'s (§6). Keys in the l10n inventory: `npc.<id>.answers.struck`, `actor.<id>.answers.struck`, `actor.<id>.answers.touched`, kind `Refusal`, each with its `situation` (the body's area and the beats it stands in, as the body's `name` rows already carry). A body of a kind the game keeps on peaceful may declare either line; it is admitted and counted.

**What is not changed.** `vulnerable`, `unleash-actor`, `strike`, `strike-npc`, the NPC dialogue, `watch`, `traversal` and `DW0350` keep their meaning. `audience: presser` on a `strike`-class trigger becomes admissible (§6), which is the one authority (`attributes_its_actor`) saying what §1 measured.

## 5. Emission

### 5.1 The answer body

**Authored.** An NPC's hitbox is its answer body. An actor that declares `answers`, or is owed them, gets one: the `spawn-actor` summon emits, after the puppet, `summon minecraft:interaction <x> <y> <z> {width:<w>f,height:<h>f,response:1b,Invulnerable:1b,Tags:["dw_fixture","dw_actor_<id>","dw_abody_<id>",<answer trigger tags>]}` with `<w>`,`<h>` the enclosing dims (§3); `despawn-actor` and `unleash-actor` kill `@e[tag=dw_abody_<id>]` on the line before they touch the puppet; the walk's `tp @e[tag=dw_actor_<id>]` moves both. An actor with no `answers` and none owed emits what it emits today (byte-identical). An NPC's hitbox dims move from the constant to the enclosing rule for every NPC whose body exceeds `1.0 × 2.0`; the cost is stated: those campaigns' `setup_finish` bytes move; `gallery/baseline/` is regenerated once.

### 5.2 The answers, lowered

**Authored.** For each declared line the compiler synthesizes one presser trigger, `trigger/dw-answer-<body>-struck` or `-touched` (`<body>` the NPC's or actor's safe id), exactly as `collect_press_answers` lowers a `sealed_hint`: `Body::Rides` on the hitbox tag, effects `narrate { style: actionbar, text }` under `Audience::Solo`, `cue_line(CUE_REFUSED)`, and `scoreboard players set #ans_<body> dw.sys <n>`. The `touched` trigger's advancement is the `player_interacted_with_entity` every press answer uses; the `struck` trigger's advancement is `minecraft:player_hurt_entity` with the same entity predicate (`Tags` contains the hitbox tag), through `press_dispatch_fn`, revoking itself in its reward so the body answers every blow. **Ungated**: the box stands exactly while the body is inert (§5.1), so the answer's state is the box's existence and no gate term is derived. **One authority**: the criterion for a press is read off the press kind in one place (`emit/trigger.rs`, beside `trigger_record`), and `attributes_its_actor` is the DSL-side statement of the same table; the agreement test of spec-0117 §5.2 is extended with the `struck` row.

### 5.3 `DW0427`, corrected at the one authority

**Authored.** `attributes_its_actor` returns true for `strike`, `strike-npc` and `strike-assembly` as it does for `use` and `step`; `audience: presser` on them is admitted and dispatched by a `player_hurt_entity` advancement keyed on the trigger's carrier tag (`trigger_carrier_tag`), with the tick poll and the `attack`-record clear retired for a presser trigger as they already are for a presser `use`. `approach` alone stays refused: no carrier entity exists for it. The doc comments of `TriggerAudience` and `DW0427` and the press-answer section of `compiler.md` say what §1 measured and point at `click-attribution.md`; `STRIKER_STORAGE` (the UUID captured for `unleash-actor`'s aggro lock) is unchanged, because a presser dispatch makes `@s` the striker and the storage path remains for the polled, party-addressed form.

### 5.4 Nothing else moves

The dialogue advancement, `strike-npc`'s ride on the NPC hitbox, the cast ledger, `DW0359`, `DW0489`, `DW1025`, the bot's `talk-to` and `strike` steps, the harness: unchanged. A bot never strikes a statue, so it never reads an answer; the PackTests do.

## 6. Diagnostics

**`DW1055` — a harmless monster-kind body in reach with no line** (`delvec`, build tier, exit 1; beside `DW1037` in `holders.rs`'s stance family or in a `compiler::bodies` module the implementation names, because the quantifier needs the assembled world). Quantifier: every `npcs[]` and `actors[]` entry of the primary document. **Owed**: for a body whose shipping kind `removed_on_peaceful` holds, that is inert in some state (every NPC; every actor not `vulnerable`), that no `strike`/`strike-npc` trigger rides, and that has a stance cell at some declared station — `struck`; and, for such an actor, `touched`. **Missing** any owed line is refused. Path: the body's `answers`. Message: the body, its kind and why the game calls it a monster (the peaceful table), the station and the stance cell that reaches it, what a player meets there (a blow that does nothing and says nothing), and the remedy — write the line in the campaign's own words, or stage the body where no walked route reaches it; the l10n key. Binding line: `body answers (DW1055): B bodies — M monster-kind, I inert, R in reach, O lines owed, D declared, V volunteered on kinds not owed, X refused; S out of reach, T carried by a strike trigger`.

**`DW1056` — a line on a body that cannot show it** (`dsl`, validation tier, exit 1, in `npc.rs`/`actor.rs`'s checks, since every arm is read off the declaration alone). `answers` on a `vulnerable` actor (no box can stand on a body that must take blows); `touched` on an NPC (its right-click is its dialogue); `struck` on a body a `strike`/`strike-npc` trigger rides (the blow is the trigger's). An inert declaration is refused, never ignored (spec-0093 §7's rule). Remedy: drop the line, or change what makes it unreachable (`vulnerable`, the trigger, the dialogue).

**`DW0427`, shrunk under its own code.** Refuses `audience: presser` on `approach` only; its message says a presser needs a carrier entity and an approach has none. Spec-0117 §6's widening of `DW0427` to `answers` on `strike`-class triggers is withdrawn by §1's measurement (§10).

## 7. Gallery element and refusal probes

**Gallery.** The primary gains one inert actor of a kind the peaceful table holds — a `minecraft:zombie` statue in iron, not `vulnerable`, never unleashed — on a station within reach of the walked route, with both lines; and one NPC whose shipping kind the table holds (a `minecraft:husk` flavour NPC, or one of the villagers re-bodied) with `struck`. `actor/hall-usher` (a villager, inert, in reach) stays without a line and is counted as not owed; `actor/sergeant` (`vulnerable`) stays without a line and is the `DW1056` probe's base. `check-gallery-coverage.py` enumerates `Npc.answers`, `Actor.answers`, `BodyAnswers.struck`, `BodyAnswers.touched` and reports each bound. **Perturbation**: removing a line moves the emitted bytes of its synthesized trigger and of the spawn summon's tag list; moving the statue's anchor out of every stance cell turns `DW1055` silent and moves the binding line's `S`.

**Probes**, each the primary plus one declared edit: `a-statue-that-takes-blows-in-silence` (`DW1055`: the statue's `answers` removed); `a-line-on-a-body-that-bleeds` (`DW1056`: `answers.struck` added to `actor/sergeant`, which is `vulnerable`); `a-touch-that-already-talks` (`DW1056`: `answers.touched` added to `npc/curator`). Spec-0117's `an-answer-on-a-press-nobody-can-be-named-for` is re-aimed at an `approach` trigger (§6).

## 8. Demo level

The row for `docs/demo-levels.md`: **The Sentinels** — a short gallery hall lined with armoured figures on plinths, each within a sword's reach of the carpet: four zombie and husk statues (`vulnerable: false`, never unleashed) each with a `struck` line and a `touched` line in a different register (dust, cold, a warning, a joke); one figure that is the hall's real elite, inert until a story beat at the hall's end unleashes it, so a blow before the beat is answered and a blow after it bleeds; one iron golem among them with a volunteered line (not owed — the game keeps it on peaceful); and at the far door a figure woken by the blow itself, a `strike` trigger riding its body, which therefore carries no line. A player strikes and presses every figure and reads one action-bar line and hears one cue each time; a second player beside them reads nothing. The look is what no machine decides: whether the line reads at the crosshair before it fades, whether the cue reads as *stone* rather than *click*, whether a player stops hitting after one line or keeps testing, and whether the elite's waking reads as a payoff after a hall of statues.

## 9. Pages

## 10. Interaction with spec-0117

**Reused, by name**: the synthesized press-answer trigger and its id namespace (§5.2 of 0117), `NarrateStyle::Actionbar`, `CUE_REFUSED` and `cue_line`, the `#ans_` trace write, `press_dispatch_fn`, `collect_press_answers`, `Body::Rides`, `TextKind::Refusal`, `DW0428`, `DW0429`'s reasoning, `DW0811`'s claim. Nothing in this spec is a second authority for any of them.

**Amended**: 0117 §6 widens `DW0427` to refuse `answers` on `strike`, `strike-npc`, `strike-assembly`, `approach` and `step`, and 0117's non-goals list "an answer on a left-click — vanilla cannot name the presser". §1 of this spec measured otherwise for every interaction-carried press. When both land: `answers` and `audience: presser` are admitted on `strike`-class triggers and dispatched by `player_hurt_entity`; `DW0427` refuses `approach` alone; 0117's probe `an-answer-on-a-press-nobody-can-be-named-for` is re-aimed at `approach`; 0117 §11's "a `strike` that stands down and says nothing" closes, since a `serves` `strike-assembly` can now answer its settled state. The planner lands the two drafts' implementations in that knowledge; this spec's implementation follows 0117's on `emit/trigger.rs` and `plan/trigger.rs`, and the `struck` row is added to 0117's agreement test rather than a second test being written.

## 11. What stays open

- **A hostile look the game does not name.** A neutral kind staged as a menace, a skinned mannequin in black plate — the engine reads only the peaceful table. A reading of *looks hostile* that is not the game's own would be an authored category; none is proposed.
- **A ranged blow.** An arrow passes through an interaction entity (spec-0082 §3.4); a statue shot from across the hall says nothing. The vanilla primitive that would name an archer for an invulnerable body is `player_hurt_entity` on a living body, which an invulnerable body never fires (§1): an open capability gap, not a hack to be made.
- **The body does not move when struck.** No flinch exists for an interaction entity; the cue and the line are the whole reaction. Whether that reads as *stone* is the demo level's question.
- **`STRIKER_STORAGE`'s polled form.** With a striker nameable at dispatch, the polled capture for `unleash-actor`'s aggro lock is the second way of naming one player; folding it into the presser dispatch is proposed, not done.
