# spec-0131: A line of the plan states its activity — every planned quest declares the acts the player performs on it and the expansion is held to the declaration, the acts are counted and priced on every `validate`, and the pacing figure reads the route and the acts as one number against `target_minutes`

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Ground**: written against engine `70e2e5eaf` (`main`), read whole for: `Objective`, `Act` and `Objective::completion_acts` in `crates/dsl/src/quest/objective.rs`; `Quest::objective_acts` in `crates/dsl/src/quest/mod.rs`; `PlannedQuest` in `crates/dsl/src/quest_plan.rs`; the act lexicon, `Lexicon::head` and `check_acts` (`DW1045`) in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/telling.rs`; the metrics table and `Reads` in `crates/dsl/src/metrics.rs`; both `DW0822` call sites (`crates/dsl/src/layout.rs`, `crates/delvec/src/compiler/blockout.rs`); the gym's `target_minutes` derivation in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/gym.rs`; `docs/reference/compiler.md` stage 4 and its ledger list; `docs/reference/web-narrative.md`; `docs/reference/game-writing.md` §1; spec-0114; the `/new-delve` pages `SKILL.md`, `references/story.md`, `references/content.md`, `references/build.md`, `references/quest-capabilities.md`. Content read: the campaign the finding came from at two revisions of its own branch, the talk-only version and its rewrite, and the eight campaigns on content `main` at `c1e29e0c`.
- **What it is for**: a campaign authored through `/new-delve` passed every step with eighteen objectives, sixteen of them `talk-to` and two `reach-anchor`, and played to its ending in about twenty minutes against a `target_minutes` of 150; every line was *go somewhere and talk*. Nothing on the way had counted what the lines were made of: the plan records structure and never what the player does, the content page has no rule on objective kinds, and the pacing line measures the route alone and accepts any explanation of the gap. This spec makes each line's activity a thing the plan states and the engine counts, so a talk-only campaign is seen as one before the content step closes and the length is estimated from what the player does.
- **Research**: `docs/reference/web-narrative.md` §7 is the record, each rule marked **cited** or **authored**, its sources listed there. This spec states what the engine and the page do with it.
- **Numbers**: spec `0131`; one diagnostic, `DW1079`; `dsl_version` `0.70.0` (the plan gains a required field, §3); the metrics table's own `METRICS_VERSION` moves with its new entries.
- **Non-goals**: reading the goal's words for the activity (§3 says why not); pricing environment triggers, shops, pulses or loops (they are not objectives; recorded in `web-narrative.md` §6); a threshold on the pacing figure (it stays a statement, `DW0822`'s rule); refusing an act the line performs beyond its declaration (§4); any change to what the five objective kinds complete on.

## 1. The defect, measured

**Cited — the talk-only version** (content, the campaign's branch at its first playtested revision; the engine's census of the implementation, §6): 7 planned quests, 18 objectives — `reach 2, talk 16`; about 32 minutes of activity and 35 of route, about 67 in all against `target_minutes` 150; all 7 lines talk-or-reach only. Given the `activity` its design record declares per line, on a scratch copy at 0.70.0, the engine refuses 3 of its 7 lines: the lighthouse spoke (declared `reach, talk`, performs `talk`), the chorus strand (declared `reach, talk`, performs `talk`) and the curtain strand (declared `talk, give` — the by-law carried back — performs `talk`); `activity binding (DW1079): 7 of 7 … 3 refused; 0 line(s) perform an act beyond their declaration`. The plan promised arrivals and a delivery and the expansion delivered conversations; the declaration this spec adds refuses that where the plan is written.

**Cited — the rewrite** (the same branch after the round that rewrote the quests so the town is played): 7 planned quests, 35 objectives — `reach 4, talk 12, take 6, use 19, present 2, give 2` (a `give-item` on completion counts as a taking); about 74 minutes of activity and 54 of route, about 128 in all against 150, beside the design record's own estimate of 130–185; one line talk-or-reach only, the hub. Given its design record's declarations, no line is refused, and two perform an act beyond what the record names (`activity binding (DW1079): 7 of 7 … 0 refused; 2 line(s) perform an act beyond their declaration` — the loaf also gives, where a vessel is laid, and the curtain also takes, where a clue is picked up), counted and not refused. The owner's twenty minutes against the first figure and the design estimate against the second are the first two calibration points for the prices (§5).

**Cited — the goal's words, as a reading of the activity** (the design §3 rejects; the instrument is spec-0114's head rule hand-run over every goal, cross-checked by an engine build of that design): over the eight campaigns of content `main`, 46 planned quests, 38 goals with a lexicon head, 15 of them naming an act no objective of their line performs — chiefly *Climb to the Lord's Hall above the gate and hear the guide there* over a single `talk-to`, a tour whose every line walks the party to a conversation, one campaign with 6 of its 9 lines so — and in the engine's own corpus (47 directories) 9 fixture goals, 3 generic strings standing in 28 files; the rewrite's finale, *Put on the Founders' Play at sunset*, refused for a phrasal verb the lexicon lacks. A reading that refuses wording where the player's activity is right is the measure of why the activity is declared and not read.

## 2. What is counted, and in what vocabulary

**Cited** (spec-0114 §4; `Objective::completion_acts`; `Quest::objective_acts`): every objective's completion performs a known set of acts from a closed vocabulary of seven — `reach`, `talk`, `kill`, `take`, `use`, `present`, `give` — derived from its kind and the fields that shape its completion, with a `give-item` among its completion effects adding `take`. **Cited** (`docs/reference/web-narrative.md` §7.1, Doran & Parberry): a quest is a sequence of atomic actions and lines differ by which actions they are made of; a talk-only line is one strategy repeated.

**Authored.** A *line* is a planned quest (stage 4) with its stage-5 expansion. Its **activity** is the multiset of acts its objectives perform, counted per act (an objective performing several acts counts under each), and its **minutes** are the sum over its objectives of the price of each objective's dearest act (§5). The census over the plan is the lines, the planned quests with no expansion (counted as awaiting stage 5, the state `DW0150` names), the totals, the minutes over all lines and over the mandatory lines alone, and the lines whose every act is `talk` or `reach` — the shape a line has when nothing on it is done but talking and arriving. The census is a **statement with its denominator**: it refuses nothing, because a plan may declare a line of conversations and the constitution forbids a check that infers an undeclared intent in order to refuse.

## 3. The declaration: a field on the planned quest, not a reading of its words

Two designs were put to this spec.

**The goal's head**, read as a title's is (spec-0114 §3), against the acts the line performs. Rejected, **authored**: it is the review shape the constitution names — a parameter inferred from arguments when the caller knows more. The author knows the activity; a reading of the goal's first word infers it, and the inference fails on the ordinary shapes of a goal sentence: a causative (*Get the keeper to open the door*) and a phrasal verb the lexicon lacks (*Put on the play*) are refused for their wording, not for what the player does. Measured over content `main` (§1), fifteen of thirty-eight judged goals would have been refused that way, and the finale of the rewrite that fixed the motivating campaign among them.

**A declared `activity` field on the planned quest.** Chosen, **authored**: the caller states it. `activity` is a required list of acts from the spec-0114 vocabulary (`reach`, `talk`, `kill`, `take`, `use`, `present`, `give`, spelled as `Act::as_str` spells them), declared where the line is planned — before the goal is written, when the body of the line is decided (§7). It is a surface change and moves `dsl_version` to 0.70.0, with every document the repository holds (ADR-0024). It is not the third declaration spec-0114 §3 rejected for a title: a title is one objective's text beside its kind, and the kind already states the act; a planned quest has no kind, and before stage 5 exists nothing states what its line will do. The declaration is confirmed at stage 5 against the union of the acts the line's objectives perform (`Quest::objective_acts`), never inferred from them: the engine reads no English here.

## 4. The rule, and the message

**`DW1079` — a planned quest's declared `activity` is not carried out by its expansion** (`dsl::quest::check`; error; validation tier, exit 1). Quantifier: every planned quest that has a stage-5 quest of the same id. Path: `/content/quests/<i>/activity` of the quest plan. Refused when a declared act is performed by no objective of the line, or when the declaration is empty and the line's objectives perform acts — a line that does something says what. Not refused: an act the line performs beyond its declaration, which is counted in the binding line, because a check confirms what the author declared and never infers an undeclared intent; and a planned quest with no stage-5 quest, which has nothing to read against and is counted as awaiting stage 5 (`DW0150`'s state). Message: the quest, the declared and performed act sets, each objective's own acts, the act(s) not carried out, then both remedies — give the line an objective that performs the act (the kind per act named: a `collect` takes, an `interact` uses and with `requires_item` presents and with `takes_item` gives, a `kill` kills, a `talk-to` talks, a `reach-anchor` reaches), or withdraw the act from `activity`. On a spoke declared as a search, the first is the repair the page prescribes (§7); the order is the message's, the repair is the author's. Judged at `delvec validate`, where the plan is written; nothing reaches the datapack.

**The binding line**: `activity binding (DW1079): <judged> of <lines> planned quest(s) declare an activity their expansion is read against; <awaiting> awaiting stage 5; <refused> refused; <n> line(s) perform an act beyond their declaration (<quest> also <acts>; …)`.

## 5. The price of an act, and where it lives

**Cited** (`crates/dsl/src/metrics.rs`): the metrics table is the one authority for a building number; `pacing.route-blocks-per-minute` is a provisional seed read through `Reads`, so `DW0813` names it on every run that rests a figure on it; `DW0822` carries no threshold until a walked gym and a full playtest calibrate the coefficient.

**Authored — the surface.** Seven building entries, `pacing.minutes-per-<act>` for each act of the vocabulary, `Provenance::Provisional`, in minutes; `ActivityRates::read` is the one reader and reads all seven on every call, so a run that prices a line has read every seed and `DW0813` names each. `reach` is priced at zero: the route coefficient already prices the walk, and the two figures are summed, so a reach priced above zero would price the walk twice. The table's `METRICS_VERSION` moves with the entries and the digest test moves with it. The seeds and their reading are `web-narrative.md` §7.3; the numbers are the table's and are not restated here.

**Authored — the figure.** `DW0822`, at the projection over the graph and at the measurement over the built blockout, states the route minutes, then the activity clause — the activity minutes over so many objectives on so many lines, the acts counted, the price list, the planned quests not yet expanded named as outside the total — then the sum of the two against `target_minutes`. The figure remains a statement with no threshold. The gym's own `target_minutes` adds the price of its two objectives through the same reader, so the gym is built from the activity entries as it is from the route coefficient and `DW0840` names only the entry nothing reads.

## 6. Where the census lives, and why

Three homes were considered: the engine, a script of the `/new-delve` plugin, the author's own count.

**The engine — chosen, authored.** `delvec validate` runs at every step of the page from step 3 on, so a census it prints cannot be skipped by a step that forgets to run a script; it is computed from the parsed documents by the one derivation of an objective's acts (spec-0114), so it cannot hold a private copy of that rule, which a script reading `quests.json` would; and the declaration it confirms (§3) is refused where it is written, which no report field and no hand count can do; and the declaration is a field of the plan, so it cannot be skipped either. The build writes the same census to `validation/activity-census.json` (the top-level `examined` is the lines read), with the activity verdict under `declared` (`dsl::quest::check::ActivityBinding`); neither reads a player-facing string, so a `--lang` bake computes both over its own campaign. A script would be a second implementation of the act derivation and could be skipped; a hand count errs silently.

**What `validate` prints** under `-- what this run examined`: `activity census: <lines> line(s) over <objectives> objective(s) — <act> <n>, …; about <minutes> min of activity (<mandatory> on the mandatory lines) against `target_minutes` <target>; <k> line(s) talk-or-reach only (<ids>); <m> planned quest(s) awaiting stage 5 (<ids>)`, then one line per planned quest: `` `<quest>`[ (optional)]: <objectives> objective(s) — <act> <n>, …; about <minutes> min``, then the binding line of §4.

## 7. The page

The `/new-delve` page changes at the engine pin that carries this spec (the pull request names the engine's next tag, ADR-0029 §3). The rule has one home, `web-narrative.md` §7; the page carries the step and points at it.

- `SKILL.md`: the showcase paragraph says what is never extra under either brief — every line has a body and is budgeted for it; step 3 requires every line to declare its activity in `activity`, decided before the goal is written, and to be budgeted per activity in `DESIGN.md`; step 5 closes on the census.
- `references/story.md`: the quest-plan bullet names the declaration; a section *Every line has an activity* states the three steps — decide the body before the goal; declare it in `activity`, which `DW1079` holds stage 5 to, and open the goal on it; budget the evening per activity against `target_minutes` in a `DESIGN.md` table — and points at `web-narrative.md` §7.
- `references/content.md`: the step does not close until the census is read against the plan's table, with the three readings a census can give and what each prescribes — a talk-or-reach-only spoke gets its body, never a narrowed declaration; a refused declaration takes the message's first repair on a spoke; a sum well under the target is lines missing.
- `references/build.md`: the pacing line is the sum of route and acts, read as the evening; a gap is content, never a note.
- `references/quest-capabilities.md`: the pacing bullet names both halves and points at the story page.

## 8. What the check does not judge, by name

The **goal's words**: nothing reads them for the activity (§3); F1 governs them. The **count** of what a line does: a line of one `interact` declared `use` agrees, however many uses the design meant. An act performed **beyond** the declaration: counted, not refused (§4). **Side content**: an environment trigger, a pulse, a shop and a loop are not objectives and are neither counted nor priced (`web-narrative.md` §6). An item handed over by a **dialogue option** is not a taking the line performs (spec-0114 §6.4). The price of a **fight** is authored, not read from a playthrough. Whether a line's body is **fun**: the playtest's.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

Each is a test or a gate run, named. The field replaced a reading of the goal's words; no criterion asserts less than its predecessor about the same object — the lexicon-head assertions are gone with the surface they judged, and criterion 4's perturbation moves the declaration where it moved the lexicon.

1. **Every act is priced, under one name.** `crates/dsl/tests/it/activity.rs` asserts every `Act` resolves a `pacing.minutes-per-<act>` entry and `ActivityRates::read` reads all seven; the metrics digest test (`crates/dsl/tests/it/metrics.rs`) moves with `METRICS_VERSION` in the same change.
2. **The surface.** `delvec schema --stage quest-plan` exports `activity` as a required member of the planned quest whose items are the seven acts; a plan without it, or with a word outside the vocabulary, does not parse (`crates/delvec/tests/it/line_activity.rs`); `tools/ci/check-gallery-coverage.py` reports the field and every act value bound (the gallery's four lines declare all seven between them). Every document the repository holds declares it, at `dsl_version` 0.70.0.
3. **The census counts and prices.** `line_activity.rs`: one line of one objective per kind counts one of each act and prices the sum of the five at the table's rates; an `interact` with `requires_item` and `takes_item` counts under use, present and give and is priced once, at its dearest act; a line of conversations and walks is named talk-or-reach only in the header; a plan with no stage 5 counts its quests as awaiting stage 5, prices nothing and refuses nothing; the ledger states `examined` as the lines read. The census of one campaign is deterministic.
4. **`DW1079` raised and silent.** The same file: a declaration every act of which some objective performs is green on every kind, on a union of two and on the richer `interact` shapes; a declared act nobody performs is refused at `/content/quests/<i>/activity` on every kind, the message naming the missing act and each objective's acts; an empty declaration over objectives that perform acts is refused and over no objective is not; an act performed beyond the declaration is counted and not refused; a `give-item` on a talk's completion carries out a declared `take`. **Perturbation**: the same expansion under `["talk", "reach"]` is green and under `["talk", "reach", "take"]` is refused — the declaration alone turns the verdict; and the check runs inside `validate_campaign`, not only when called.
5. **The pacing figure reads both halves.** `DW0822`'s projection on the `blockout` fixture states the activity minutes, their denominator and the price list beside the route and the sum; emptying stage 5 turns the clause to `0 minute(s) over 0 objective(s) on 0 line(s)` and names the planned quests not yet expanded (`line_activity.rs`); the second call site's message carries the same clause (`crates/delvec/tests/it/blockout.rs`).
6. **The gym reads the prices.** `crates/delvec/tests/it/gym.rs`: every entry the gym claims was read through the table's accessor, and `DW0840` names exactly the entries unread — with the seven price entries read, the one it names is the walk-only ceiling.
7. **The shipped corpus is green.** `line_activity.rs` reads hello-world, the gallery primary and every overlay and asserts no `DW1079` and at least one line judged in each; `tools/ci/check-gallery-coverage.py` runs the probe `a-line-that-declares-what-nobody-does` (`units: []`, declaring `take` beside the long hall's two arrivals) and it is refused with `DW1079`.
8. **Catalog, tests and messages.** `DW1079` has its row on `docs/reference/dsl/quest/check.md` and tests asserting its code; `tools/ci/check-dw-codes.py` is green with no `PENDING` entry and its registry equal to the built binary's; `tools/ci/check-diagnostic-messages.py` is green over the new messages.
9. **The ledger and its reader.** Every build writes `validation/activity-census.json` with the fields `compiler.md` lists; the gallery coverage gate reads its top-level `examined` and reds on zero.
10. **Pages.** `docs/reference/web-narrative.md` carries §7 with every rule marked cited or authored and its sources listed; `compiler.md` stage 4 (prose and the `quests[].activity` row) and the ledger list, `dsl/layout.md`'s `DW0822` row and `dsl/quest/check.md` name the check and the census; the `/new-delve` pages carry §7's steps and the field, and `tools/ci/check-skill-page.py` is green at the engine's next tag.
11. **The case.** The engine's `validate` over the talk-only version and the rewrite of §1, each given the `activity` its design record declares for every line, prints the census and the binding line stated in §10 of the commit that lands this; the design records are the second method and agree on every refused line.
