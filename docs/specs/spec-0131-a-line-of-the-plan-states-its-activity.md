# spec-0131: A line of the plan states its activity — every planned quest's acts are counted and priced on every `validate`, a quest's `goal` opens on an act its line performs, and the pacing figure reads the route and the acts as one number against `target_minutes`

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Ground**: written against engine `70e2e5eaf` (`main`), read whole for: `Objective`, `Act` and `Objective::completion_acts` in `crates/dsl/src/quest/objective.rs`; `Quest::objective_acts` in `crates/dsl/src/quest/mod.rs`; `PlannedQuest` in `crates/dsl/src/quest_plan.rs`; the act lexicon, `Lexicon::head` and `check_acts` (`DW1045`) in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/telling.rs`; the metrics table and `Reads` in `crates/dsl/src/metrics.rs`; both `DW0822` call sites (`crates/dsl/src/layout.rs`, `crates/delvec/src/compiler/blockout.rs`); the gym's `target_minutes` derivation in `crates/delvec/src/compiler/gym.rs`; `docs/reference/compiler.md` stage 4 and its ledger list; `docs/reference/web-narrative.md`; `docs/reference/game-writing.md` §1; spec-0114; the `/new-delve` pages `SKILL.md`, `references/story.md`, `references/content.md`, `references/build.md`, `references/quest-capabilities.md`. Content read: the campaign the finding came from at two revisions of its own branch, the talk-only version and its rewrite, and the eight campaigns on content `main` at `c1e29e0c`.
- **What it is for**: a campaign authored through `/new-delve` passed every step with eighteen objectives, sixteen of them `talk-to` and two `reach-anchor`, and played to its ending in about twenty minutes against a `target_minutes` of 150; every line was *go somewhere and talk*. Nothing on the way had counted what the lines were made of: the plan records structure and never what the player does, the content page has no rule on objective kinds, and the pacing line measures the route alone and accepts any explanation of the gap. This spec makes each line's activity a thing the plan states and the engine counts, so a talk-only campaign is seen as one before the content step closes and the length is estimated from what the player does.
- **Research**: `docs/reference/web-narrative.md` §7 is the record, each rule marked **cited** or **authored**, its sources listed there. This spec states what the engine and the page do with it.
- **Numbers**: spec `0131`; one diagnostic, `DW1079`. No `dsl_version`: no document gains a field (§3 says why), and the one surface that moves is the metrics table, whose own `METRICS_VERSION` moves with it.
- **Non-goals**: a declared `activity` field on the planned quest (§3); pricing environment triggers, shops, pulses or loops (they are not objectives; recorded in `web-narrative.md` §6); a threshold on the pacing figure (it stays a statement, `DW0822`'s rule); judging a goal's object or place; reading a non-English goal (a sidecar row is bound by provenance, spec-0114 §5); any change to what the five objective kinds complete on.

## 1. The defect, measured

**Cited — the talk-only version** (content, the campaign's branch at its first playtested revision; the instrument is §4's rule hand-run over its documents, cross-checked by the engine's own check in §9.5): 7 planned quests, 18 objectives — `talk-to` 16, `reach-anchor` 2; `target_minutes` 150. Of its 7 goals, 5 open on a lexicon head and 2 do not; 4 of the 5 name an act nobody performs on their line: *Go out to the lighthouse and bring the town its Founder* (`go` → reach) over two conversations; *Gather the tavern's scattered shanty chorus* (`gather` → take) over four conversations; *Get the weaver's new curtain hung on the square* (`get` → take) over three conversations; *Put on the Founders' Play at sunset* (`put` → give) over an arrival and a conversation. Every spoke and strand is talk-or-reach only.

**Cited — the rewrite** (the same branch after the round that rewrote the quests so the town is played): 7 planned quests, 35 objectives — `interact` 19, `talk-to` 12, `reach-anchor` 4; of 7 goals, 6 judged, 1 refused (the finale's *Put on the Founders' Play at sunset*, over an arrival, a use and a conversation — the phrasal verb is not a lexicon head, so `put` is read, and the remedy is a re-heading). No spoke or strand is talk-or-reach only.

**Cited — content `main`** (eight campaigns): 46 planned quests, 38 goals judged, 8 unknown, 15 refused, chiefly of one shape — *Climb to the Lord's Hall above the gate and hear the guide there* over a single `talk-to` — a tour whose every line walks the party to a conversation; one campaign has 6 of its 9 lines so. These campaigns are released and reproduce through their pinned engine; the reading is the measure of how common the shape is, not a debt.

**Cited — the engine's own corpus** (every `quest-plan.json` under `crates/` and `gallery/`, 47 directories): the gallery primary and its four overlays judge 10 goals and refuse none; the fixtures refused 9 goals before this spec, 3 generic strings standing in 28 files (*Get the Keeper to open the door…*, *Take the Keeper's charge…*) and 6 fixture-specific; each is re-headed in the change that lands this spec with a word of its line's acts.

## 2. What is counted, and in what vocabulary

**Cited** (spec-0114 §4; `Objective::completion_acts`; `Quest::objective_acts`): every objective's completion performs a known set of acts from a closed vocabulary of seven — `reach`, `talk`, `kill`, `take`, `use`, `present`, `give` — derived from its kind and the fields that shape its completion, with a `give-item` among its completion effects adding `take`. **Cited** (`docs/reference/web-narrative.md` §7.1, Doran & Parberry): a quest is a sequence of atomic actions and lines differ by which actions they are made of; a talk-only line is one strategy repeated.

**Authored.** A *line* is a planned quest (stage 4) with its stage-5 expansion. Its **activity** is the multiset of acts its objectives perform, counted per act (an objective performing several acts counts under each), and its **minutes** are the sum over its objectives of the price of each objective's dearest act (§5). The census over the plan is the lines, the planned quests with no expansion (counted as awaiting stage 5, the state `DW0150` names), the totals, the minutes over all lines and over the mandatory lines alone, and the lines whose every act is `talk` or `reach` — the shape a line has when nothing on it is done but talking and arriving. The census is a **statement with its denominator**: it refuses nothing, because a plan may declare a line of conversations and the constitution forbids a check that infers an undeclared intent in order to refuse.

## 3. The declaration: the goal's head, not a field

Two designs were put to this spec.

**A declared `activity` field on the planned quest**, named from the act vocabulary and confirmed against stage 5. Rejected, **authored**: it is a surface change, so it moves `dsl_version` and every document the repository holds (ADR-0024), and this spec was handed no such number; and it is the third declaration spec-0114 §3 rejected for a title — a field beside a goal beside a kind, each able to disagree, written by the same author who wrote the goal.

**The goal's head**, read as a title's is. Chosen, **authored**: the planned quest already carries one prose statement of what the player does on the line, `goal`, and F1 (`game-writing.md`) says functional text opens on its verb. The engine reads the goal's **head** by the title's head rule (spec-0114 §3: the longest lexicon phrase, up to four words, the goal begins with, case-folded, non-letters read as spaces) and compares the acts it names with the **union** of the acts the line's objectives perform; disjoint is refused (`DW1079`), intersecting agrees, a head the lexicon does not know states no act the engine knows and is counted and listed. The union, not any one objective, because a goal states the line: *Hear the keeper out and leave* is true of a conversation followed by an arrival. The page's rule (§7) makes the head the declaration: the body of the line is decided first, and the goal opens on its verb.

**What the reading cannot see — authored, recorded.** A goal is freer prose than a title. A causative (*Get the keeper to open the door*) and a phrasal verb the lexicon lacks (*Put on the play*) are read at their first word and refused; the remedy is a re-heading, named in the message, and the corpus reading of §1 is the measure of how often it is asked. The lexicon is the engine's and is extended by an engine change with the corpus re-read (spec-0114 §4); this spec adds no phrase.

## 4. The rule, and the message

**`DW1079` — a planned quest's `goal` names an act none of its objectives performs** (`compiler::telling`; error; validation tier, exit 1). Quantifier: every planned quest that has a stage-5 quest of the same id, whose goal's head is in the lexicon. Path: `/content/quests/<i>/goal` of the quest plan. Message: the quest and its goal; the head and the act(s) the lexicon gives it; the acts the line performs, each objective's own named; then both remedies, in this order: re-head the goal with a word of what the line does (three lexicon words of the line's acts named), or give the line an objective that performs the act the goal names. On a spoke whose goal promises a search, the second is the repair the page prescribes (§7); the order is the message's, the repair is the author's. Judged at `delvec validate`, where the plan is written; nothing reaches the datapack.

**The binding line**: `line act binding (DW1079): <judged> of <expanded> English goal(s) read against their line's acts, over <lines> planned quest(s); <unknown> head(s) the lexicon does not know; <awaiting> awaiting stage 5; <refused> refused`, followed by each unknown goal with its path.

## 5. The price of an act, and where it lives

**Cited** (`crates/dsl/src/metrics.rs`): the metrics table is the one authority for a building number; `pacing.route-blocks-per-minute` is a provisional seed read through `Reads`, so `DW0813` names it on every run that rests a figure on it; `DW0822` carries no threshold until a walked gym and a full playtest calibrate the coefficient.

**Authored — the surface.** Seven building entries, `pacing.minutes-per-<act>` for each act of the vocabulary, `Provenance::Provisional`, in minutes; `ActivityRates::read` is the one reader and reads all seven on every call, so a run that prices a line has read every seed and `DW0813` names each. `reach` is priced at zero: the route coefficient already prices the walk, and the two figures are summed, so a reach priced above zero would price the walk twice. The table's `METRICS_VERSION` moves with the entries and the digest test moves with it. The seeds and their reading are `web-narrative.md` §7.3; the numbers are the table's and are not restated here.

**Authored — the figure.** `DW0822`, at the projection over the graph and at the measurement over the built blockout, states the route minutes, then the activity clause — the activity minutes over so many objectives on so many lines, the acts counted, the price list, the planned quests not yet expanded named as outside the total — then the sum of the two against `target_minutes`. The figure remains a statement with no threshold. The gym's own `target_minutes` adds the price of its two objectives through the same reader, so the gym is built from the activity entries as it is from the route coefficient and `DW0840` names only the entry nothing reads.

## 6. Where the census lives, and why

Three homes were considered: the engine, a script of the `/new-delve` plugin, the author's own count.

**The engine — chosen, authored.** `delvec validate` runs at every step of the page from step 3 on, so a census it prints cannot be skipped by a step that forgets to run a script; it is computed from the parsed documents by the one derivation of an objective's acts (spec-0114), so it cannot hold a private copy of that rule, which a script reading `quests.json` would; and the declaration it confirms (§3) is refused where it is written, which no report field and no hand count can do. The build writes the same census to `validation/activity-census.json` (the top-level `examined` is the lines read), with the goal verdict under `goals` carried over the English as `Plan::line_binding` for the reason `Plan::act_binding` is. A script would be a second implementation of the act derivation and could be skipped; a hand count errs silently.

**What `validate` prints** under `-- what this run examined`: `activity census: <lines> line(s) over <objectives> objective(s) — <act> <n>, …; about <minutes> min of activity (<mandatory> on the mandatory lines) against `target_minutes` <target>; <k> line(s) talk-or-reach only (<ids>); <m> planned quest(s) awaiting stage 5 (<ids>)`, then one line per planned quest: `` `<quest>`[ (optional)]: <objectives> objective(s) — <act> <n>, …; about <minutes> min``.

## 7. The page

The `/new-delve` page changes at the engine pin that carries this spec (the pull request names the engine's next tag, ADR-0029 §3). The rule has one home, `web-narrative.md` §7; the page carries the step and points at it.

- `SKILL.md`: the showcase paragraph says what is never extra under either brief — every line has a body and is budgeted for it; step 3 requires every line to declare its activity in its goal and to be budgeted per activity in `DESIGN.md`; step 5 closes on the census.
- `references/story.md`: the quest-plan bullet names the declaration; a section *Every line has an activity* states the three steps — decide the body before the goal; the goal opens on the body's verb, which `DW1079` holds stage 5 to; budget the evening per activity against `target_minutes` in a `DESIGN.md` table.
- `references/content.md`: the step does not close until the census is read against the plan's table, with the three readings a census can give and what each prescribes — a talk-or-reach-only spoke gets its body, never a re-headed goal; a refused goal takes the message's repair; a sum well under the target is lines missing.
- `references/build.md`: the pacing line is the sum of route and acts, read as the evening; a gap is content, never a note.
- `references/quest-capabilities.md`: the pacing bullet names both halves and points at the story page.

## 8. What the check does not judge, by name

The **object** of the goal's verb and the **place** it names (spec-0114 §8). The **count** of what a line does: a line of one `interact` and a goal of *Search the castle* agrees. **Side content**: an environment trigger, a pulse, a shop and a loop are not objectives and are neither counted nor priced (`web-narrative.md` §6). An item handed over by a **dialogue option** is not a taking the line performs (spec-0114 §6.4). The price of a **fight** is authored, not read from a playthrough. Whether a line's body is **fun**: the playtest's.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

Each is a test or a gate run, named.

1. **Every act is priced, under one name.** `crates/dsl/tests/it/activity.rs` asserts every `Act` resolves a `pacing.minutes-per-<act>` entry and `ActivityRates::read` reads all seven; the metrics digest test (`crates/dsl/tests/it/metrics.rs`) moves with `METRICS_VERSION` in the same change.
2. **The census counts and prices.** `crates/delvec/tests/it/line_activity.rs`: one line of one objective per kind counts one of each act and prices the sum of the five at the table's rates; an `interact` with `requires_item` and `takes_item` counts under use, present and give and is priced once, at its dearest act; a line of conversations and walks is named talk-or-reach only in the header; a plan with no stage 5 counts its quests as awaiting stage 5, prices nothing and refuses nothing; the ledger states `examined` as the lines read. The census of one campaign is deterministic.
3. **`DW1079` raised and silent.** The same file: a goal whose head names an act some objective of the line performs is green on every kind and on a union of two; a head naming an act nobody performs is refused at `/content/quests/<i>/goal` on every kind, the message naming the head's act, the line's objectives with their acts and the re-heading; a head the lexicon does not know is counted and listed, never refused; a `give-item` on a talk's completion makes a `take` head true; `find` passes on a walk, a conversation and a taking and is refused on a fight.
4. **Perturbation toward the vacuous shape.** A lexicon of no phrases judges nothing, refuses nothing and states `0 judged` beside `1 unknown` on a goal the engine's lexicon refuses; on a fixture with a layout graph, emptying stage 5 turns `DW0822`'s activity clause to `0 minute(s) over 0 objective(s) on 0 line(s)` and names the planned quests not yet expanded, rather than summing.
5. **The pacing figure reads both halves.** `DW0822`'s projection on the `blockout` fixture states the activity minutes, their denominator and the price list beside the route and the sum (`line_activity.rs`); the second call site's message carries the same clause (`crates/delvec/tests/it/blockout.rs`, `dw0822_measures_the_built_route_at_the_second_call_site`, extended).
6. **The gym reads the prices.** `crates/delvec/tests/it/gym.rs`: every entry the gym claims was read through the table's accessor, and `DW0840` names exactly the entries unread — with the seven price entries read, the one it names is the walk-only ceiling (`docs/reference/delvec/compiler/gym.md`).
7. **The shipped corpus is green.** `line_activity.rs` reads hello-world, the gallery primary and every overlay and asserts no `DW1079` and at least one goal judged in each; `tools/ci/check-gallery-coverage.py` runs the probe `a-line-whose-goal-promises-what-nobody-does` (`units: []`, patching the long hall's goal to a `take` head over two arrivals) and it is refused with `DW1079`.
8. **Catalog, tests and messages.** `DW1079` has its row on `docs/reference/delvec/compiler/telling.md` and tests asserting its code; `tools/ci/check-dw-codes.py` is green with no `PENDING` entry; `tools/ci/check-diagnostic-messages.py` is green over the new message.
9. **The ledger and its reader.** Every build writes `validation/activity-census.json` with the fields `compiler.md` lists; the gallery coverage gate reads its top-level `examined` and reds on zero.
10. **Pages.** `docs/reference/web-narrative.md` carries §7 with every rule marked cited or authored and its sources listed; `compiler.md` stage 4 and the ledger list, `dsl/layout.md`'s `DW0822` row and `game-writing.md` F1's machine half name the check and the census; the `/new-delve` pages carry §7's steps and `tools/ci/check-skill-page.py` is green at the engine's next tag.
11. **The case.** The engine's `validate` over the talk-only version and the rewrite of §1 prints the census and the binding line stated in §10 of the commit that lands this; the hand reading of §1 is the second method and agrees on every refused goal.

## 10. Measurements this spec states
